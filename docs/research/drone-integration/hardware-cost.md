## Summary

- DJI announced Dock 4 on 14 October 2026. It adds a 25-second battery swap and the Matrice 5TD thermal camera has 1280×1024 sensor resolution. DJI's published rain limit is 2 mm/h. The searches checked for this report did not find a Malaysian Dock 4 price; that is not proof that no private dealer quote exists. [DJI Dock 4 launch][s9] [Dock 4 specs][s10] [Dock 4 FAQ][s11]
- Separate Malaysian listings display RM55,140 for a Dock 3 overseas edition marked pre-order and RM25,740 for a Matrice 4TD marked sold out. Their arithmetic sum is RM80,880, before installation or a confirmed stock quote. [Dock 3 listing][s3] [Matrice 4TD listing][s4]
- DJI Dock 2, Dock 3 and Dock 4 FAQs state a maximum rainfall intensity of 2 mm/h. **Analyst's read:** Dispatch after local weather and site checks return within limits. Do not treat any of these systems as a heavy-rain patrol solution. [Dock 2 FAQ][s6] [Dock 3 FAQ][s2] [Dock 4 FAQ][s11]
- **Analyst's read:** The opened Aerodyne pages describe drone-as-a-service, inspection and analytics but show no service tariff. The Sanyeong and KTV pages cited in the research pack could not be opened. Three follow-up searches did not find a comparable published Malaysian water-edge inspection rate or award. This does not establish that vendors have no rates. [Aerodyne services][s24] [Aerodyne infrastructure][s25] [Sanyeong inspection][s26] [KTV inspection][s27]
- **Analyst's read:** Priced-input models give RM64,642 for a hired manual Matrice 4E pilot, RM23,017 for the same route using allocated operator time, RM88,548 for one Dock 3/4TD park, and RM442,740 for a modelled five-park township. The dock totals use a 2024 FlightHub 2 price as a placeholder. They exclude installation, current dock licensing, suitable mobile data, approvals for any SUP operation, insurance, repairs and other items listed below. [Matrice 4E listing][s54] [Dock 3 listing][s3] [Matrice 4TD listing][s4] [2024 Malaysia price report][s7] [Unifi broadband][s39] [Indeed gardener salary][s42]
- **Analyst's read:** Subang averaged 211.8 recorded rain days in the cited 2020–2024 series. Treating every rain day as unavailable gives a crude 42% rain-free-day share in a 365-day model year. It does not measure flyable hours or days. [DOSM Environment Statistics 2025][s51]
- CAAM lists BVLOS among Special UAS Project (SUP) operations. A 2025 CAAM notice documents one site-specific Dock 2/M3TD nested operation under a SUP; that notice expired on 4 September 2025 and is not current operating permission. [CAAM UAS rules][s33] [CAAM TNB Dock 2 AIP Supplement][s52]

### 7. Tropical weather limits and likely flyable windows

| Evidence               | What it says                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                               |
| ---------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| Rain and thunderstorms | MetMalaysia says thunderstorms can occur throughout the year, are more frequent in April–May and October–November, and Subang records the highest number of thunderstorms. [MetMalaysia weather phenomena][s50]                                                                                                                                                                            |
| Subang rain days       | DOSM's 2025 environment statistics, using MetMalaysia data, reports 206 rain days in 2020, 188 in 2021, 223 in 2022, 227 in 2023 and 215 in 2024. The five-year mean is **211.8 days/year**. For 2024, the report records 3,321.9 mm rainfall and 289 days with lightning at Subang. Lightning days are not the same measure as thunderstorm days. [DOSM Environment Statistics 2025][s51] |
| Drone threshold        | DJI Dock 2, Dock 3 and Dock 4 FAQs state 2 mm/h maximum rainfall intensity. Dock wind limits differ; Dock 2's landing limit is lower than the aircraft's in-flight wind resistance. Skydio lists up to 0.25 in/h for X10 operation and 4 in/h for dock standby. [Dock 2 FAQ][s6] [Dock 3 FAQ][s2] [Dock 4 FAQ][s11] [Skydio Dock specs][s12]                                               |

**Analyst's read:** If every day with any recorded rain is conservatively treated as unavailable, 365 − 211.8 = 153.2 rain-free days, or about **42%** of a 365-day model year. This is only a clear-day proxy. It is not a measured share of days or hours a dock can fly: some rainy days have dry windows, while wind, lightning, wet surfaces, local restrictions or technical faults can make nominally dry periods unsuitable. Hourly rain-intensity, lightning and wind data for an Elmina site were not found in the MetMalaysia/DOSM climate material reviewed, so a more defensible flyability percentage cannot be calculated from these sources.

**Analyst's read:** Set the dispatch trigger to “inspect after site and aircraft weather checks return within limits,” not “launch during or immediately after heavy rain.” Dock 4's battery swap improves turnaround after a sortie; its 2 mm/h rainfall limit remains.

## Recommendations for Safe Edge

1. **Analyst's read — Finding 1.** Quote Dock 3/4TD and Dock 2/3TD with Malaysian dealers. Require each quote to state local warranty, aircraft and battery count, installation, power/network, software term, spare parts and confirmed stock.
2. **Analyst's read — Findings 5 and 6.** Start with a manually flown VLOS route and existing staff walks while SDP confirms CAAM's classification for the proposed autonomous operation. Treat BVLOS as SUP pending CAAM review.
3. **Analyst's read — Findings 3 and 6.** Ask Aerodyne and at least two other service providers for the same fixed scope: one named park, weekly route, one post-rain callout, imagery retention, review time, travel and permit handling.
4. **Analyst's read — Finding 7.** Use site weather checks and a delayed-after-rain dispatch rule. Record each missed or delayed patrol and its weather reason during the pilot.
5. **Analyst's read — Findings 1, 5 and 6.** Do not approve a township-wide fleet budget until SDP names the asset owner, identifies retained water edges, secures a repair budget and obtains insurance and CAAM costs for the actual operation.

## What we can and cannot claim

**Safe claims**

- DJI's published Dock 3/4 systems specify a 2 mm/h rainfall limit, and Matrice 4TD/5TD include thermal imaging. [Dock 3 FAQ][s2] [Dock 4 FAQ][s11] [Dock 3 specs][s1] [Dock 4 specs][s10]
- Separate public Malaysian listings display Dock 3 at RM55,140 and Matrice 4TD at RM25,740, for an observed sum of RM80,880 before installation. The dock is marked pre-order and the aircraft sold out. [Dock 3 listing][s3] [Matrice 4TD listing][s4]
- CAAM publishes a RM250 Standard ATF fee for the described small surveillance aircraft/location scope, with a maximum 90-day application period. CAAM lists BVLOS as SUP; confirm the category for the actual operation. [CAAM UAS rules][s33] [CAAM Malay UAS rules][s34]
- Subang recorded an average of 211.8 rain days per year in the cited 2020–2024 series. That measure does not equal no-fly days. [DOSM Environment Statistics 2025][s51]

**Claims to avoid**

- **Analyst's read:** Avoid “Safe Edge prevents drownings” or “the AI certifies an edge safe.” The reviewed evidence supports inspection workflows, not a measured reduction in drowning or a safety certification.
- **Analyst's read:** Avoid “the dock patrols through tropical rain” or “launches immediately after a storm.” DJI publishes a 2 mm/h rainfall limit, and the reviewed sources do not provide site-level hourly flyability data. [Dock 2 FAQ][s6] [Dock 3 FAQ][s2] [Dock 4 FAQ][s11]
- **Analyst's read:** Avoid “a Malaysian township dock costs RM88,548 all-in.” That is a one-park model using a historical software price and excluding approvals, installation, suitable mobile data, insurance and repairs.
- **Analyst's read:** Avoid “every drone operation in Malaysia must have liability insurance.” The cited CAAM rule concerns SUP; confirm the exact operating category and policy requirement. [CAAM SUP rules][s36]
- **Analyst's read:** Avoid “one dock covers an entire township.” Manufacturer radius figures are test-condition maxima and do not establish safe route coverage, radio link, approvals or asset coverage at a real site. [Dock 3 specs][s1] [Dock 4 specs][s10]
