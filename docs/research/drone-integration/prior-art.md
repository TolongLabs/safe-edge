## Summary

This verification checked the cited pages as available on 17 October 2026. Source claims are attributed to their publishers. Any comparison or conclusion is marked **Analyst's read:**.

- **Analyst's read:** Drone patrol, AI-based inspection, and water-activity monitoring are not defensible as new categories. PUB's closed Smart Drain Inspector challenge is the closest cited comparator: it seeks automated defect detection, mapping, and inspection reports for open and closed drains. The challenge accepts non-drone approaches as well as drone approaches. ([PUB challenge](https://www.pub.gov.sg/Industry/Events-and-Programmes/Global-Innovation-Challenge/Challenges/2023/Smart-Drain-Inspector))
- **Analyst's read:** The cited municipal sources show separate precedents in reservoir patrol, river alerts, flood operations, dam and dike inspection, and park maintenance. This scan did not find an opened source that documents the complete Safe Edge defect-review-repair-retest workflow for a Malaysian township water edge. That is a limit of this scan, not evidence of global novelty.
- Rescue drones, lifeguard cameras, and monitored lifebuoys cover distinct parts of water safety. Camera systems can raise alerts before a swimmer is submerged; a lifebuoy monitor can flag equipment removal; rescue drones can deliver flotation. Safe Edge's proposed role is recurring inspection of visible edge conditions and follow-up of human-approved repairs. ([Little Ripper](https://rippercorp.com/pages/little-ripper-lifesaver), [GFE Smart Lifebuoy](https://www.gfe-international.eu/smartlifebuoy), [MOBOTIX SwimEye](https://www.mobotix.com/en/swimeye), [Ellis Aquatic Vigilance](https://eaisafety.com/services/ellis-aquatic-vigilance-system-eavs/))
- Commercial property and asset tools already combine aerial capture, condition records, AI analysis, issue tracking, and work management. **Analyst's read:** Safe Edge should differentiate through the water-edge asset scope, accountable repair ownership, and documented re-test, rather than claim a new drone or AI stack. ([DroneDeploy](https://www.dronedeploy.com/solutions/property-management), [Sitemark](https://www.sitemark.com/), [SLA SUAVE](https://www.sla.gov.sg/properties/management-of-state-land-and-properties/smart-solutions/))
- Malaysian competition records include HydroRise Sentinel, Smart Manhole, Flood-Assist Drone, RescueAI, and MySelamat. The 2026 AquaVengers and Water Drone for Flood Rescue pages were only available as search snippets in this check. The entries describe water, flood-response, or rescue work; the opened sources do not show the same routine township-edge repair loop. ([Air Selangor](https://hydrohub.airselangor.com/visual/data-digital-hackathon-2024/?lang=ms), [USM VHack](https://cs.usm.my/index.php/news/news-cs/848-varsity-hackathon-2025-a-global-stage-for-innovation-at-usm), [APU 2022 report](https://publicwebsite-media.apu.edu.my/assets/2024-03/2022-APU-Sustainable-Development-Report.pdf), [APU MySelamat](https://apu.edu.my/news/apus-ctrl-chill-team-crowned-champion-great-malaysia-ai-hackathon-2025))
- The Star reported 371 drowning deaths among children and teenagers nationwide from 2023 through August 2026. In a separate all-age location breakdown, it reported 82 drowning incidents at lakes and mining ponds; these are incident counts, not the child-and-teen death total. ([The Star, drowning figures](https://www.thestar.com.my/news/nation/2026/10/16/371-youngsters-lost-to-drowning))
- The Star's Tasik Bukit Raja story says a fisherman was reported missing on 15 October 2026 and found on 16 October. It reports no fence, bank, or other edge defect. ([The Star, Tasik Bukit Raja](https://www.thestar.com.my/news/nation/2026/10/16/missing-fisherman-found-dead-at-tasik-bukit-raja))

## Findings

### 1. Commercial drone and AI inspection platforms

**Analyst's read:** These products establish commercial availability of several building blocks. The checked pages do not establish that these vendors offer Safe Edge's proposed township water-edge repair workflow.

- **Percepto AIM. SNIPPET-ONLY.** The cited page did not open in this check. Its feature claims are omitted here pending a readable source. ([Percepto AIM](https://percepto.co/aim/))
- **Skydio** markets drone solutions for utility inspection and emergency response. Its power-generation page lists dam inspection and refuse-pond monitoring. The checked pages do not support the prior claim about post-event inspection. ([Skydio utilities](https://www.skydio.com/solutions/utilities), [Skydio power generation](https://www.skydio.com/solutions/utilities/power-generation))
- **DroneDeploy** describes aerial property capture of roofs, facades, parking lots, grounds, and campuses; its property page also describes dated condition records, before-and-after views, vendor-work verification, and ground robots or 360-degree cameras. ([DroneDeploy property management](https://www.dronedeploy.com/solutions/property-management))
- **FlytBase and Axpo.** The case study describes a drone-in-a-box system at a hydropower site beside a reservoir. It says missions inspect water catchments and snow levels and provide emergency situational awareness; captured findings enter Axpo's inspection management system. ([FlytBase Axpo case study](https://flytbase.com/case-studies/axpo-hydropower-plant-monitoring-autonomous-drones))
- **Aerodyne** describes Drone-as-a-Service and its AI-powered, cloud-based Vertikaliti asset-management product, which analyzes drone data. ([Aerodyne Group](https://aerodyne.group/))
- **Hammer Missions** describes drone or 360-camera analysis, AI defect detection, 3D models, and inspection reporting for buildings, parking structures, and bridges. ([Hammer Missions](https://www.hammermissions.com/))
- **Scopito** describes machine-learning analysis, AI fault detection, historical inspection data, and predictive maintenance. Its page lists power lines, wind turbines, buildings, and solar PV; the checked page does not establish the stronger phrase “critical-infrastructure focus.” ([Scopito](https://scopito.com/))
- **Sitemark** describes renewable-energy asset workflows from construction through operations, with drone and robot capture, AI analytics, forms, ticketing, issue tracking, and work orders. ([Sitemark](https://www.sitemark.com/))
- **Nearthlab** describes autonomous wind-turbine inspection and its Zoomable inspection-data platform. ([Nearthlab Enterprise](https://www.nearthlab.com/en/enterprise))

Skydio's utility page also links to Drone as First Responder and public-safety products. ([Skydio utilities](https://www.skydio.com/solutions/utilities))

**Analyst's read:** The vendor pages reviewed do not present a complete public-safety offer for open township water edges. That is a statement about these pages, not proof that no such product exists. Their published descriptions overlap with autonomous inspection, visual records, condition analysis, issue tracking, and work documentation.

### 2. Government and municipal drone programmes

**Singapore.** On 27 May 2021, PUB announced plans to deploy beyond-visual-line-of-sight drones at six reservoirs: MacRitchie, Marina, Serangoon, Kranji, Lower Seletar, and Lower Peirce. The release says the drones would monitor water quality, aquatic plants, and water activities along reservoir edges, and could alert on activities such as illegal fishing. ([PUB reservoir monitoring](https://www.pub.gov.sg/Resources/News-Room/PressReleases/2021/05/PUB-to-deploy-autonomous-drones-to-enhance-reservoir-monitoring))

PUB's Smart Drain Inspector challenge is marked **Closed**. PUB says its existing autonomous drones inspect GPS-denied, unlit closed drains wider than 2 metres; public drains are typically inspected once every five years. The challenge seeks AI-based defect detection and mapping for open and closed drains, with horizontal and vertical mapping accuracy of 0.1 metres and automatic reports containing location data, images, and video timings. The challenge is open to drone and non-drone solutions. The official challenge index lists it on 24 November 2023. ([PUB challenge](https://www.pub.gov.sg/Industry/Events-and-Programmes/Global-Innovation-Challenge/Challenges/2023/Smart-Drain-Inspector), [PUB challenge index](https://www.pub.gov.sg/Industry/Events-and-Programmes/Global-Innovation-Challenge/Challenges))

**Analyst's read:** PUB is the closest cited technical comparator. Its scope is drainage inspection, while Safe Edge proposes open township water-edge assets and repair ownership. A judge can still reasonably challenge the drone-plus-AI-inspection overlap.

**Not found in this scan.** The searches described by the analyst did not surface a public NEA or Singapore town-council programme specifically for post-construction water-edge defect inspection. This is a bounded search result, not proof that no such programme exists.

**Hong Kong.** The Drainage Services Department's 30 March 2023 briefing says a drone fleet assists river management and maintenance. The same release separately describes water-level sensors and remotely operated robots for desilting rivers and box culverts; it does not say that the drones detect Safe Edge's target edge defects. ([DSD Annual Media Briefing 2023](https://www.dsd.gov.hk/EN/What_s_New/What_s_New/news30879.html))

**Japan.** The Ministry of Land, Infrastructure, Transport and Tourism's working-group page records meetings from 14 November 2024 through 5 February 2026 and includes trial reports, AI anomaly-detection material, and a river-patrol inspection guide. Its separate DX example describes a March 2023 autonomous Yamaguni River trial: a preset 25-kilometre route took 25 minutes without battery replacement, gathered point-cloud and orthophoto data, and produced a 3D model two hours after landing. ([MLIT river-drone working group](https://www.mlit.go.jp/mizukokudo/river/mizukokudo04_mn_000007.html), [MLIT drone examples](https://www.mlit.go.jp/river/gijutsu/dx/example/drone.html))

**South Korea.** On 8 April 2026, the Ministry of Climate, Energy and Environment said it had completed and started operating a Smart Dam Safety Management System for 37 national-management dams. It combines real-time monitoring, aerial and underwater unmanned inspections, digital twins, and AI analysis. The ministry separately reports that a 2025 Seongdeok Dam demonstration cut inspection time from 100 days to 10 and cost from KRW 140 million to KRW 7 million; those are the ministry's reported trial figures. ([Korean environment ministry](https://www.mcee.go.kr/home/web/board/read.do%3Bjsessionid%3DE-OuCHsBlppceh2n5GRXxgoMGjQm5F2-z2JvtQt3.mehome2?boardCategoryId=&boardId=1855940&boardMasterId=939&decorator=&maxIndexPages=10&maxPageItems=10&menuId=10598&orgCd=&pagerOffset=150&searchKey=titleOrContent&searchValue=))

**Anyang. SNIPPET-ONLY.** The cited city page could not be opened. Its indexed result is the only basis for the reported summer AI-drone river-warning details. ([Anyang City](https://anyang.go.kr/main/selectPressRelease.do?bbsNo=1687&key=4107&nttNo=443239))

**Seoul.** Seoul's 5 March 2023 announcement described plans to test autonomous drone routes and AI alerts around riversides, ecological parks, and wetlands. It described a planned alert system, not a completed service. ([Seoul Metropolitan Government](https://english.seoul.go.kr/safety-surveillance-drones-to-protect-citizens-from-accidents/))

**Dubai.** Dubai Municipality's 10 November 2025 Smart Municipality Eye announcement describes AI-equipped drones mapping green spaces across 52 million square metres, assessing plant health, and flagging irrigation or pest issues for predictive maintenance. A separate 15 March 2026 beach-rescue announcement could not be opened; its claim remains snippet-only. ([Dubai Municipality](https://www.dm.gov.ae/ai-driven-urban-management-system-launched/), [Dubai beach rescue system](https://www.mediaoffice.ae/en/news/2026/march/15-03/dubai-municipality-redefines-beach-safety-with-deployment-of-an-integrated-aipowered-rescue-system))

**United Kingdom.** The Environment Agency's TEAM2100 page, updated 24 November 2025, says Estuary Eye combines traditional asset surveys with drone videos and satellite data for flood-defence asset decisions. The page also describes condition assessments informing repair and maintenance planning. ([Environment Agency, TEAM2100](https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/thames-estuary-asset-management-team-2100-programme/thames-estuary-asset-management-team-2100-programme))

**The Netherlands.** Waterschap Hunze en Aa's procedure, published 7 February 2024, lists drone uses that include enforcement, water-plant recording, pump and dike inspections, incident recording, and surveying. It planned a six-month pilot with one drone for enforcement. SkyDike, an active Waterschap Brabantse Delta pilot updated 9 June 2026, combines robotics, drones, sensors, data models, and AI to assess dike grass-cover and detect deviations. The page says the work is still in a test phase and identifies data management and AI-model training and validation as outstanding attention points. ([Hunze en Aa's drone procedure](https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/wsb-2024-1977.html), [SkyDike](https://wbd-rd.nl/innovation/projects/inv_skydike))

**United States.** Dearborn's 2 February 2026 Drone as First Responder announcement includes aerial inspections of rivers, drains, culverts, and flood-prone areas during rain, plus storm-damage assessment. A Lafayette 2024 annual-report claim could not be checked against the PDF and is omitted as snippet-only. ([Dearborn](https://www.dearborn.gov/index.php/dearborn-first-state-deploy-drone-first-responder-program-boost-response), [Lafayette 2024 annual report](https://www.lafayettela.gov/docs/default-source/annual-report-documents/2024-annual-report.pdf?sfvrsn=88b013d2_1))

**Malaysia.** Bernama reported on 26 November 2024 that JBPM planned to deploy drones to help monitor Terengganu floods during the Northeast Monsoon. The cited JPS 2024 Annual Report and JPS WPLB FAQ did not open in this check, so their training and pond-ownership claims remain snippet-only and are not treated as confirmed facts. ([Bernama, JBPM flood monitoring](https://www.bernama.com/en/news.php?id=2367784), [JPS Annual Report 2024](https://www.water.gov.my/jps/resources/LAPORAN_TAHUNAN_2024.pdf), [JPS WPLB FAQ](https://wplb.water.gov.my/faq))

**Analyst's read:** A named asset owner and repair budget are prerequisites for a credible SDP pilot. The cited material does not establish who owns a particular SDP water edge after handover.

### 3. Drowning-prevention technology: rescue, detection, and prevention

- **Rescue drones.** Surf Life Saving NSW's 31 January 2018 story describes the rescue of two swimmers with a Little Ripper rescue pod. The incident date is **18 January 2018**, corroborated by the organisation's March 2018 operations memo, its April 2018 magazine, and Ripper Corporation's product page. The January web story itself says the lifeguards were preparing on 17 January, which conflicts with those records. ([Surf Life Saving NSW story](https://www.surflifesaving.com.au/news/slsnsw-uav-world-first-rescue/), [SLSNSW operations memo](https://www.surflifesaving.com.au/wp-content/uploads/sites/2/2018/03/Memo-2018-NSW-UAV-Operations.pdf), [SurfLIFE April 2018](https://www.surflifesaving.com.au/wp-content/uploads/sites/2/2021/07/SurfLIFE-Issue-36.pdf), [Little Ripper Lifesaver](https://rippercorp.com/pages/little-ripper-lifesaver))
- **Little Ripper Lifesaver.** The product page describes a custom payload-delivery system and rescue pods. It does not support the report's earlier “camera-equipped” wording. ([Little Ripper Lifesaver](https://rippercorp.com/pages/little-ripper-lifesaver))
- **AUXdron.** General Drones describes Auxdron as a drone customers can rent or buy and lists rescue-drone beach services and deployments. ([General Drones services](https://www.generaldrones.es/servicios/))
- **Smart ring buoys.** GFE Smart Lifebuoy describes real-time alerts when equipment is removed or used, remote central monitoring, location views, event history, and audit-ready records. ([GFE Smart Lifebuoy](https://www.gfe-international.eu/smartlifebuoy))
- **Camera-based detection.** MOBOTIX describes SwimEye as a camera system mounted underwater in pools to detect swimmers in distress and alert lifeguards. Ellis Aquatic Vigilance markets AI video monitoring for aquatic facilities and says its system can alert staff before a person is submerged and unresponsive. The cited pages do not establish validation for unsupervised open lakes, drains, or retention ponds. ([MOBOTIX SwimEye](https://www.mobotix.com/en/swimeye), [Ellis Aquatic Vigilance](https://eaisafety.com/services/ellis-aquatic-vigilance-system-eavs/))
- **Park and lake safety.** PUB's reservoir patrol and Seoul's planned river and wetland patrols monitor activity or detect anomalies near water. Anyang's similar details remain snippet-only. ([PUB](https://www.pub.gov.sg/Resources/News-Room/PressReleases/2021/05/PUB-to-deploy-autonomous-drones-to-enhance-reservoir-monitoring), [Seoul](https://english.seoul.go.kr/safety-surveillance-drones-to-protect-citizens-from-accidents/), [Anyang](https://anyang.go.kr/main/selectPressRelease.do?bbsNo=1687&key=4107&nttNo=443239))

**Analyst's read:** Rescue payload delivery, distress detection, equipment-status monitoring, and edge-condition maintenance solve different operational tasks. GFE overlaps with Safe Edge's proposed missing-buoy check. None of these cited pages describes independent re-testing after an edge repair.

**Analyst's read:** Safe Edge can propose an upstream inspection and repair-tracking role, but it cannot claim to prevent drowning or replace distress detection and rescue. The Star's drowning statistics describe the problem; they do not evaluate Safe Edge. ([The Star, drowning figures](https://www.thestar.com.my/news/nation/2026/10/16/371-youngsters-lost-to-drowning))

The page describes ASCEND functions for asset management, contract administration, and feedback and incident management, with digital process flows and documentation. ([SLA Smart Solutions](https://www.sla.gov.sg/properties/management-of-state-land-and-properties/smart-solutions/))

([OneService, facade inspections](https://www.oneservice.gov.sg/awards/oneservice-awards/award-categories/drones-and-ai-for-building-facade-inspections/))

The Flying Glass page did not open and is not relied on. **Analyst's read:** Targeted searches did not surface a public post-handover water-edge drone programme for Gamuda Land, SP Setia, IOI Properties, or EcoWorld. This is not proof that no private or unpublished programme exists. ([Flying Glass](https://www.flyingglass.com.au/drone-services-for-property-developers/))

Sime Darby Property's 2025 Integrated Annual Report says it had 27 active townships and describes Procore as a platform for construction quality, inspections, project communication, CPC inspection traceability, and defect rectification. Its 2025 Sustainability Report describes wetlands, town parks, and water-retention areas as flood-mitigation infrastructure and community amenities, and says drainage systems are routinely inspected and desilted, particularly around monsoon seasons. ([SDP Integrated Annual Report 2025](https://www.simedarbyproperty.com/sites/default/files/2026-04/SDP%20IAR2025%20Mobile.pdf), [SDP Sustainability Report 2025](https://www.simedarbyproperty.com/sites/default/files/2026-04/SDP%20SR%202025%20Mobile.pdf))

**Analyst's read:** A water-edge workflow could extend SDP's documented inspection practice. The reports do not establish post-handover ownership of any particular edge asset or an available Procore integration interface.

### 6. Close patent check

EP4186046A1, “Property monitoring and management using a drone,” has a listed priority date of 21 July 2020 and publication date of 31 May 2023. Its independent method claim uses images outside a property to determine that a person is approaching or has entered, identify a property state, choose a drone action, and direct the drone to that person. ([Google Patents, EP4186046A1](https://patents.google.com/patent/EP4186046A1/en))

**Analyst's read:** The published claim concerns person/property security and interaction. This is a high-level reading of one published application, not a legal scope conclusion, patentability assessment, or freedom-to-operate search.

**Analyst's read:** Safe Edge's defensible contribution is the proposed operating protocol: flag a visible hazard, keep a human reviewer in control, route an approved repair to a named owner, independently re-test the repaired area, and let the internal status expire. The sources reviewed here do not establish that sequence as globally novel.

**Analyst's read:** PUB Smart Drain Inspector is the strongest challenge from this scan. A judge could see Safe Edge as applying drone-based AI inspection to a new site type. The answer should show the open-water edge asset scope, accountable ownership, closure evidence, and the AI's limited role as a flagging aid rather than a safety certifier.

## Recommendations for Safe Edge

1. **Analyst's read:** Pitch an accountable township water-edge maintenance loop, not the first drone, AI inspector, or water-safety patrol.
2. **Analyst's read:** Bound the pilot to one SDP-retained water asset with a named owner and repair budget. Confirm ownership of each fence, cover, bank, path, sign, and rescue item before accepting it into the system.
3. **Analyst's read:** Demonstrate a real baseline image or record, a human-confirmed flag, and a post-repair re-test. Show how “Fix Lens” reveals routes left open by a proposed barrier, but do not present that view as proof of safety.
4. **Analyst's read:** Treat “pass” as an expiring internal inspection status with a named reviewer, date, scope, and unresolved exceptions. Do not imply AI certification or guaranteed safety.
5. **Analyst's read:** Keep rescue deployment and live drowning detection outside the first pilot. Measure confirmed defects, time to review, time to repair, and re-test results. Make no claim about deaths prevented without outcome evidence.

## What we can and cannot claim

**Safe claims**

- The Star reported 371 child and teen drowning deaths nationwide from 2023 through August 2026. It separately reported 82 all-age drowning incidents at lakes and mining ponds. ([The Star](https://www.thestar.com.my/news/nation/2026/10/16/371-youngsters-lost-to-drowning))
- SDP's 2025 Sustainability Report describes wetlands, town parks, and water-retention areas as flood infrastructure and community amenities, as well as routine drainage inspection and desilting. ([SDP Sustainability Report 2025](https://www.simedarbyproperty.com/sites/default/files/2026-04/SDP%20SR%202025%20Mobile.pdf))
- SDP's 2025 Integrated Annual Report describes Procore's use for construction quality, inspections, defect rectification, and traceability. ([SDP Integrated Annual Report 2025](https://www.simedarbyproperty.com/sites/default/files/2026-04/SDP%20IAR2025%20Mobile.pdf))
- Safe Edge is a proposed human-reviewed workflow for flagging visible changes and supporting repair tracking. The pack contains no field-accuracy or operational-deployment evidence.
- **Analyst's read:** The scan found prior art across drone inspection, reservoir patrol, river warnings, rescue, distress detection, and property-maintenance workflows. Present Safe Edge as a specific combination and application, not a first-of-its-kind category.

**Claims to avoid**

- **Analyst's read:** Avoid “first,” “only,” or “no competitor does this.” PUB, Anyang's snippet, Seoul, Dutch water boards, and commercial vendors show overlap across several components. ([PUB](https://www.pub.gov.sg/Industry/Events-and-Programmes/Global-Innovation-Challenge/Challenges/2023/Smart-Drain-Inspector), [SkyDike](https://wbd-rd.nl/innovation/projects/inv_skydike))
- **Analyst's read:** Avoid “prevents drowning,” “saves lives,” or detection-accuracy claims until a real pilot measures those outcomes.
- **Analyst's read:** Avoid saying a drone or AI certifies an edge as safe. An aerial image alone cannot verify submerged or occluded conditions.
- **Analyst's read:** Do not imply the Tasik Bukit Raja death was caused by a missing barrier or another site defect; the story reports no such defect. ([The Star](https://www.thestar.com.my/news/nation/2026/10/16/missing-fisherman-found-dead-at-tasik-bukit-raja))
- **Analyst's read:** Do not claim a live SDP deployment, confirmed post-handover ownership, or an approved autonomous flight until SDP and relevant authorities confirm them. The public SDP reports confirm Procore use but do not establish an available integration interface.

## Open questions

- Which Elmina Central Park water asset is retained by SDP, and who owns its fence, drain cover, bank, path, signs, and rescue equipment?
- Who approves a finding, funds a repair, performs the independent re-test, and decides when an internal status expires?
- What real site imagery, asset records, maps, and water-level data can the team use, and what permissions cover them?
- What drone operator, flight permissions, privacy controls, and weather or rain limits would SDP require for a live pilot?
- Which SDP system should receive a reviewed issue, and what integration access can SDP provide?
- What pilot evidence would justify expansion: confirmed-defect rate, false-alert burden, review time, closure time, or re-test results?
