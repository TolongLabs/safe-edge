# Green developments

What leading developers and building owners in Malaysia and Singapore do on green buildings and townships, what they measure and who pays, as of 2026-10-10. It shows which green features are now normal, which are still rare and where Sime Darby Property (SDP) stands. Sources are 2024 to 2026 owner reports, official registers and releases; property-level energy and solar commitments by peers are summarised in [Energy, HVAC and solar](/docs/research/energy-hvac-solar.md#what-peer-developers-do) and aren't repeated.

## What is normal now

Each practice below appears at many of the roughly 50 properties checked. The grouping is our inference.

- **Green ratings:** GBI, GreenRE, LEED or BCA Green Mark on nearly every flagship. Sunway's Green Township Policy, adopted in 2023, requires every new township from 2025 to be green-certified ([Sunway Property][sunway-prop]).
- **Rooftop solar, mostly reported as a group total:** Sunway generated 22 GWh across 34 assets in 2025 ([Sunway SR 2025][sunway-sr25]); EcoWorld met 20% of its electricity from 3,351 kWp of on-site solar ([EcoWorld SR 2025][ecoworld-sr25]). S P Setia, EcoWorld and Mah Sing sell solar-ready homes.
- **Building basics:** efficient lighting and sensors, rainwater harvesting, EV charging bays, covered walkways, parks and lakes.
- **Species surveys:** Gamuda Gardens recorded 50 bird, 6 mammal, 4 reptile and 185 insect species in 2024 ([Gamuda Land][gamuda-gardens]); Matrix Concepts recorded 72 species across about 660 ha of Bandar Sri Sendayan and restored 18.88 ha of green space ([Matrix SR 2025][matrix]); Sunway City Kuala Lumpur counts 22 bird species ([Sunway SR 2024][sunway-sr24]).
- **Cooling upgrades at malls:** Sunway Pyramid's Oasis refurbishment replaced 11 air-handling units, upgraded six lifts and added 15 pairs of motion-sensor escalators, about RM6.8 million of the cost ([Sunway REIT SR 2024][sreit-24]).
- **Green-labelled sales:** S P Setia counts a unit as "green-enabled" by its features (Green Switch, solar PV, solar conduit, efficient fittings, rainwater harvesting), not by certification. Such units were 41% of its 2025 launch sales, about RM2 billion; of the properties sold, 79% had Green Switch, 45% solar PV and 38% rainwater harvesting ([S P Setia IR 2025][setia-ir25]).

## What is still rare

Each practice below was found at only one or two properties.

- **Cooling billed per home:** Tengah's opt-in shared cooling meters each flat and shows use in an app ([Tengah](#tengahs-shared-cooling)).
- **A district digital twin:** Punggol Digital District's platform takes live data from more than 20,000 sensors ([Punggol](#punggol-digital-district)).
- **Paying tenants to save:** CDL rebates tenants' energy savings dollar for dollar ([Tenant incentives](#tenant-incentives)).
- **Positive-energy buildings:** Gamuda's Wetlands Arboretum Centre runs on 100% solar PV and reports RM25,758 a year in energy-cost savings in its 2025 National Energy Awards entry ([NEA 2025][nea-wac]).
- **Solar with no developer capital:** UEM Sunrise contracted 1,890 kWp at The Beat 2.0, Kiara Bay, to BM Green Energy under a power purchase agreement ([UEM Sunrise, June 2025][uem-beat2]). Completion isn't confirmed.
- **Renting homeowners' roofs:** SDP's CREAM pilot at Elmina is the only one found ([Where SDP stands](#where-sdp-stands)).
- **Township water from a lake:** Sunway's plant treats South Quay lake water to drinking standard and can supply up to 8.5 million litres a day ([Sunway][sunway-env]).
- **Automated waste collection:** Mah Sing's M Azura ([Mah Sing][mazura]) and Tengah's pneumatic system ([HDB][tengah-annex]).
- **Measured results per property,** covered next.

## Ratings are mostly promises

- **Ratings come at design stage.** Of the 676 GreenRE ratings issued from 2024 to 2026, 465 are provisional, valid until a year after the certificate of completion ([GreenRE database][greenre]). Gamuda Cove's GBI township Gold is a "Final Planning" assessment; final certification comes only when the township is finished, expected just before 2040 ([Gamuda][gamuda-esg]).
- **Few owners publish measured results for one property.** The clearest found:
  - IOI City Mall's 15,757 panels met 8.2% of its electricity and saved RM5.4 million in FY2025, and its total energy use fell 2.7% ([IOI IAR 2025][ioi-iar25]).
  - UEM Sunrise's 1,004 kWp of solar at Kiara Bay ran all of 2025 with 100% of output used on site ([UEM Sunrise IAR 2025][uem-iar25]).
  - Gamuda's arboretum centre, above.
- **Design figures read like results.** Merdeka 118's LEED Platinum (2024) comes with 43% less water and 28% better energy efficiency than a baseline, which are LEED design figures, not meter readings ([Terrapin][terrapin]). JTC reports 35% lower carbon at Punggol Digital District without a baseline or method ([JTC, September 2026][jtc-pdd]).
- **Sources disagree.** IOI City Mall's solar appears as 7.56, 7.569 and 7.9 MWp ([The Edge][edge-1u]; [IOI, November 2024][ioi-roadmap]; [IOI, October 2024][ioi-ceo]). Paramount calls Uptown Residences 1 GreenRE Gold while the GreenRE database lists final Bronze ([Paramount AR 2025][paramount]). Sunway City Kuala Lumpur fell from a 3-Diamond Low Carbon City rating in 2024 to 2-Diamond in 2025, unexplained ([Sunway SR 2025][sunway-sr25]).
- **Solar covers little of a mall.** IOI City Mall's 8.2% is the best found; solar was 1.75% of Sunway REIT's electricity in FY2025 ([Sunway REIT SR 2025][sreit-25]). Inference: in malls and offices, cooling moves the bill more than rooftop solar.

## Where SDP stands

- **Ratings:** the GreenRE database lists 19 SDP ratings issued from 2024 to 2026, across 13 projects. All but one are provisional; Maya Ara Residences is the only final rating (Gold, January 2026). City of Elmina Phases 1 to 3 hold provisional township Platinum (June 2025), as do BBRX Business Park Phases 1 to 5 (February 2024). Elmina Lakeside Mall is provisional Silver (October 2024) ([GreenRE database][greenre]).
- **Smart Township Malaysia 2025:** KPKT rated 5 of 15 developer applications ([Bernama][kpkt]). They are Sunway City Iskandar Puteri (Platinum), Cyberjaya and Gamuda's twentyfive7 (Gold), and Gamuda Cove and Gamuda Gardens (Silver) ([Sunway Property][sunway-smart]; [StarProperty][cyberjaya]; [Gamuda Land][gamuda-acc]). None is an SDP township, and whether SDP applied isn't public. The rating scores seven pillars against MS ISO 37122:2019.
- **Solar:** about 2% of SDP's electricity in 2025. Its CREAM pilot, where Elmina homeowners lease their roofs at no upfront cost, is the only residential roof-leasing scheme found among peers. Details are in [Sime Darby Property](/docs/research/sime-darby-property.md#energy-cooling-and-solar-measures).

## Malaysian townships

- **Sunway City Kuala Lumpur:** 800 acres, 40% green and blue space, electric buses on elevated lanes and a 4.3 km canopy walkway ([Sunway][sunway-env]; [Sunway SR 2024][sunway-sr24]).
- **Sunway City Iskandar Puteri:** 40% green and open space and more than 534,000 trees and shrubs planted ([Sunway SR 2025][sunway-sr25]).
- **Gamuda Cove:** a 90-acre wetlands arboretum built with FRIM and Universiti Malaya, an e-tram, EV chargers and bike paths ([Gamuda Land, July 2026][gamuda-nature]).
- **Gamuda Gardens:** eight lakes supply all of the park's landscape irrigation ([Gamuda Parks][gamuda-parks]).
- **S P Setia:** smart-home and energy features in 28 projects by December 2025; Setia Federal Hill got Malaysia's first LEED for Neighbourhood Development Platinum in 2025 ([S P Setia IR 2025][setia-ir25]).
- **Mah Sing M Azura:** GreenRE Silver, provisional (2024), with EV charging and solar-ready systems ([GreenRE database][greenre]; [Mah Sing][mazura]).

## Malaysian malls and offices

- **IOI City Mall:** solar in two phases, RM10 million in 2020 and RM8 million in 2024 ([IOI][ioi-hlib]). Phase 2 gained GBI certification in May 2025 ([IOI IAR 2025][ioi-iar25]).
- **1 Utama:** more than 5.7 MWp of solar on roofs and car parks. Its ice and chilled-water storage meets 40% to 50% of peak daytime cooling and cuts peak cooling costs by about 40% ([The Edge, September 2026][edge-1u], citing the mall's 2024 report).
- **Sunway REIT:** its Green Lease Partnership Programme covered 92% of retail and 96% of office tenants in FY2025 ([Sunway REIT SR 2025][sreit-25]); it aims to certify all its properties green by 2034 ([Sunway REIT SR 2024][sreit-24]). No savings-sharing terms were found.
- **Merdeka 118:** LEED Platinum in 2024 ([Bernama][merdeka]); 70% of first-year energy bought as green power and all hot water from rooftop solar thermal ([Terrapin][terrapin]).
- **S P Setia headquarters:** GBI Platinum renewed in June 2026 with 94 of 100 points, including all 35 energy-efficiency points ([S P Setia][setia-hq]).
- **MRCB Penang Sentral:** voltage optimisation is forecast to cut operational emissions by 8% (220 tCO2e) in 2026; rooftop solar is planned for 2029 to 2030 ([MRCB IAR 2025][mrcb]).
- **Mall recycling:** from June 2026, malls need recycling facilities to apply for or renew a business licence ([Sime Darby Property](/docs/research/sime-darby-property.md#reporting-pressure-on-sdp)).

## Singapore

### Tengah's shared cooling

- **The town:** Tengah is the first HDB town planned with town-wide smart technology, including smart common-area lighting, solar power for common services, rainwater harvesting and pneumatic waste conveyance ([HDB][tengah-annex]).
- **How it works:** rooftop chiller plants send chilled water to an optional fan-coil unit in each flat. Each household has its own meter, pays per kWrh on its SP bill and tracks use in the SP app ([MyTengah][tengah-ccs]). The Q4 2026 rate is 10 cents per kWrh before GST ([MyTengah][tengah-rate]).
- **Uptake:** more than 9,000 subscribers by 12 February 2024, 70% of leaseholders at the time ([SP update][tengah-update]).
- **Problems:** early blocks had condensation and workmanship issues, and about 80 cases were still open in February 2024 ([SP update][tengah-update]).
- **Savings claim:** SP's model puts a four-room flat's yearly life-cycle cost at S$1,218, against S$1,742 for split units, about 30% less on its own assumptions ([SP statement, updated October 2025][tengah-sp]). No measured comparison was found.
- **Operators:** in 2024 HDB gave Keppel a 20-year contract to supply the cooling for three more Tengah projects ([HDB, 2024][keppel]).

### Punggol Digital District

- **Ratings:** Green Mark Districts Platinum in May 2024, with 17 buildings at Platinum and 3 at Platinum Super Low Energy ([JTC, May 2024][jtc-gm]).
- **District cooling:** ENGIE runs plants of more than 30,000 refrigeration tons ([JTC, May 2024][jtc-gm]). Each tenant signs its own chilled-water agreement with ENGIE and negotiates the rate ([JTC tender FAQ][jtc-faq]).
- **Smart grid:** Univers and PacificLight are building solar, batteries, smart meters and demand response; it was still in design in November 2024 ([JTC][jtc-grid]). JTC reported more than 3,000 MWh a year of solar in September 2026 ([JTC][jtc-pdd]).
- **Digital twin:** the Open Digital Platform takes data from more than 20,000 sensors, and Panasonic began testing facility-management tools on live data in July 2026 ([JTC][jtc-panasonic]). No measured savings were published.

### Tenant incentives

- **CDL:** its 12-month City Green Tenant Bonus, launched July 2024, pays Republic Plaza tenants CDL vouchers equal to their energy savings ([CDL][cdl-bonus]). A machine-learning model for City House's chillers, with smart lighting at Republic Plaza, cut air-conditioning and ventilation energy by about 12% ([CDL AR 2024][cdl-ar]).
- **CICT:** green leases set minimum standards for tenant equipment and lighting; over 96% of its Singapore lettable area had adopted them by the end of 2024 ([CICT SR 2024][cict]).
- **CapitaLand's pilots:** AI video analytics at IMM detected unsafe practices with at least 80% accuracy; new air filters at a US property saved 890 kWh per air-handling unit a year ([CapitaLand, October 2024][capitaland]).

## Who pays

| Model              | Example                   | Who pays, who gains                                                                |
| ------------------ | ------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Owner capital      | IOI City Mall             | The owner paid RM18 million over two phases; it reports RM5.4 million saved a year |
| Roof lease         | SDP CREAM, Elmina         | Homeowners pay nothing and earn roof rent; SDP's Innovation Park takes the power   |
| No-capital PPA     | UEM Sunrise, The Beat 2.0 | A solar firm funds the system; the building buys its power                         |
| Metered cooling    | Tengah                    | Residents opt in and pay per kWrh; an operator owns the plant                      |
| Negotiated cooling | Punggol Digital District  | Each tenant agrees a chilled-water rate with ENGIE                                 |
| Green lease        | Sunway REIT, CICT         | Tenants commit to standards; no savings-sharing terms published                    |
| Tenant rebate      | CDL Republic Plaza        | The landlord pays vouchers equal to tenants' energy savings                        |

Sources are in the sections above. Inference: an idea that saves energy should say which of these models it uses, or who keeps the saving.

## What this means for an idea

Each point is our inference from the evidence above.

- **Features and ratings don't stand out.** Solar-ready homes, LEDs, rainwater tanks, EV bays and green ratings are normal among SDP's peers.
- **Proof stands out.** Few owners show measured results for one property, and all but one of SDP's ratings are provisional. An idea that measures a green feature after handover and shows the result in kWh, litres, species or ringgit fills a real gap, and could help a rating become final.
- **Cooling beats solar for malls and offices.** Rooftop solar met 2% to 8% of the electricity found; cooling storage and air-handling upgrades are where peers spend.
- **Engagement is a gap in Malaysia.** Singapore has cooling billed per home with an app, and landlords paying tenants to save. In Malaysia only Sunway REIT's green lease was found, with no savings-sharing terms.
- **Tengah shows the risk.** Shared cooling raised condensation complaints and needed a 20-year operator; a demo should show how faults get handled.
- **Smart Township Malaysia is a ready scorecard.** Its seven pillars follow MS ISO 37122, and no SDP township is among the five rated so far.

## Unverified and not found

- An HDB page says Keppel's contracts now cover 12 Tengah projects and about 14,000 households, but its body wouldn't load.
- NUS's SDE4 building generated more solar than it used in 2022, per a page that renders only in a browser; it is unchecked and the data predates 2024.
- Setia City Mall Phase 2's GBI Silver (2025 to 2028) was seen only in a directory search result.
- No Malaysian residential project publishes how energy or water savings reach residents, or how service charges are split.
- No 2024 or later measured rainwater saving, or mall food-waste tonnage, was found for a Malaysian property.
- Older flagships such as the Diamond Building, the Green Energy Office, TRX and Menara KEN were left out, because no 2024 or later source backs their figures.
