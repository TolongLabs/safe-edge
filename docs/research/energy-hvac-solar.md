# Energy, HVAC and solar

What Malaysia's rules, prices and companies say about air-conditioning, solar and ESG reporting, as of 2026-10-10. It tests our hunch that HVAC and solar are what Malaysian corporates focus on for ESG. Sources are 2024 to 2026; the one older figure is labelled, because nothing newer exists. SDP's own energy numbers are in [Sime Darby Property](/docs/research/sime-darby-property.md#performance-against-the-targets).

## Verdict on the hunch

Mostly true for energy, not proven as "the top ESG priority".

- **Companies are acting on both.** In EY's 2026 survey of Malaysian businesses, 72% are considering on-site renewables such as rooftop solar and 80% want digital energy-management tools ([EY, January 2026][ey]). In PwC's 2024 survey, 85% of Malaysian CEOs were improving energy efficiency ([PwC, 2024][pwc-energy]).
- **The law now pushes HVAC.** The Energy Efficiency and Conservation Act 2024 forces large energy users to hire energy managers and run audits, and office buildings to carry energy labels. Air-conditioner standards rose in 2026, and government offices must keep air-conditioning at 24°C or warmer.
- **Cooling is the biggest load in commercial buildings.** The newest national figure found is 42% of commercial electricity (2016 data).
- **But no survey ranks HVAC or solar first.** The pain points companies report are ESG data quality, skills, financing and measuring impact ([PwC, gaps in reporting][pwc-gaps]; [Deloitte, 2025][deloitte]).
- **For SDP, energy is the whole story.** Purchased electricity is 95% of its Scope 1 and 2 emissions, and those emissions rose 24% from 2023 to 2025 ([SDP Sustainability Report 2025][sdp-sr]).
- **The pattern holds abroad.** HVAC and cooling products recur among CapitaLand's challenge winners ([Past winners](/docs/research/past-winners.md#developer-and-township-challenges-abroad)).

## Air-conditioning and cooling

### How much energy cooling uses

- **Commercial buildings:** space cooling took 42% of commercial-sector electricity, 16,441 of 39,106 GWh, in the Energy Commission's 2016 survey of 5,000 premises ([ERIA, 2021][eria]). Pre-2024 (2016 data); no newer national figure was found.
- **Homes:** 68.8% of Malaysian households owned an air-conditioner in 2024, up from 61.7% in 2022 ([DOSM Basic Amenities Survey 2024][dosm-ac]).
- **One unit:** a typical 1.5 HP room unit uses about 785 kWh a year at today's market-average efficiency, assuming 5.4 hours a day ([UNEP U4E, 2024][u4e]).
- **Inverters:** about 70% of room units sold in 2023 were fixed-speed, though 65% of models on shelves were inverters ([UNEP U4E][u4e]).

### Rules now in force

- **Energy Efficiency and Conservation Act 2024 (Act 861)** started on 1 January 2025 in Peninsular Malaysia and Labuan ([ST enforcement notice][eeca-start]).
  - It covers any business using at least 21,600 GJ in 12 months at one place, equal to 6 GWh if it is all electricity ([ST, energy consumers][eeca-consumer]).
  - After ST's notice, the consumer must appoint a registered energy manager within 3 months, run an energy management system within a year, and submit an audit within a year, then every five years ([ST, energy consumers][eeca-consumer]).
  - ST said the first notices would go out from January 2026; 2025 reporting was voluntary ([ST, transition][eeca-transition]).
  - Office buildings of 8,000 m² gross floor area or more need an energy intensity label, renewed yearly, with at least two stars from the fifth year. Five stars means 90 kWh/m²/year or less; one star is above 200 ([ST building guideline, 2026][eeca-building]).
  - Fines reach RM50,000 for core failures such as not appointing an energy manager ([Act 861][act861]).
- **Air-conditioner standards:** from 2026 to 2029 a wall-mounted split unit up to 7.1 kW needs at least two stars, a seasonal efficiency (CSPF) of 4.10 below 4.5 kW. The floor rises to 6.09 in 2030 ([ST product guideline, 2026][meps]).
- **24°C rule:** government offices must set air-conditioning to at least 24°C from 5 April 2026. A UPM dean estimated each 1°C rise saves about 7% of cooling energy; that is an estimate, not a measured result ([Bernama][bernama-24c]).
- **Selangor building by-law 38A:** new or renovated non-residential buildings with over 4,000 m² of air-conditioned space must meet MS 1525 envelope limits and run an energy management system ([Selangor UBBL amendment][ubbl]).
- **Refrigerants:** Malaysia froze HFC use at its baseline in 2024 and must cut 10% by 2029, 30% by 2035, 50% by 2040 and 80% by 2045. Making and importing R-407C split units is banned from 1 January 2029 ([DOE Kigali plan, 2025][kigali]).

### Tariffs and time of use

The Peninsular schedule runs from 1 July 2025 to 31 December 2027 ([tariff schedule][tariff]). A monthly fuel adjustment (AFA) is added on top ([ST release][st-tariff]).

| Customer                    | Energy charge, sen/kWh                        | Other charges                                      |
| --------------------------- | --------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------- |
| Home, up to 1,500 kWh       | 27.03; time of use 28.52 peak, 24.43 off-peak | Capacity 4.55, network 12.85 sen/kWh; RM10 a month |
| Home, above 1,500 kWh       | 37.03; time of use 38.52 peak, 34.43 off-peak | Same                                               |
| Shop or office, low voltage | 27.03; time of use 28.52 peak, 24.43 off-peak | Capacity 8.83, network 14.82 sen/kWh; RM20 a month |
| Medium voltage (malls)      | 29.83; time of use 31.32 peak, 27.23 off-peak | Capacity and network charged per kW of demand      |

- **Peak hours** are 2 p.m. to 10 p.m., Monday to Friday. Off-peak covers 10 p.m. to 2 p.m. on weekdays, all weekend and 15 public holidays ([TNB][tnb-tou]).
- **Energy Efficiency Incentive:** homes using up to 1,000 kWh a month get a rebate from 25 sen/kWh down to 0.5 sen/kWh ([ST release][st-tariff]).
- **Our read:** peak cooling costs 4.09 sen/kWh more than off-peak, so pre-cooling before 2 p.m. or shifting load matters most for medium-voltage sites, which also pay for peak demand.

### What building owners already do

- **AI chiller optimisation:** ADF Technologies' ADF OMNI combines IoT sensors, building-management data and deep learning; MDEC reports about 20% chiller-plant savings at Menara HLX, where the plant used over 60% of building energy ([MDEC][mdec]). A supplier case, not independently verified.
- **Energy performance contracts:** Susten reports a 500 RT Kuala Lumpur office chiller plant going from 1.257 to about 0.69–0.74 kW/RT since 2021, and 23.1 million kWh saved across its portfolio to June 2026 ([Susten results][susten]).
- **Government help:** SEDA's NUR:CHILLER 2026 programme funds chiller projects, allows ESCO contracts, requires before-and-after verification and a cooling-load profile to avoid oversizing, and ends 31 December 2026 ([SEDA][nurchiller]).
- **District cooling:** Megajana runs two Cyberjaya plants with 22,000 RT capacity and 97,500 RTh of thermal storage ([Megajana][megajana]).
- **Retrofits:** replacing a water-cooled chiller with VRF cut one building's ACMV energy from 21,274 to 16,397 kWh in a 2025 UiTM study ([UiTM][uitm-vrf]).

### Documented pain points

- **No visibility per tenant or area:** at Great Eastern Mall, "a single utility meter cannot help the team manage cost", and energy data was "only available after the fact" ([Cobler case study, 2025][cobler]). A vendor account of one site.
- **Humidity:** in a 2024 classroom study, the fan-coil unit couldn't control 77% humidity without overcooling ([UTHM][uthm-humid]).
- **Price over efficiency:** importers say upfront price keeps fixed-speed units popular, and contractors lack inverter-installation skills ([UNEP U4E][u4e]).
- **Refrigerant leaks count as emissions:** SDP's Scope 1 rose 21% in 2025, partly from refrigerant refills after major air-conditioning repairs ([SDP SR 2025][sdp-sr]).
- No representative Malaysian study measures split incentives between landlords and tenants, poor maintenance or oversizing.

## Solar

### Schemes in 2026

| Scheme                   | What it does                                                                                      | Key rules                                                                                                                                                                                                             |
| ------------------------ | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Solar ATAP               | Rooftop solar for self-use, exporting the surplus; replaced NEM, applications from 1 January 2026 | Homes up to 5 kW (single-phase) or 15 kW (three-phase). Home exports credited at the energy charge only; credits can't offset AFA; unused credit is lost; up to 10 years ([ST guideline][atap-st]; [SEDA][atap-seda]) |
| NEM 3.0                  | Net metering with one-for-one offset                                                              | Closed on 30 June 2025 ([SEDA][nem])                                                                                                                                                                                  |
| SELCO                    | Self-consumption with no export                                                                   | Non-domestic systems above 1 MWac need at least 1 hour of battery storage from 1 July 2026 ([ST][selco])                                                                                                              |
| CREAM                    | Pools home rooftops to supply nearby green buyers over the grid; launched March 2025              | Community access charge 9 sen/kWh ([Single Buyer][cream]; [PETRA][cress-charge])                                                                                                                                      |
| CRESS                    | Companies buy green power from a developer's plant over the grid; from September 2024             | Plants at least 30 MW; access charge 20 sen (firm) or 40 sen (non-firm) per kWh; a 2026 acceleration package offers 14 sen ([ST][cress]; [PETRA, September 2026][cress-2026])                                         |
| Green Electricity Tariff | TNB customers pay a premium for renewable certificates                                            | 3 to 5 sen/kWh; the 2025 quota of 6,600 GWh was fully subscribed by July 2026 ([mGATS][get])                                                                                                                          |
| Large Scale Solar 6      | Utility-scale bidding                                                                             | 2,500 MW solar with storage plus 150 MW, announced July 2026, operating by end-2029 ([PETRA][lss6])                                                                                                                   |

- Solar ATAP has no fixed overall quota, but the government may cap it if grid stability is threatened ([PETRA, December 2025][atap-petra]).
- **Our read:** crediting home exports at the energy charge, not one-for-one as under NEM, makes self-consumption matter more. Using solar power at home during the day, for example for pre-cooling, is worth more than exporting it.

### Home solar economics

- **Size:** 5 to 6 kWp suits a terrace or smaller semi-detached house, at about RM18,000 to RM30,000 ([Solar100, 2026][solar100-size]). Vendor estimate.
- **Cost:** about RM3,000 to RM4,000 per kWp ([Solar100 price guide, August 2026][solar100-cost]). Vendor estimate; no official 2025 or 2026 price survey exists.
- **Yield at Elmina:** about 1,382 kWh per kWp a year by PVGIS ([Open data](/docs/research/open-data.md#energy-and-solar)), against SEDA's planning figure of 1,200 for the central region ([SEDA homeowner guide][seda-guide]).
- **Payback:** 5 to 7 years for a 6 to 9 kWp system under the old NEM rules ([Suncatcher, 2025][suncatcher]). No Solar ATAP payback study was found.
- **Financing:** Solar ATAP allows zero-upfront leases and power purchase agreements through registered investors ([SEDA][atap-seda]). Maybank finances up to RM100,000 over 10 years ([Maybank][maybank-solar]).

### Uptake and barriers

- **National:** Malaysia had 5,777.73 MW of solar at the end of 2025 and met its interim target of 31% renewable capacity ([IEA PVPS, 2026][iea-pvps]). The 2050 target is 70% renewable capacity ([NETR, search excerpt][netr]).
- **Homes:** by the end of 2024, SEDA's annual report lists 45,161 operating NEM Rakyat home systems, 346.46 MW ([SEDA Annual Report 2024, search excerpt][seda-ar24]). No 2025 or 2026 count was found.
- **Barriers:** a 2025 student survey of Kuala Lumpur homeowners found high upfront cost, few reliable installers and knowledge gaps ([TAR UMT][taruc]). Strata roofs are common property under the JMB ([Seremban City Council][mbs-jmb]). TNB publishes a hosting-capacity map for its medium-voltage network ([TNB, search excerpt][tnb-der]).

### Shared solar and storage

- **SDP's CREAM programme** with TNB targets 450 homes and 4.5 MWp across City of Elmina and Bandar Bukit Raja; TNB's FY2025 report still called the residential system "upcoming" ([TNB IAR 2025][tnb-iar]).
- **SDP's commercial rooftops:** TNB reports 14 SDP commercial sites with 1.7 MWp, avoiding about 1,500 tCO2e a year ([TNB IAR 2025][tnb-iar]). This differs from SDP's own 603 kWp across nine sites ([SDP SR 2025][sdp-sr]); the sources don't explain the gap.
- **Storage at Elmina:** TNB installed three community energy storage units (76.8 to 170 kWh each) in City of Elmina to store solar power for local use. No results are published ([TNB][tnb-cess]).
- **Solar EV charging:** SEDA tendered a solar-powered DC fast charger with battery storage and a mobile app in December 2025 ([SEDA tender][seda-tender]).

### Who already sells what

- **Monitoring apps already exist:** Pekat's Solaroo app sends system alerts and performance updates ([Solaroo][solaroo]); Sunview lets owners monitor their system in real time from a phone ([Sunview][sunview]); Solarvest monitors systems through Powertrack ([Solarvest, search excerpt][solarvest]).
- **Quotes and calculators:** GSPARX, TNB's solar arm, offers home packages and a quotation calculator ([GSPARX, search excerpt][gsparx]).
- **Roof-level potential:** Google's Solar API now covers Malaysian homes ([Open data](/docs/research/open-data.md#energy-and-solar)), and a "solar opportunity mapper" is already taken.
- **Our read:** "a solar app that shows generation" is not new. New ground is in how solar power gets used, shared or timed across a township.

## ESG reporting and carbon pricing

- **Reporting:** Main Market companies with a market value of RM2 billion or more, SDP included, report under IFRS S1 and S2 from FY2025 ([SC][sc-nsrf]). Wider Scope 3 disclosures start in FY2027 for that group ([NSRF response paper][nsrf-paper]). Reasonable assurance of Scope 1 and 2 starts for periods from 1 January 2028 ([SC, September 2026][sc-assurance]).
- **Data is the pain:** PwC finds weak data ownership, spreadsheet errors and "absence of a centralised 'source of truth' for ESG data" ([PwC][pwc-gaps]). In Deloitte's CFO survey, 78% struggle to measure ESG impact ([Deloitte][deloitte]).
- **Carbon tax:** planned from 2026 for iron, steel and energy, then deferred on 5 October 2026 ([The Star][carbon-tax]; [MOF, June 2026][mof-carbon]).
- **Carbon market:** Bursa Carbon Exchange traded 50,486 tCO2e of credits in 2025, up 60.9% ([Bursa IAR 2025, search excerpt][bursa-iar]). A 2026 national carbon-market policy plans an emissions trading scheme in a later phase ([NRES reply][nres-carbon]).
- **Low-carbon cities:** the national target is 200 low-carbon zones by 2030; 70 local authorities were implementing measures in February 2026 ([MGTC][mgtc]).

## Unverified and not found

- No national figure newer than 2016 gives air-conditioning's share of electricity, for commercial buildings or homes.
- No measured mall energy-intensity benchmark for Malaysia was found, only award criteria and single buildings.
- No Solar ATAP payback study, official home installation cost survey or 2025 home installation count was found.
- No study quantifies roof unsuitability, JMB solar approvals or TNB connection delays.
- The only peer-to-peer energy trading pilot found was SEDA's 2019 to 2020 sandbox, which is too old to rely on.
- ST's 2026 star table appears to leave CSPF values from 5.10 to below 5.60 unrated for 4.5 to 7.1 kW units; confirm with ST before using those bands.
