# What already exists

Products and services already used in Malaysian townships, condominiums and cities for each focus area, as of 2026-10-10, with SELKitar and SDP App reviews added on 2026-10-16. The organisers reject "an existing app plus AI" and have named ideas that are already taken ([BRIEF, the challenge](/docs/BRIEF.md#the-challenge)), so check an idea against this page before scoring it. Evidence is from 2024 to 2026 unless marked; scale figures are company claims unless stated. Absence from this page doesn't prove absence from the market; see [Checking that an idea is new](/docs/research/methods/ideation-methods.md#checking-that-an-idea-is-new).

## Safety

- **Volunteer Smartphone Patrol (VSP)**, PDRM's app, takes tips with photos, video, GPS and a panic button. Police say it is underused and that they act within 12 minutes of a report ([Malay Mail, May 2024][vsp]).
- **SaveME 999**, part of the new national emergency system NG MERS 999 since November 2025, sends location, video and text to 999 operators ([Malay Mail][saveme]).
- **Skim Rondaan Sukarela**, the Rukun Tetangga resident patrol, needs at least 20 members aged 18 or over ([government portal][srs]).
- No Malaysian residential AI video-analytics product was found; the examples found were malls ([Small Caps][icetana], undated).

## Mobility and parking

- **Demand-responsive vans:** Rapid Penang ran 31 vans in six zones averaging 1,828 passengers a day in May 2025 ([paultan.org][drt-penang]). Rapid KL moved 19 low-ridership routes to on-demand vans from 16 January 2026 ([Malay Mail, excerpt][drt-kl]).
- **Setel** says it serves more than nine million users across fuel, parking and EV charging, with drive-in, drive-out number-plate parking at KLCC ([The Sun, May 2025][setel]).
- **Flexi Parking and Smart Selangor Parking** handle council street parking; a cyberattack took both down from about 30 June to 2 July 2026 ([Malay Mail][flexi]).
- **Carpooling:** Grab Group Ride launched in May 2026 ([Bernama, excerpt][grab-group]).
- **E-scooters:** Subang Jaya runs a two-year e-scooter sandbox in SS15 from February 2025 ([Malay Mail, excerpt][scooter]).

## Energy and cooling

- **myTNB** shows a personal dashboard and usage graph ([App Store][mytnb]). TNB had installed more than 5 million smart meters by September 2025 ([Newswav][smartmeter-5m]), targeting about 10.4 million homes in Peninsular Malaysia by 2028 and beyond ([Bernama][smartmeter-target]).
- **Building energy:** ADF OMNI (AI chiller optimisation), Susten (energy performance contracts) and Megajana (district cooling) serve commercial buildings ([Energy, HVAC and solar](/docs/research/landscape/energy-hvac-solar.md#what-building-owners-already-do)). Cobler installs sub-metering and live energy reporting in malls ([Cobler][cobler]).
- **Smart plugs** with energy monitoring sell on Lazada for about RM20 ([Lazada guide, excerpt][plugs]).
- No live TNB or Energy Commission demand-response programme for homes was found.

## Crowded and open ground

Each point is our read of the evidence above.

- **Crowded:** visitor and guardhouse apps, complaint channels, recycling-for-rewards apps, parking payment, solar monitoring apps, EV charging maps, flood alerts.
- **Thin or missing in Malaysia:** home demand response or energy coaching tied to the new time-of-use tariff; township-level sharing of solar or storage beyond SDP's own pilot; resident-facing tools for older people living alone; anything that measures resident outcomes rather than app usage; and neighbourhood waste facilities, which the national blueprint asks developers to build.
- **Developer apps stop at handover or at the guardhouse.** None found manages a township service after handover, such as energy, cooling, waste or mobility.

## Unverified and not found

- No current usage numbers were found for JaGaApp (2020), i-Neighbour (2022), Doctor Anywhere or Homage in Malaysia.
- No bike-sharing operator, residential AI video product or live home demand-response scheme was found in Malaysia.
- The MGTC household carbon calculator page returned 404, so its grid factor and features are unchecked.
- VSP's 1.7 million registrations and SaveME's download count rest on Bernama pages that wouldn't open.
