## Portfolio at a glance

- SDP reports 27 active townships and developments, more than 100,000 homes and about 12,950 acres of landbank at 31 December 2025 ([Integrated Annual Report 2025][iar2025]). The briefing's figure was 28.
- **City of Elmina** had 67,000 residents in January 2025, with 150,000 expected at completion, over 1,100 acres of green space and a 6,500-acre footprint ([Elmina Lakeside Mall launch][elmina-mall]). Its township page describes a 5,000-acre masterplan, a 300-acre Central Park, 90 km of cycling and jogging tracks and a 2,700-acre neighbouring forest reserve ([City of Elmina][elmina]). SDP doesn't reconcile the 5,000 and 6,500 acre figures.
- **Bandar Bukit Raja** had about 40,000 residents in 2022, with 105,000 expected at full development; SDP calls it "a more mature township" ([Jalan Meru flyover release, 2022][bbr-flyover]).
- **Data centres:** Google's first hyperscale data centre at Elmina Business Park was completed and handed over, with rent starting in Q2 FY2026 ([Q1 FY2026 results][q1-2026]). The two Elmina data-centre leases cover 126 acres with a combined rental value of RM7.6 billion ([IAR 2025][iar2025]).
- **Malls:** KL East Mall (402,894 sq ft), Elmina Lakeside Mall (206,733 sq ft) and KLGCC Mall (227,484 sq ft, opened 24 October 2025) ([IAR 2025][iar2025]). Elmina Lakeside Mall averaged 640,000 visitors a month after opening ([Elmina Lakeside Mall launch][elmina-mall]).
- **New in 2025 and 2026:** Vision Business Park, 760 acres, launched April 2025 ([Vision Business Park][vbp]); Elmina Business Park 2, 600 acres, launched January 2026 ([EBP2][ebp2]); a 556.96-acre Kulai township site bought for RM418.5 million ([Kulai acquisition][kulai]); a RM1.25 billion New Economy Venture fund for data centres and logistics, which is a property fund, not a startup accelerator ([NEV fund][nev]).

## Sustainability Strategy and Roadmap 2030

SDP calls its plan the Sustainability Strategy and Roadmap 2030 (SSR2030), themed "Fostering Resilient Communities", launched in mid-2025. Its four pillars are Energy & Carbon, Urban Biodiversity, Climate Resilience and Community Experience ([Sustainability Report 2025][sr2025]).

[sr2025]: https://www.simedarbyproperty.com/sites/default/files/2026-04/SDP%20SR2025%20%28Website%29.pdf

### Targets

| Target                                    | Deadline | Baseline                            |
| ----------------------------------------- | -------- | ----------------------------------- |
| Cut Scope 1 and 2 emissions by 40%        | 2030     | 2023 (16,387 tCO2e), per the report |
| Net zero                                  | 2050     | Not stated                          |
| Cut operational water-use intensity 30%   | 2030     | Being reset in 2026 from 2025 data  |
| Net-positive biodiversity                 | 2030     | Not stated                          |
| Plant 250,000 trees                       | 2030     | 191,827 planted since 2011          |
| At least 15% green space in each township | 2030     | Not stated                          |

Source: [Sustainability Report 2025][sr2025]. No numeric target exists for Scope 3, renewable energy, energy intensity, waste diversion or community outcomes.

### Performance against the targets

| tCO2e                       |   2023 |   2024 |   2025 |
| --------------------------- | -----: | -----: | -----: |
| Scope 1                     |    537 |    852 |  1,029 |
| Scope 2 (location-based)    | 15,850 | 17,027 | 19,290 |
| **Scope 1 and 2**           | 16,387 | 17,879 | 20,319 |
| Scope 3 (travel, commuting) |  1,989 |  2,775 |  4,284 |

Source: [Sustainability Report 2025][sr2025], checked against the PDF text. The 2025 Scope 1 and 2 figures had external limited assurance.

- **Reported emissions are rising.** Scope 1 and 2 grew 24% from the 2023 baseline, partly from a wider reporting boundary, so the like-for-like trend is unknown. Reaching the 40% cut means getting to about 9,800 tCO2e by 2030, half the 2025 level. **Our read**, from the table.
- **Electricity is the problem.** SDP used 24.9 GWh in 2025. Its asset-management segment (malls and offices) emitted 10,218 tCO2e from electricity, leisure (golf, convention) 6,315 and property development 2,757 ([SR 2025][sr2025]).
- **Scope 1 rose 21%,** partly from "significant refrigerant refills following major air-conditioning repairs" ([SR 2025][sr2025]).
- **Solar is still small.** 603 kWp across nine sites generated 495 MWh and avoided 383 tCO2e in 2025, about 2% of electricity use. SDP also bought 2,312 MWh through TNB's Green Electricity Tariff ([SR 2025][sr2025]).
- **Green ratings are mostly design-stage.** GreenRE lists 19 SDP ratings issued from 2024 to 2026, and only Maya Ara Residences (Gold, January 2026) is final. City of Elmina Phases 1 to 3 hold provisional township Platinum from June 2025 ([GreenRE database][greenre]). No SDP township is among the five Smart Township Malaysia ratings of 2025; see [Green developments](/docs/research/landscape/green-developments.md#where-sdp-stands).
- **Scope 3 covers only travel and commuting.** Construction materials and the energy used in homes SDP sold aren't counted yet; a broader inventory is planned for 2026 ([SR 2025][sr2025]).
- **The report puts the 2025 rise down to a wider boundary:** "Scope 1 and 2 emissions increased by 13.6% compared to 2024 due to boundary expansion" ([SR 2025][sr2025] p. 44). It doesn't name the added assets. **Our read:** the malls that opened in this period (Elmina Lakeside Mall, launched January 2025; KLGCC Mall, opened October 2025) are likely among them.

### Energy, cooling and solar measures

- **Cooling:** a chiller replacement at Sime Darby Convention Centre cut its energy use by 45%; chillers had been 52% of the centre's energy ([Sustainability Report 2024][sr2024]). In 2025, KL East Mall's air-handling-unit optimisation was estimated to save 841 MWh, KLGCC optimised chillers and the convention centre added sub-metering ([SR 2025][sr2025]).
- **Malls:** KL East Mall has chiller controls, HVAC optimisation, tenant sub-metering, motion-sensor LEDs and a 25 kWp rooftop solar system ([SR 2024][sr2024]). Elmina Lakeside Mall is open-air "to reduce air-conditioning use" and has 16 chargEV DC bays ([Elmina Lakeside Mall launch][elmina-mall]).
- **Homes:** SDP describes passive design, roof insulation and natural ventilation at Elmina. It disclosed no residential air-conditioning programme ([SR 2025][sr2025]).
- **Community solar (CREAM):** in a Community Renewable Energy Aggregation Mechanism pilot with TNB's GSPARX, homeowners lease their roofs at no upfront cost and earn roof rent. The power goes through TNB's grid to SDP's Innovation Park within a 5 km radius. The first residential project launched at City of Elmina on 26 September 2025 ([CREAM launch][cream-launch]; [community solar rollout][cream-rollout]). SDP publishes no home count or capacity; TNB says the project will deliver "4.5 MWp for 450 homes at City of Elmina and Bandar Bukit Raja" ([TNB IAR 2025][tnb-iar] p. 103).
- **Solar homes:** Elmina Ridge 1 has 228 houses with rooftop solar and battery storage, 1 MWp in total ([SR 2024][sr2024]).
- **EV charging:** 40 stations company-wide in 2024, including a battery-integrated chargEV hub at KLGCC ([SR 2024][sr2024]).

### Climate risk, nature and community

- **Physical risk:** a desktop assessment with Swiss Re across seven higher-exposure townships found flood exposure at Bandar Universiti Pagoh and Bandar Bukit Raja, plus chronic heat, drought and extreme rain risks. Wider coverage is planned for 2026 and full IFRS S1/S2 alignment for 2027 ([SR 2025][sr2025]).
- **Flood design:** raised platforms and roads, exceedance routes, bigger drains, detention and retention ponds, flap gates and pumps ([SR 2025][sr2025]).
- **Nature:** 191,827 trees planted since 2011, a 2.1 km Elmina Urban Biodiversity Corridor, and partners TRCRC, Wetlands International, FRIM and five universities ([SR 2025][sr2025]).
- **Community:** 76,402 beneficiaries in 2025, up from 53,475 in 2023 ([SR 2025][sr2025]). SDP's customer net promoter score was 88% in 2025, up from 74% in 2024 ([IAR 2025][iar2025] p. 47); the Sustainability Report gives no satisfaction score.
- **Ratings:** FTSE4Good Bursa Malaysia member, FTSE score 3.50, CDP B, MSCI BBB ([SR 2025][sr2025]).

## Technology SDP already offers residents

- **The Sime Darby Property App** covers the buyer's journey: billing, construction progress, e-documents, vacant possession, household members, event RSVPs, referrals and defect reports after key collection. Its older PRIME app was discontinued ([app FAQ][app-faq]).
- **Elmina Valley Community** is a separate township app listed on the App Store, with no ratings ([App Store listing][elmina-app]).
- **Wellness:** Technogym is a township-wellness partner, with app-connected fitness equipment and "AI-driven digital solutions" planned over five years ([SR 2024][sr2024]).
- **Innovation Park:** a 6-acre hub in Elmina City Centre for experiments and collaboration, with a full-scale double-storey home mock-up ([SR 2024][sr2024]). It already receives CREAM solar power, so it is a natural pilot site.
- **Older security work:** 22 solar-powered CCTV cameras linked to police in Ara Damansara (2009) and 32 auxiliary police officers across townships (2016) ([Safe City release][safe-city]; [auxiliary police release][aux-police]). Current status unknown.
- **Construction safety:** SDP is piloting SafePass AI, "which supports multilingual safety documentation", and Guardian AI, "which assists in identifying unsafe acts and behaviours", on its construction sites ([SR 2025][sr2025] p. 47).
- **Not found:** township-wide parcel lockers, Wi-Fi, smart parking, demand-responsive transport, smart bins or a working digital twin. SDP calls Elmina a "smart and safe city", but the evidence is mostly physical design ([Elmina launch, 2018][elmina-2018]).

## Who runs a township after handover

- SDP draws its reporting boundary by "level of ownership and management control" and leaves out premises where a tenant has full management control ([SR 2024][sr2024]). It keeps running its malls and leisure assets ([IAR 2025][iar2025]).
- For strata property, the developer manages common property until the joint management body (JMB) or management corporation takes over ([Seremban City Council JMB guide][jmb]).
- The city council maintains public roads, drains, traffic signs and street lights; for Elmina that is Shah Alam City Council, MBSA ([MBSA engineering department][mbsa], search excerpt only).
- **Our read:** a pilot is easiest where SDP still has control: its malls, Innovation Park, sales galleries, parks under SDP management, or homes before handover. Anything on public roads needs the council, and anything on strata common property needs the JMB or management corporation.

## Reporting pressure on SDP

- **IFRS S1 and S2 now apply.** SDP's market value was RM11.5 billion at the end of 2024 ([IAR 2024][iar2024]), above the RM2 billion line for Group 1 of the National Sustainability Reporting Framework, which starts in FY2025 ([SC media release][nsrf]). Group 1's wider Scope 3 disclosures start in FY2027 ([NSRF response paper][nsrf-paper]).
- **Assurance is coming.** Reasonable assurance of Scope 1 and 2 emissions starts for Group 1 for periods beginning 1 January 2028 ([SC, September 2026][assurance]).
- **Energy law:** the Energy Efficiency and Conservation Act 2024 covers sites using at least 21,600 GJ a year and office buildings of 8,000 m² or more. Details are in [Energy, HVAC and solar](/docs/research/landscape/energy-hvac-solar.md). **Our read:** SDP's malls and convention centre are likely candidates.
- **Mall recycling:** from June 2026, malls need recycling facilities to apply for or renew a council business licence, phased in ([Malay Mail][mall-recycling]). SDP runs three malls.
- **Green finance:** SDP's New Economy Venture set up a RM2.6 billion sukuk programme in July 2026, with a Gold-rated green finance framework covering a hyperscale data centre and a warehouse in City of Elmina ([sukuk release][sukuk]).

## What this means for an idea

Each point is our read of the evidence above.

- **The Energy & Carbon pillar is off track,** and grid electricity in malls and leisure assets drives it. An idea that measurably cuts that electricity, or the refrigerant losses, speaks to the most urgent number in SDP's own report.
- **SDP has no township-wide resident platform.** The SDP App ends at handover, and the Elmina Valley Community app covers only visitor registration, maintenance fees and residents' association notices in selected Elmina phases. Most post-handover resident experience is open ground, but an app alone risks the "existing app plus AI" label.
- **SDP measures beneficiaries and its customers' NPS, not resident outcomes.** A credible way to measure resident wellbeing or satisfaction would fill a gap SDP itself hasn't closed.
- **The Innovation Park and the CREAM pilot are ready-made pilot hooks** for the feasibility criterion.
- **Flood and heat risk are documented** at named townships (Bandar Bukit Raja, Bandar Universiti Pagoh), which gives a climate-resilience idea a real site.

## Unverified and not found

- CREAM uptake isn't published, and SDP gives no home count; the 450-home, 4.5 MWp figure is TNB's.
- The nine solar sites aren't all named; no SDP solar PPA or CRESS deal was found.
- No GBI, LEED or Green Mark rating was found for SDP, only GreenRE ratings.
- No public matrix assigns each township asset to SDP, the council, a residents' association or a JMB.
- No earlier SDP university hackathon was found; the 2022 Concept Home 2030 contest was for architects. See [Past winners](/docs/research/competition/past-winners.md).
