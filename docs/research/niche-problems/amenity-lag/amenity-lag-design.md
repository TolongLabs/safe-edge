## Summary

- **The public-data gap is specific.** MOE publishes district and state aggregates. CheckSekolah displays school-level records and coordinates from three MOE registries, but says bulk CSV/JSON downloads are no longer published. MyGDX lists KPM APIs, with access requiring registration and some APIs restricted. KKM’s facility master contains public-facility coordinates; its 5,160 records are dated 31 December 2025. [MOE school counts](https://data.gov.my/data-catalogue/schools_district) · [CheckSekolah data notes](https://checksekolah.my/data/) · [MyGDX listing](https://www.mygdx.gov.my/en/landing-page/listCatalog/5dc300f5-fe7c-4743-a82f-d430db57bc27?theme=first-theme) · [KKM facility register](https://github.com/MoH-Malaysia/data-resources-public/blob/main/README.md)
- **Useful pilot inputs exist, with limits.** Prasarana’s static GTFS files describe scheduled services, and its realtime documentation currently covers vehicle positions. DOSM’s district series runs through 2026, while NAPIC’s archive now includes H1 2026 reports. The SITS map’s indexed result shows route labels, but live vehicle positions and route-stop details could not be verified from the cited page. None of these sources joins housing-phase handovers to amenity opening dates. [GTFS static](https://developer.data.gov.my/realtime-api/gtfs-static) · [GTFS realtime](https://developer.data.gov.my/realtime-api/gtfs-realtime) · [DOSM population](https://data.gov.my/data-catalogue/population_district) · [NAPIC archive](https://napic.jpph.gov.my/en/archives/laporan-status-harta-tanah) · [SITS map](https://akses.selangor.gov.my/)
- **Rapid KL On-Demand is a possible interim service.** Prasarana’s February 2025 notice says T772B was to start on 17 February; its May 2025 notice says the Alam Budiman/Setia Alam–Setia City Mall zone was to start on 15 May with two vans. MOBI currently lists both areas, but a live booking should be checked in the app. A January 2026 Prasarana notice says the fixed fare became RM2 per trip on 1 February 2026, with stated concessions. The launch and fare PDFs were available only as indexed text in this check. [February notice, snippet-only](https://myrapid.com.my/wp-content/uploads/2025/02/RAPID-BUS-SIARAN-MEDIA-PERKHIDMATAN-RAPID-KL-ON-DEMAND-DIPERLUAS-DENGAN-PENGENALAN-ENAM-LALUAN-BAHARU.pdf) · [May notice, snippet-only](https://myrapid.com.my/wp-content/uploads/2025/05/RAPID-BUS-SIARAN-MEDIA-MOBILITI-KOMUNITI-DAN-PELAJAR-DIPERTINGKAT-DENGAN-RAPID-KL-ON-DEMAND.pdf) · [MOBI service page](https://mobi.badanbascoach.com.my/) · [Fare notice, snippet-only](https://myrapid.com.my/wp-content/uploads/2026/01/PRASARANA-SIARAN-MEDIA-ROD-CATAT-PERTUMBUHAN-POSITIF.pdf)
- **Facility timing is the hard part.** A March 2023 parliamentary answer described school delivery as taking more than five years and possibly up to ten in the context of a particular project. It is not a measured national average or a service standard. JPN’s stated functions include forecasting enrolment, classes and teachers and coordinating school projects. [Parliamentary Hansard, 6 March 2023](https://repositori.parlimen.gov.my/bitstream/123456789/3429/15/DR14-06032023.pdf) · [JPN Johor function chart](https://www.moe.gov.my/assets/images/CARTA%20FUNGSI%20JPN%20JOHOR.pdf)
- **Analyst's read:** A feasible hackathon product is a phase-level readiness and access monitor. It could connect real handover data with verified facility status, public-transport coverage, walking access, interim service plans and a named owner for each next action. Use SDP’s phase data and agency/operator confirmation. Do not infer a school opening from a nearby map point or fill missing demand with synthetic residents.

**School buses.** APAD says companies and individuals providing public transport, including school buses or vans, need an operator licence. It treats school bus as a distinct service class with an approved operating area. Confirm the required vehicle permits and driver requirements with APAD before using a developer-owned vehicle to carry pupils commercially.

I found no current standard public “cost per seat” schedule in the reviewed material. Bernama reported on 19 October 2024 that the Transport Ministry cited RM90 per month as the then-current school-van fee; that is an old reported figure, not a current Klang Valley quote. In an article dated 12 April 2026, NST reported parent-paid examples of RM180/month for a 6.5 km route and RM150/month for a 4.2 km route. These are reported fares from individual routes, not a tariff or operator cost benchmark. [Bernama, 19 October 2024](https://www.bernama.com/en/news.php?id=2353959) · [NST, 12 April 2026](https://www.nst.com.my/amp/news/nation/2026/04/1415600/diesel-price-surge-parents-fear-school-bus-fares-will-spike). **Analyst's read:** budget with written operator quotations that state monthly seat price, occupancy assumptions, route length, operating days, standby capacity, insurance and escalation terms.

**Developer-funded shuttles.** SDP’s 19 October 2017 Pagoh announcement described shuttle-bus services as part of the planned education hub, alongside pedestrian paths and bicycle lanes; it does not establish current operation or cost. Gamuda Gardens currently advertises a free shuttle network. Neither source gives a comparable cost per passenger or establishes that a shuttle substitutes for scheduled public transport. [SDP Pagoh announcement, 2017](https://www.simedarbyproperty.com/press-releases/pagoh-education-hub-welcomes-first-batch-5000-students) · [Gamuda Gardens township](https://gamudagardens.com.my/township). **Analyst's read:** test a time-limited, demand-counted shuttle contract against a school-term calendar, and publish occupancy and cost per passenger before deciding whether to continue.

**Smart Selangor route additions.** A Selangor DUN answer for the July 2025 sitting says the relevant PBT identifies need for a rural route and submits a proposed addition to the state Permanent Committee on Mobility for approval. A 2018 answer lists historical criteria such as filling service gaps, avoiding duplication and linking dense housing to public services. Confirm current scoring with the state/PBT. [Selangor DUN answer, July 2025](https://dewan.selangor.gov.my/question/bas-smart-selangor-47/) · [Selangor DUN answer, 2018](https://dewan.selangor.gov.my/question/perkhidmatan-bas-smart-selangor/). **Analyst's read:** a developer can give the PBT measured demand and access evidence, but cannot add a state route on its own.

## How openings are decided

### Schools

A March 2023 parliamentary answer describes a school needs assessment involving JPN/PPD, local authorities, existing nearby schools and capacity, population/catchment and planned development. A 2025 answer names similar coordination factors, but its PDF was available only as a search snippet in this check. JPN Johor’s function chart lists enrolment/class/teacher forecasting, school project planning and coordination of openings or relocations. [Parliamentary Hansard, 6 March 2023](https://repositori.parlimen.gov.my/bitstream/123456789/3429/15/DR14-06032023.pdf) · [2025 parliamentary answer, snippet-only](https://www.parlimen.gov.my/files/jindex/pdf/JDR14082025.pdf) · [JPN Johor function chart](https://www.moe.gov.my/assets/images/CARTA%20FUNGSI%20JPN%20JOHOR.pdf).

Once need is accepted, funding, land/site readiness and project delivery still follow. A JKR tender record for a Putrajaya school project gives a construction window from 28 January 2025 to 24 January 2028. That is one project’s contract period, not a national build duration. A Deputy Education Minister’s March 2023 statement said delivery can take more than five years and possibly up to ten in discussion of a particular project; it is not a measured average or formal SLA. [JKR tender record](https://tender.jkr.gov.my/kenyataan/tender/berjaya?BerjayaSearch%5Bnotender%5D=JKR%2FCKUB%2F22712%2F2024) · [Parliamentary Hansard, 6 March 2023](https://repositori.parlimen.gov.my/bitstream/123456789/3429/15/DR14-06032023.pdf). I did not identify an end-to-end clock from land reservation to first school day in the reviewed sources; the JKR period begins at construction.

**What a developer can do:** provide JPN/PPD and PBT with an accurate phase schedule, planned unit counts, handovers/occupancy when available, school-age demand evidence, land status and access/utilities early. Reserve and transfer a suitable site with clear title and infrastructure readiness. Ask KPM/JPN for written milestones and interim options. These are analyst recommendations based on the cited planning functions, not a guarantee of approval or opening by a developer’s date. A March 2026 parliamentary answer describes modular classrooms and other measures as interim responses to overcrowding. **Snippet-only:** the cited answer PDF could not be opened. [Parliamentary answer, 12 March 2026](https://parlimen.gov.my/files/jindex/pdf/JBLDN12032026.pdf)

### Clinics

KKM’s Health Facility Planning Section describes assessing service requirements and workload, preparing medical briefs and checking facility planning/design standards. [KKM Health Facility Planning Section](https://hq.moh.gov.my/perancangan/health-facility-planning-section/). KKM’s clinic-classification page, last updated 25 October 2019, lists workload and approximate catchment bands: KK3 at 300–500 daily visits and population above 30,000 to 50,000; KK4 at 150–300 visits and population above 20,000 to 30,000. These are classifications on that page, not a universal entitlement to a clinic per number of residents or a current opening rule. [KKM clinic classification](https://hq.moh.gov.my/bpkk/index.php/klasifikasi-klinik-kesihatan.html).

Land readiness can delay a specific clinic. A Selangor answer dated 15 November 2024 said Klinik Kesihatan Subang Bestari was still at the land-handover stage and the district health office was checking the land status; state health officials would review the site before any land swap. A November 2025 answer says the existing Klinik Kesihatan Paya Jaras site is 0.2 acres and had averaged 183 daily visits in 2024. Selangor was seeking a 4–5-acre site in Paya Jaras for a new KK3 clinic. [Subang Bestari answer, 2024](https://dewan.selangor.gov.my/question/tapak-klinik-kesihatan-subang-bestari-dun-kota-damansara/) · [Paya Jaras answer, 2025](https://dewan.selangor.gov.my/question/isu-kesesakan-keselamatan-dan-keperluan-tapak-baharu-bagi-klinik-kesihatan-paya-jaras/).

**What a developer can do:** offer a properly located, serviced site early; resolve title, zoning, access and utilities; share an evidence-backed occupancy and travel-demand forecast with KKM/JKN; and ask whether the authority would consider an interim approved premises or outreach service. **Analyst's read:** site readiness can remove a known bottleneck, but staffing, procurement and KKM budget decisions remain public-sector decisions. I did not find a published national clinic-opening timeline in the reviewed material.

## Existing products and projects

- **UK Section 106 infrastructure reporting:** English local authorities publish annual Infrastructure Funding Statements on planning obligations and contributions, including education, health and transport. Government guidance published 19 May 2016 and updated 1 September 2019 describes the reporting framework. It can track obligation details, triggers, contributions and spending; it is not a real-time tracker of whether a facility is open for residents. [UK government guidance](https://www.gov.uk/guidance/planning-obligations).
- **Singapore HDB town planning:** HDB describes integrated town planning for schools, shops, parks, community facilities and transport. This is a planning precedent, not a public phase-by-phase readiness dashboard. [HDB planning and design](https://www.hdb.gov.sg/about-us/our-role/plan-and-design-towns).
- **Malaysian adjacent systems:** PLANMalaysia’s S-CHARMs maps village facilities and profiles; its public map service includes education, health, transit and village layers. It is not a general urban township phase tracker. KPKT’s TEDUH provides project/phase progress and, for covered projects, CCC and vacant-possession dates; its status pages reported HIMS data through 13 October 2026. These help with existing facilities and some housing delivery, but do not show actual occupancy or link a housing phase to nearby amenity opening dates. [S-CHARMs map service](https://scharms.planmalaysia.gov.my/arcgis/rest/services/SCHARMS/Mobil_Data_Kampung_Kemudahan_1/MapServer) · [TEDUH open data](https://teduh.kpkt.gov.my/data-terbuka) · [TEDUH project status example](https://teduh.kpkt.gov.my/semakan-status-kemajuan?kodProjek=3981-42).
- **Student transport projects:** UKM’s 2025 technical report describes MySchoolCommute for school-student transport management. UTeM’s 2021 project is about campus-bus management, not school buses. The 2023 Rapid Bus x UTM FAQ documents a higher-education category and mobility challenges; UTeM’s winner listing names “Data Analytics on Service Efficiency in Rapid Bus.” These sources do not establish that no Malaysian student project has addressed school commute or amenity readiness. [UKM report](https://ftsm.ukm.my/v6/public/assets/file/technicalreport/A177335_TReport.pdf) · [UTeM project, 2021](https://digitalcollection.utem.edu.my/27271/) · [Rapid Bus x UTM Hackathon FAQ, 2023](https://myrapid.com.my/wp-content/uploads/2023/09/MyRapid-Hack-FAQ-1.pdf) · [UTeM winner listing](https://ftmk.utem.edu.my/web/index.php/ptta-portfolio/data-hackhaton-2023-sekalung-tahniah/).
- **Analyst's read:** SDP could differentiate a prototype with an “amenity lag ledger” for each real phase: handover and occupied-unit evidence, facility status and responsible agency, walking/transit access, interim transport and a dated action owner. It should show uncertainty and let agencies/operators correct their records. This is a proposed product direction, not an existing product found in the review.

## Worked example

**City of Elmina, Shah Alam — open-data screen.** This is a proximity screen, not an official service-access audit.

**Method.** I used the mapped “Elmina City Centre” feature as a centre proxy at approximately 3.18298° N, 101.50565° E. GeoView’s indexed page describes it as an OSM brownfield feature last edited 8 September 2024; **snippet-only** because that page did not load in this check. ## Gaps

- **No ungated official school point-plus-enrolment download was identified in the reviewed sources.** MOE’s public district tables cannot establish the closest school or its remaining capacity for an SDP phase. CheckSekolah shows school-level records and coordinates but says bulk downloads are no longer published; MyGDX lists KPM APIs, with registration and restrictions. This is a gap in open access, not evidence that the underlying data does not exist. [MOE catalogue](https://data.gov.my/data-catalogue?source=MOE) · [MyGDX listing](https://www.mygdx.gov.my/en/landing-page/listCatalog/5dc300f5-fe7c-4743-a82f-d430db57bc27?theme=first-theme) · [CheckSekolah notes](https://checksekolah.my/data/).
- **I did not find a public national clinic pipeline or opening timeline in the reviewed KKM sources.** The facility master gives facility points; KKM’s planning page describes planning functions, while the Selangor cases show specific site issues. They do not provide a national register of planned clinics with land, budget, construction and opening milestones. [KKM facility resources](https://github.com/MoH-Malaysia/data-resources-public/blob/main/README.md) · [KKM planning section](https://hq.moh.gov.my/perancangan/health-facility-planning-section/).
- **Housing sources do not reach general township-phase occupancy.** NAPIC reports completions and stock at broader geographies. TEDUH reports progress and CCC/VP dates for covered projects, but the reviewed sources do not connect actual occupied homes in a general township phase to nearby school, clinic and transit readiness. [NAPIC archive](https://napic.jpph.gov.my/en/archives/laporan-status-harta-tanah) · [TEDUH open data](https://teduh.kpkt.gov.my/data-terbuka).
- **Transport records need local verification.** GTFS and SITS can help identify scheduled-route leads. The current on-demand booking area should be checked in the operator app, and school-bus seat prices need written operator quotes. A route near a township boundary does not prove safe access from each phase.
- **The reviewed sources did not show a Malaysian amenity-readiness tracker linking phase handovers to school/clinic opening dates, or a national school/clinic opening-time standard.** This is a finding from the sources listed here, not a claim that no such work exists anywhere. **Analyst's read:** a pilot should make these gaps visible rather than filling them with synthetic demand or assumed opening dates.
