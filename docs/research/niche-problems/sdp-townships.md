# Sime Darby Property townships

Snapshot date: 16 October 2026. SDP’s IAR2025 reports 27 active townships and developments but does not publish a complete named roster; the active list below reconstructs that count from its 2025 portfolio and earlier portfolio disclosures. The older table repeats mature places that still appear in SDP’s current portfolio. “New” means the township or park launch was in 2018 or later. “not found” means no reviewed source stated the fact. ## Gaps

- IAR2025 gives the count of 27 but no complete 27-name roster. SDP’s earlier report also groups townships, precincts, signature developments and JVs differently; membership of this reconstructed list is uncertain. [1][3]
- Acreage definitions conflict or appear to have changed: City of Elmina is 5,000 acres at 2018 launch versus 6,500 in 2025; Bandar Bukit Raja is 4,333, 5,240 or 5,333 across SDP and news sources; Serenia City is 1,775 at launch versus 2,370 later; Putra Heights is 1,700–1,796; Planters’ Haven is 250–270. [4][6][7][10][12][15][16][17][18][34][43]
- Population figures are sparse and often dated. The table keeps SDP’s historical forecasts separate from resident counts and does not treat nearby catchment or student numbers as residents.
- SR2025 names present-day acute flooding risk at Bandar Bukit Raja and Bandar Universiti Pagoh. It describes future extreme precipitation, drought and heatwaves/heat stress as scenario-dependent hazards but does not assign them to individual townships. [2]
- Malaysian Gazette reported traffic and road-capacity concerns around City of Elmina; this is external reporting, so it is not placed in “Named risks.” SDP’s 2026 Kulai announcement targets a first phase in 2028, while SDP’s current site lists Bandar Suasana Harvard–Gurun in Kedah; these are outside the IAR2025 three-region count or not yet launched, so they are not added to the 27. [47][48][49][50]
