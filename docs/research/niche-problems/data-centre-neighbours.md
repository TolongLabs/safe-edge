# Living next to a data centre

Evidence pack on living next to a hyperscale data centre, researched and then fact-checked by two separate Codex runs on 16 October 2026 for [Niche township problems](/docs/research/niche-problems.md). The checker tested 57 claims: 30 confirmed, 10 corrected, 15 removed and 2 it could only see in search snippets. Lines marked "Analyst's read" are the worker's inference, not findings; scores are the worker's, before our own ranking.

## One-line problem

- **Analyst's read:** Residents need a trusted way to document construction and operating impacts and get a response. Johor reporting gives examples of construction complaints; this review did not locate public, site-level impact measurements for Elmina. ([The Straits Times, published 10 Mar and updated 11 Mar 2026][r1]; [Media Selangor, 20 Aug 2026](https://mediaselangor.com/en/2026/08/390529))

[r1]: https://www.straitstimes.com/asia/se-asia/whatll-become-of-our-village-johoreans-choke-on-dust-noise-as-data-centres-move-in-next-door

## How big and how bad

- Johor residents told _The Straits Times_ that dust entered homes, construction noise disrupted nights, children had coughs or skin allergies, and household water pressure dropped. These are residents' reports, not exposure measurements or proof of medical causation. The paper reported that local authorities issued ZData a **14-day** stop-work order in **August 2025** for breaches of construction protocols. ([The Straits Times, 10 Mar 2026][r1])
- At Gelang Patah, residents cited earthwork dust and alleged that work continued until **10pm**. They said one free car wash was insufficient and requested help with laundry, medical expenses, and higher utility bills from extra cleaning. ([EdgeProp, 16 Feb 2026][r2])
- In Kota Damansara, MBPJ reported about **290** formal objections as of **21 July 2026**. A residents' coalition said it represented **over 500 households**. Concerns included possible 24-hour operations, noise, heat, energy use, and traffic impacts. These were objections to a proposal, not measurements of operating impacts. The consultant's withdrawal letter was received on **28 July 2026** and, according to MBPJ, sought to reassess site feasibility and development components. The cited articles do not establish what role residents' objections played in the withdrawal. ([The Star, 27 Jul 2026][r3]; [The Star, 30 Jul 2026][r4])
- **Analyst's read:** This review found no public, site-level Elmina readings for noise, dust, heat, water pressure, or power reliability, and no reliable national count of affected households or nuisance frequency. The search was not exhaustive.

## Trend and triggers

- Johor had **51 approved projects worth RM182.96 billion** as of **November 2025**. Selangor reported **39 approvals** between **2021 and 2025**. ([The Straits Times, 10 Mar 2026][r1]; [Bernama, 6 Aug 2026](https://bernama.com/en/news.php?id=2591012))
- _The Sun_ described a Johor gathering in **February 2026** as Malaysia's “first demonstration of its kind” over data-centre construction. That is the newspaper's description, not a government count of past protests. ([The Sun, 12 Feb 2026][r5])
- SPAN's Water Supply Guidelines for Data Centre Development took effect **1 September 2025**. Separately, Selangor's data-centre water tariff is **RM5.31/m³** from that date. The state said developers must provide new water infrastructure and storage for **three days** of demand. **Analyst's read:** A tariff category and storage requirement do not by themselves show that households have experienced shortages. ([Bernama, 8 Dec 2025][r6]; [Air Selangor, 1 Aug 2025][r7]; [Bernama, 6 Aug 2026](https://bernama.com/en/news.php?id=2591012))
- PLANMalaysia published Circular 4/2024 on **18 October 2024**. It tells agencies to adopt a common planning reference for data-centre projects above **1 MVA**. MBPJ said the 2024 guideline calls for a **50 m** buffer from residential boundaries at Kota Damansara. **Analyst's read:** The circular establishes a planning reference; it is guidance rather than a standalone Act. ([PLANMalaysia, Circular 4/2024][r8]; [The Star, 27 Jul 2026][r3])
- **Analyst's read:** I found no public seasonal series measuring data-centre-related impacts in Malaysia.

## Who suffers and who owns the fix

- The cited reports document residents' complaints and objections, including concerns about children, sleep, dust, water pressure, heat, and traffic. They do not measure worker or road-user exposure. **Analyst's read:** Residents' groups and JMBs can help collect and escalate complaints, but the sources do not give them regulatory powers.
- MBPJ stated it has statutory authority to approve or reject the Kota Damansara planning application under Act 172. DOE's EIA FAQ says an EIA is required when a proposed project is listed in the Environmental Quality (Prescribed Activities) (EIA) Order 2015. DOE also publishes environmental-noise guidance. ([The Star, 27 Jul 2026][r3]; [DOE EIA FAQ][r9]; [DOE noise-guidance record](https://edl.doe.gov.my/overview/41445))
- Sime Darby Property's public role is developer and lessor under build-and-lease agreements; named construction contractors deliver the projects. Media Selangor described the first Elmina facility as Google's and reported that Google expected to start operations within nine months of **20 August 2026**. **Analyst's read:** These contracts give SDP and contractors potential levers over construction practice, but I found no disclosed lease-level neighbour-response duty. ([SDP release, 2 Dec 2024][r10]; [SDP IAR 2025][r11]; [The Edge, 1 Apr 2026](https://theedgemalaysia.com/node/798211); [Media Selangor, 20 Aug 2026](https://mediaselangor.com/en/2026/08/390529))

## SDP's exposure and lever

- SDP's **3 March 2025** results briefing lists **two** hyperscale data centres at Elmina Business Park spanning **126 acres**, with a total lease value of **RM7.6 billion over 20 years**. It does not call this amount rental revenue or identify the two leases as Google leases. ([SDP Q4 FY2024 briefing][r12])
- Media Selangor reported on **20 August 2026** that Google's Elmina facility was under construction and expected to begin operations within **nine months**; no exact operating date was set. I found no public handover notice. The second data-centre construction contract covers about **77 acres**, with completion targeted for **Q3 2027**. ([Media Selangor, 20 Aug 2026](https://mediaselangor.com/en/2026/08/390529); [The Edge, 1 Apr 2026](https://theedgemalaysia.com/node/798211))
- A Google representative told Media Selangor that the company conducts regular noise testing and that noise typically needs to fall within **50–65 decibels**. The article also describes inward-facing generators, setbacks, and noise-dampening fans. **Analyst's read:** The article provides no Elmina test record or site reading. ([Media Selangor, 20 Aug 2026](https://mediaselangor.com/en/2026/08/390529))
- SDP's IAR 2025 lists four sustainability pillars: Community Experience, Energy and Carbon, Urban Biodiversity, and Climate Resilience. **Analyst's read:** A search of the report did not locate Elmina-specific neighbour monitoring. ([SDP IAR 2025][r11])

## What already exists

- Malaysia has planning guidance, DOE environmental-noise guidance, and SPAN water-supply guidance. At Gelang Patah, Tropicana Firstwide described water spraying and netting and committed to hydro-seeding and **10,000 m²** of added green netting. ZData said its facility uses **100% reclaimed water**; that is the company's claim, not independent verification. ([EdgeProp, 16 Feb 2026][r2]; [DOE noise-guidance record](https://edl.doe.gov.my/overview/41445); [SPAN guidelines, via Bernama][r6])
- The UK Environment Agency's permit guidance, updated **12 May 2026**, covers engaging neighbours, complaint response and records, off-site noise monitoring, and possible trigger values for further monitoring or remedial action. It calls for background sound assessment; it does not use the word “baseline” on the page. ([UK Environment Agency][r13])
- In Tallaght, Ireland, a district heating network uses waste heat from an Amazon data centre. It supplied public buildings from **April 2023** and was extended in **2024** to include **133** cost-rental apartments. ([South Dublin County Council, Climate Action Plan Annual Report 2024, published 2025][r14])
- Meta says its community-grant programmes fund local projects in places where it operates data centres, including the United States, Canada, Ireland, Denmark, Sweden, and Singapore. ([Meta Data Centers, Community Grants][r15])
- St Louis's **21 April 2026** conditional-use permit conditions require annual reporting on waste heat rejected and recovered or reused. The city also negotiated a community-benefits agreement. Aurora's 2026 rules require public hearings and sound studies for new data centres, and annual energy, water, and noise reporting or testing for new and existing centres. These are not Malaysian rules. ([City of St. Louis, 21 Apr 2026][r16]; [City of Aurora, regulations approved 25 Mar 2026][r17])
- Two recent US examples are still plans for future monitoring. Oracle announced on **8 September 2026** that Project Jupiter will publish onsite noise, water, heat, and emissions data, with twice-yearly independent assessments after the data centres are operational. The University of Colorado Colorado Springs describes its DC-IRE Lab as an upcoming community monitoring project; its schedule calls for pre-operation baseline data and a public dashboard in **Spring 2027**. ([Oracle, 8 Sep 2026][r18]; [UCCS DC-IRE Lab][r19])
- **Gap — Analyst's read:** This review found no public Elmina neighbourhood baseline, independent impact series, or disclosed complaint-response log. Foreign precedents do not establish a Malaysian monitoring or remedy route. Measurement without a route to remedy would not resolve dust or noise complaints.

## Hackathon and startup prior

- **Analyst's read:** Across the listed sources and three supplemental searches, I found no **2022–2026** hackathon winner focused specifically on data-centre neighbour impacts. This is a search result, not proof that none exists. UMPSA reported a Google Data Centre Hackathon champion on **17 September 2026**; the event covered server setup, networking, and a Capture the Flag cybersecurity challenge. ([UMPSA, 17 Sep 2026][r20])
- WFEO's hackathon concluded on **4 March 2026**. Its finalist list includes “GreenCore Sustainable Data Center” from Shanghai Jiao Tong University, but the page does not describe the entry's design. ([WFEO, 5 Mar 2026][r21])
- Raspberry Shake markets sensors for noise and vibration monitoring. Its **25 May 2026** page says no peer-reviewed studies specific to data-centre monitoring with Raspberry Shake hardware had yet been published; that is a vendor statement, not an efficacy review. ([Raspberry Shake, 25 May 2026][r22])
- Civitar describes a US product that assembles cited public records into data-centre site briefings and change alerts. It is a public-record service, not a resident sensor network. ([Civitar, undated page checked 16 Oct 2026](https://www.civitar.org/))

## Real data for a prototype

- **DOE APIMS:** [public dashboard](https://eqms.doe.gov.my/apims/main), with national station readings. The interactive dashboard content was available only as a search-result extract in this check; its live interface and any public API were not confirmed.
- **Monthly Air Pollution:** [catalogue][r23]; [CSV link][r24]. Catalogue data is through **31 December 2024** and covers average monthly pollutant concentrations tabulated from hourly observations at monitoring stations. The catalogue schema does not expose station-level fields. Licence: CC BY 4.0. The catalogue opened; direct CSV download was not confirmed.
- **Water Consumption by State and Sector:** [catalogue][r25]; [CSV link][r26]. Annual state totals are broken down into domestic and non-domestic sectors through **31 December 2024**. Licence: CC BY 4.0. The catalogue opened; direct CSV download was not confirmed. **Analyst's read:** State-level annual totals are too coarse to measure Elmina impacts.
- **MET Malaysia weather API:** [documentation][r27]; [forecast endpoint](https://api.data.gov.my/weather/forecast). Docs describe daily updated seven-day forecasts and filters for towns and districts. Documentation opened; a live endpoint query was not confirmed. This is forecast data, not historical site data.
- **Analyst's read:** I found no open Elmina feed for noise, dust, water, grid, traffic, or complaints. Site evidence would need calibrated fieldwork and agreed access and publication terms.

## Pilot sketch

- **Analyst's read / proposal:** At City of Elmina, map nearby homes, schools, and roads with residents and the council. The second facility is a construction case. If the first facility enters commissioning and operation as expected, it could become an operating case. ([The Edge, 1 Apr 2026](https://theedgemalaysia.com/node/798211); [Media Selangor, 20 Aug 2026](https://mediaselangor.com/en/2026/08/390529))
- **Proposed measures:** Record baseline and repeat noise, PM2.5/PM10, weather, heavy-vehicle movements, and resident reports during construction and early operation. Compare with a nearby location and log responses.
- **Analyst's read:** Cost drivers include an acoustic consultant and meters, particulate sensors and calibration, power and data, field staff, traffic counts, and meetings. I found no local quotes or credible total budget.

## Kill risks

- **Analyst's read:** SDP or Google may withhold schedules or site data. Low-cost sensors may not establish compliance or attribute readings without calibrated methods and site access. Health claims need clinical evidence.
- **Analyst's read:** The current reports focus on construction. If works end or operations create little nuisance, demand may be weak. Water and energy attribution needs operator or utility data.
- **Analyst's read:** Monitoring without a remedy may look performative. SDP may prefer contractor controls and direct engagement over a paid service.

## Scores

- **Analyst's read:** Pain — **4/5**: Johor residents report dust, sleep, and water-pressure concerns; Elmina severity is unknown. ([The Straits Times, 10 Mar 2026][r1])
- **Analyst's read:** SDP lever — **4/5**: SDP has development and lease roles at Elmina; Google was preparing to operate the first facility. ([SDP, 2 Dec 2024][r10]; [Media Selangor, 20 Aug 2026](https://mediaselangor.com/en/2026/08/390529))
- **Analyst's read:** Evidence available — **3/5**: reports and rules exist, but this review found no Elmina baseline. ([The Star, 27 Jul 2026][r3])
- **Analyst's read:** Market density — **3/5**: overseas public-record services and monitoring hardware exist; the Malaysian neighbour-response use case remains unproven. ([Civitar](https://www.civitar.org/); [Raspberry Shake][r22])
- **Analyst's read:** Hackathon prior — **1/5**: no directly focused winner was identified in the searches; adjacent operations and sustainability entries exist. ([UMPSA, 17 Sep 2026][r20]; [WFEO, 5 Mar 2026][r21])
