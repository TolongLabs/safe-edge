# Lightning in township open spaces

Evidence pack on lightning in township open spaces, researched and then fact-checked by two separate Codex runs on 16 October 2026 for [Niche township problems](/docs/research/niche-problems.md). The checker tested 85 claims: 36 confirmed, 46 corrected, 1 removed and 2 it could only see in search snippets. Lines marked "Analyst's read" are the worker's inference, not findings; scores are the worker's, before our own ranking.

## One-line problem

- Analyst's read: People using parks, tracks, fields, golf courses and worksites may remain exposed as storms approach. A general warning does not establish that users hear a stop cue or can reach shelter. METMalaysia says most lightning deaths and injuries occur outside buildings; lightning can also strike beyond heavy rain. [1]

## How big and how bad

- A 2018 study of Malaysia for 2008–2017 reported 12.5 recorded deaths per year and estimated 20.9 deaths per year with a model. The authors discuss under-reporting and model limitations. These are historical study figures, not a current official rate. [2]
- SNIPPET-ONLY: the cited UPM/CELP presentation could not be opened. Its indexed text shows a total of 235 victims (deaths and injuries combined) through September 2015. [3]
- SNIPPET-ONLY: the cited 2016 case-study page exposed its title but not the abstract in the publisher view. Search-indexed abstracts say it covered January 2008–July 2015 and describe cases in Melaka and Selangor, mostly involving outdoor or sports activity. [4]
- Analyst's read: a reliable recent breakdown by age, sex, job and site was not located in the sources checked. The older 2008 review's 100–150 annual-death estimate is attributed to an unnamed Malaysian lightning researcher in a personal communication, not an audited national count. [5]
- Setia Alam, 10 March 2019: one death was reported. Injury totals conflict: FMT reported three injured; Malay Mail reported four. [9][10]
- Alor Gajah: R. Thanesh, 28, died after a suspected lightning strike during a match on 5 July 2026; the match referee was injured and another person was unhurt. Bernama published its report on 6 July. [11][38]

## Trend and triggers

- Analyst's read: the searches did not establish a rising casualty trend or a continuous, geocoded site-level series. The available historical totals are incomplete.
- METMalaysia says thunderstorms occur year-round, more often in April–May and October–November. Land storms usually occur in the evening or at dusk. Lightning can strike as far as 10 miles from the heavy-rain area. [1]
- DOSM's 2025 Compendium reports 312 lightning days at KLIA, Sepang, in 2024, compared with 317 in 2023. These are station days, not flash counts at a township or casualty totals. [8]
- Analyst's read: “Third-highest worldwide” is not established as a country strike ranking by the cited sources. StarProperty describes Subang as having a high number of incidences and quotes a researcher saying, “For lightning impact, we are number one.” A 2021 satellite study ranks Kuala Lumpur first among national capitals for its lightning measures. Those are different places and measures. [6][7]
- Analyst's read: the searches did not locate a lightning-specific law enacted in 2025–2026. The amended Occupational Safety and Health Act took effect on 1 June 2024. A draft Malaysian adoption of IEC 62305 Parts 1–4 had a public-comment period from 15 February to 16 April 2026; the cited notice does not establish whether it was adopted later. [16][35]

## Who suffers and who owns the fix

- Players, joggers, golfers, children, event visitors and outdoor workers can be exposed. Analyst's read: the sources checked did not provide a recent demographic breakdown. [1][4]
- The OSH Act sets duties for employers and self-employed people toward others, occupiers of workplaces toward non-employees, and principals toward contractors and people working under their direction. It also requires risk assessment. Analyst's read: application to a specific park visitor depends on the circumstances and workplace/control relationship; the cited provisions do not settle every public park case. [16]
- Section 101 of the Local Government Act empowers local authorities to construct, maintain, supervise and control public parks, recreation grounds, playing fields and open spaces. The Strata Management Act assigns JMBs and management corporations duties and powers to maintain and manage common property. [17][18]
- Analyst's read: SDP, councils, event operators and contractors may each have operational roles depending on ownership, handover, contracts and who controls the activity or site. [16][17][18]

## SDP's exposure and lever

- SDP's Integrated Annual Report 2025 says the group has 27 active townships and developments, including Elmina, Serenia City and Bandar Bukit Raja. The report was filed on 20 April 2026 and covers FY2025. [31][39]
- SDP says its leisure segment owns and operates golf and other sporting facilities, including KLGCC and facilities in Kajang and Klang. The City of Elmina page advertises a 300-acre Central Park and 90 km of cycling and jogging tracks. [31][33]
- SDP's Sustainability Strategy and Roadmap 2030 includes Climate Resilience and Community Experience. The IAR describes a physical climate-risk assessment of several major townships during FY2025. Analyst's read: a text search of the accessible IAR found no lightning-specific programme. The cited Sustainability Report PDF did not open, so no claim is made about its full text. [31][32]

## What already exists

- METMalaysia issues thunderstorm warnings when observed or expected rain intensity exceeds 20 mm per hour; a single warning lasts no more than six hours. myCuaca distributes weather warnings. Malaysia's Open API provides forecasts and live warning text; its forecast locations include states, recreation centres, districts, towns and divisions. The warning endpoint updates when required. Analyst's read: the API documentation does not describe a park-level lightning sensor feed. [12][13][14]
- TNB says TNB Research developed a real-time Lightning Detection System for grid protection, with sensors at Alor Setar, Gerik, Kapar, Kulai and Paka. TNB's 11 July 2025 LinkedIn post claims each sensor detects lightning within a 323 km radius. Analyst's read: the post does not provide a public site-operator feed. [21]
- MS IEC 62793:2020, “Thunderstorm warning systems – Protection against lightning,” was published by Standards Malaysia in 2024. Analyst's read: its existence does not by itself prove that all parks must install alarms. The Energy Commission FAQ says building lightning-protection installation is determined by a risk assessment under the cited MS IEC 62305:2007. [19][20]
- Royal Selangor Golf Club's 2026 calendar is accessible in full. It describes a detector at the front-office counter that sounds after a strike within five miles (about 9 km): one continuous one-minute siren to stop play, and two 20-second notes to resume after lightning activity has ceased or abated. The rules also cover other outdoor club activities. KLGCC's booking terms allow closure for lightning but do not state that it has a detector. [22][23]
- Riajati advertises a TOA network with about 20 sensors across Malaysia, TCP/IP live data and historical records. It claims detection efficiency above 95%, location accuracy under 250 m and uptime above 99.99%; these are supplier claims, not independent test results. Analyst's read: public prices and a township-park deployment were not located in the pages checked. [24]
- Datanian advertises Voltraxx as a subscription-based lightning alert service using its Malaysian stations. Its page claims 250 m accuracy within 30 km; this is a supplier claim. [25]
- Additional supplier prior: Riajati says its Biral BTD-200 system is deployed at stadiums, golf courses, airports and schools in Malaysia, Singapore and Indonesia. It lists a 35 km detection range and optional sirens or SMS/email alerts. Treat deployment and performance statements as supplier claims. [36]
- Additional local installation evidence: Omega Trends lists an Early Warning Lightning Detection System at Tenby Schools Setia Eco Park, with a 120 dB siren. The company page gives no installation date, and this is the installer’s claim. [37]
- US National Weather Service sports guidance recommends a designated weather monitor, safe shelter and waiting at least 30 minutes after the last thunder before resuming activity. This is US guidance, not Malaysian law. [26]
- Blitzortung offers public real-time maps. Its terms prohibit commercial use of its data and say raw data are not intended for public release; participant access to calculated/raw data is described separately. [27]
- Analyst's read: public evidence checked here shows venue protocols and supplier systems, but does not establish consistent alert reach, shelter access, stop/resume rules or incident logs across SDP's public parks and tracks. Deployment at SDP public spaces remains unverified.

## Hackathon and startup prior

- The official MYHackathon page lists nine 2022 winners. MOSTI's 2024 press releases list seven Cohort 1 winners and five Cohort 2 winners. Analyst's read: none of the listed winners is clearly a lightning-safety project; this bounded search does not prove that no similar pitch or unpublished project existed. [28][29][30]
- Startup prior is not empty. Datanian advertises Voltraxx subscriptions; Riajati advertises TOA and Biral warning systems and reports venue deployments; Omega Trends lists a school installation. Analyst's read: detection alone is not a novel pitch. A stronger gap to test is local operating practice, coverage and whether warnings reach users and direct them to safe shelter. [24][25][36][37]

## Real data for a prototype

- **METMalaysia Open API:** [docs][r1]; forecasts include district and recreation-centre locations; a separate endpoint returns warning text. Forecasts update daily and warnings update when required. Analyst's read: no reuse licence was found in the API documentation checked. The live endpoint returned JSON on 16 October 2026; it is a weather-warning feed, not a lightning feed. [14][15]
- **DOSM Compendium of Environment Statistics 2025:** [PDF][r2]; published in December 2025 and includes station statistics through 2024. The KLIA series counts lightning days, not site flash density or casualties. DOSM says reproduction or distribution requires prior written permission and that reproduced/adapted content should cite DOSM. [8]
- **NASA LIS/OTD Reprocessed Flash Climatology:** [catalogue][r3]; NASA says it covers observations from April 1995 to November 2023 and provides near-global annual mean flash-rate grids at 0.1°. The catalogue lists public access and a US Government Works licence and was updated 29 September 2026. Catalogue checked; binary download not tested. It is historical context, not a live site-risk feed. [34]
- **Commercial feed:** Riajati advertises real-time TCP/IP data and searchable historical records by enquiry. Prices and terms require a quote and were not tested. TNB's post describes an internal grid-monitoring system but gives no public-data access instructions. [21][24]

## Pilot sketch

- Analyst's read: Elmina Central Park is a candidate for one managed field or track node. Confirm the landowner, maintenance lead, shelters and routes before selecting a site. SDP advertises a 300-acre park and 90 km of tracks. [33]
- Analyst's read: log the warning source, decision-maker, closure time, user exposure, alert audibility, time to shelter, missed closures, near misses and injuries. Compare venue logs with MET timestamps. Do not use rare injuries as the only short-pilot metric. [8][14]
- Analyst's read: cost drivers include a detector or licensed feed, audible and visual alerts, power/connectivity, signs, accessible shelter, installation, approvals, training, maintenance and insurance. Vendor prices were not published in the pages checked; obtain quotes before setting a budget. [24][25][36]

## Kill risks

- Analyst's read: a short pilot may see no strike or injury; historical counts are incomplete and not geocoded to sites. [2][8]
- Analyst's read: a warning without user reach or safe shelter could create false confidence. The team would need to identify closure authority and responsibility after handover.
- METMalaysia's warning trigger is rainfall-based, while lightning can occur beyond heavy rain. Commercial feeds add supplier terms and cost. [1][12][24]
- Analyst's read: RSGC, the school installation and commercial suppliers show that detection alone may look incremental. SDP's reports discuss climate resilience, but do not show whether lightning ranks among its priorities. [22][31][36][37]

## Scores

- **Analyst's read — Pain: 4/5** — potential for fatal outdoor exposure; casualty series are incomplete. [2]
- **Analyst's read — SDP lever: 4/5** — SDP operates golf and sports assets and advertises Elmina open space; ownership varies by site. [31][33]
- **Analyst's read — Evidence available: 3/5** — station data and public warnings exist; site-level casualty and alert-performance data are weak. [8][14]
- **Analyst's read — Market density: 4/5** — local suppliers and venue systems exist; township-wide public-space coverage was not established. [22][24][25][36]
- **Analyst's read — Hackathon prior: 1/5** — no clearly matching project appeared in the winner lists checked; commercial prior exists. [28][29][30]
