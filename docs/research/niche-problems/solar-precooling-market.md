Deep-dive evidence on how many homes in SDP townships could use solar pre-cooling, researched and then fact-checked by two separate Codex runs on 16 October 2026 for [Niche township problems](/docs/research/niche-problems.md). The checker tested 39 claims: 10 confirmed, 12 corrected, 1 removed and 16 it could only see in search snippets. Lines marked "Analyst's read" are the worker's inference, not findings.

## Summary

- SEDA’s 2024 Annual Report search text lists 3,064 residential NEM 2.0 projects approved (24.27 MW) and 3,053 operational (24.19 MW) at 31 December 2024. It lists 55,667 NEM Rakyat applications/projects approved (428.86 MW) and 45,161 operational projects (346.46 MW). The PDF could not be opened directly, so these report figures are **SNIPPET-ONLY**. They are project/application counts, not verified counts of unique occupied homes.
- A SEDA applicant-login search snippet retrieved on 16 October 2026 showed 683.9387 MWac approved for NEM Rakyat. A separate SEDA quota-dashboard snippet showed 683.9760 MWac. The pages could not be opened and the values conflict, so neither is a confirmed live balance. The official programme page describes a 700 MW NEM Rakyat quota for 1 February 2021–30 June 2025.
- The Star reported that, as of 30 April 2026, installed capacity under government solar schemes included 2,903 MW under NEM and 6.61 MW under Solar ATAP, across customer categories. It gave no ATAP residential count.
- SDP directly documents Elmina Ridge 1 as a 228-home solar-and-battery development with 1 MWp across the homes. Accessible SDP sources also confirm residential solar initiatives at Ilham Residence and City of Elmina, but they do not establish a current count of occupied, recruitable homes. TNB’s Ilham counts and the CREAM 450-home figure are **SNIPPET-ONLY** and are not used as a pilot denominator.
- DOSM’s 2024 table search text reports air-conditioner ownership in 68.8% of Malaysian households, 80.8% in Selangor and 73.5% in Negeri Sembilan. The detailed PDF could not be opened, so the rates are **SNIPPET-ONLY**. Household ownership is not the number of air-conditioners per home.
- Analyst’s read: Use an occupied, eligible cohort only after confirming delivery, working equipment, data access and consent. The draft’s estimate of 415 AC-owning Ilham homes is an extrapolation from two snippet-only inputs, not an observed count.

## Batteries and community storage

TNB’s directly accessible battery-management page says it piloted three community energy storage system (CESS) units at Elmina Ilham Residence, with battery capacities from 76.8 to 170 kWh. It says the units store daytime rooftop-solar generation for local use and support network stability ([TNB, undated page, accessed 16 October 2026][r19]).

The draft’s per-unit CESS figures are not reliable. A TNB DER page search snippet lists 70, 156 and 170 kWh; TNB’s FY2025 sustainability-statement snippet lists 76.8, 150 and 170 kWh. Those pages could not be opened, and the exact figures conflict with each other and with the directly accessible TNB page’s range. Use only the confirmed range above; the per-unit values are not established.

An additional search found a TNB Data Analytics page snippet saying the Elmina Smart Energy Management Infrastructure / Energy Integration Platform monitors loads and battery state of charge at low-voltage level. The page body could not be opened, so this is **SNIPPET-ONLY** ([TNB Data Analytics][r20]).

Analyst’s search: I did not locate public, quantified Elmina CESS results for energy shifted, peak-demand change, voltage performance or household savings in the TNB sources reviewed. This is a bounded search result, not evidence that no such results exist.

Analyst’s search: the household incentive pages reviewed did not describe a separate battery rebate. SolaRIS was a residential PV rebate for NEM Rakyat customers, and SuRIA Home is a Solar ATAP rebate calculated per kWac. SEDA’s 2026 NUR:DOMESTIK programme offers a RM200 e-rebate for an eligible 4- or 5-star air-conditioner or refrigerator; it is an appliance rebate, not a battery rebate. SEDA’s 14 October 2026 notice said quota remained available, subject to first-come, first-served terms ([ST SolaRIS][r21]; [SEDA SuRIA Home][r9]; [SEDA NUR:DOMESTIK 2026](https://www.seda.gov.my/nur-domestik/); [SEDA NUR announcements](https://www.seda.gov.my/nur/)).

## Air-conditioning in Malaysian homes

DOSM’s 2024 Basic Amenities Survey table search text reports air-conditioner ownership among households of Malaysian citizens at 68.8% nationally, 80.8% in Selangor and 73.5% in Negeri Sembilan. It lists urban/rural rates of 76.8% / 40.8% nationally, 81.8% / 56.6% in Selangor and 79.6% / 58.1% in Negeri Sembilan. The detailed table PDF could not be opened, so these rates are **SNIPPET-ONLY** ([DOSM Basic Amenities Survey 2024][r22]).

Analyst’s read: the DOSM 2022 and 2024 tables imply increases of 7.1 percentage points nationally, 4.5 points in Selangor and 12.4 points in Negeri Sembilan. This is a calculation from the two tables, whose detailed PDFs were not directly retrievable. The table measures household ownership, not air-conditioner units per home ([DOSM 2022][r23]; [DOSM 2024][r22]).

A March 2024 assessment prepared for United for Efficiency (U4E) by the International Institute for Energy Conservation (IIEC), with MGTC support for field data collection, surveyed 15 households in Selangor and recorded 53 room air-conditioners. That is about 3.5 units per surveyed household, a small-sample calculation rather than a state or national estimate. The assessment groups air-conditioners, fans and refrigerators/freezers as cooling appliances and reports that this group contributed 40%–70% of annual household electricity consumption among the sample households; this is not an air-conditioner-only or national share ([U4E / IIEC / MGTC, March 2024][r24]).

Analyst’s search: the sources reviewed did not provide a representative national household end-use share for air-conditioning alone or a consistent national time series for cooling electricity use. The 2021 Kuala Lumpur study cited in the draft could not be opened; its sample and consumption values are not carried into this verified summary.

## A realistic pilot size and data needs

Analyst’s read: City of Elmina is a well-documented place to explore a pilot, but the source record does not establish a ready-to-recruit cohort. Elmina Ridge 1 has 228 documented solar-and-battery homes, but SDP’s release does not establish their current occupancy or commissioning. Ilham’s 513-home count is snippet-only. The draft’s estimate of about 415 AC-owning Ilham homes is therefore removed as a market fact: it multiplied a snippet-only TNB count by a snippet-only Selangor household ownership rate, rather than measuring AC ownership at Ilham.

Analyst’s proposed design: recruit 50–100 occupied, eligible homes only after confirming delivery, working air-conditioners, control permissions, telemetry access and informed consent. Treat this as a planning target, not an observed market count or a power-calculated sample size. Screen for PV/inverter and battery telemetry, and an AC controller or manufacturer API. Measure PV self-use and export, battery state of charge and charge/discharge, evening grid import, AC energy/runtime, setpoint and overrides, and indoor temperature/humidity. Compare comfort and energy outcomes with each home’s baseline and a comparison group, while recording weather and solar conditions.

TNB’s FY2025 sustainability statement search text reports 5,563,252 smart meters cumulatively by 31 December 2025 and more than one million installations during 2025. The PDF could not be opened, so these figures are **SNIPPET-ONLY** ([TNB Integrated Annual Report 2025 Sustainability Statement][r25]). TNB’s FY2024 report search text says rollout continued in Selangor, Johor and Negeri Sembilan; no Selangor-specific installed count was located in the source checked. That PDF could not be opened either ([TNB Sustainability Report 2024][r26]).

TNB’s smart-meter FAQ search result says myTNB customers can view half-hourly usage updates; the FAQ page could not be opened, so this is **SNIPPET-ONLY** ([TNB smart-meter FAQ][r27]). Analyst’s read: a whole-home usage display does not by itself establish appliance-level AC data or control. TNB’s Elmina low-voltage monitoring claim is also snippet-only. Confirm access to TNB telemetry and customer-authorised data with the project partners before relying on it.
