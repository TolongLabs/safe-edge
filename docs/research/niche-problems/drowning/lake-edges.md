## Summary

- Design documents distinguish an **aquatic bench** from a **safety bench**. The CIRIA RP992 checklist gives an aquatic bench wider than **1 m**, no deeper than **450 mm**, and a separate safety bench wider than **3.5 m**. An Auckland Council guideline excerpt gives a planted bench at least **3 m** wide and no deeper than **300 mm** below permanent water level. The Auckland PDF could not be opened, so those figures are **SNIPPET-ONLY**. These are design specifications, not evidence that benches reduce drowning. ([CIRIA RP992 checklist](https://studylib.net/doc/6819128/design-assessment-checklists-for-ponds--wetlands); [Auckland Council guideline](https://at.govt.nz/media/1991506/stormwater-management-devices-in-the-auckand-region.pdf))
- **Analyst's search:** I found no lake-specific controlled estimate of drowning reduction from a planted lake-edge bench. A 2024 review of 12 studies on drowning prevention for people under 20 supports barriers around water, immediate resuscitation and first-responder training, and personal flotation devices (PFDs). It does not test shallow lake shelves. ([2024 global scoping review](https://www.frontiersin.org/journals/public-health/articles/10.3389/fpubh.2024.1467478/full))
- Constructed-wetland treatment results concern whole systems, not a narrow lake-edge shelf. The 2025 Michigan study reports an **estimated** cost to install a **214 m²** wetland system of **US$22,000** (**US$102.90/m²**); it excludes pond liners and monitoring equipment. This is not a safety-bench rate. ([2023 review](https://www.nature.com/articles/s43017-023-00395-z); [2025 cost study](https://doi.org/10.1111/1752-1688.70048))
- KPKT's indexed 2020–2024 “Lake/Mine” table gives fire-and-rescue search-and-rescue operation counts, not deaths. **SNIPPET-ONLY:** the PDF did not open. A separate DOSM 2025 report snippet gives **117 medically certified deaths in 2024** among children aged **0–14** from unintentional immersion, submersion, or falling into water across all settings; it is not lake-specific. ([KPKT statistics](https://www.kpkt.gov.my/kpkt/resources/user_1/GALERI/PDF_PENERBITAN/PERANGKAAN%20TERPILIH/STATISTIK_MyKPKT_2020_2024.pdf); [DOSM Statistics on Causes of Death, Malaysia, 2025](https://www.dosm.gov.my/uploads/release-content/file_20251218114249.pdf))
- GEC lists Taman Aman Lake's rehabilitation project as running **September 2024–August 2027**, with monitoring through **2034**. It names Google and Bonneville Environmental Foundation (BEF) in funding roles, MBPJ as a partner, and LimnoTech as a monitoring verifier. **Analyst's search:** the public pages reviewed did not state the project budget or a water-replenishment volume. ([GEC project page](https://gec.org.my/reference-project/rehabilitation-of-taman-aman-lake/))

**Evidence of safety effect.** Analyst's read: a broad shallow margin may reduce the sudden transition from bank to deep water and support dense planting. The sources reviewed do not test that mechanism as a lake-drowning intervention. Treat it as a design hypothesis and combine it with controls selected through a site risk assessment. RoSPA's SuDS FAQ cautions that the absence of recorded fatalities is not proof of safety. ([RoSPA SuDS FAQ](https://www.rospa.com/health-and-safety-news/suds-frequently-asked-questions); [2024 global scoping review](https://www.frontiersin.org/journals/public-health/articles/10.3389/fpubh.2024.1467478/full))

**Indicative costs, not a safety-bench rate.** A study published **31 October 2025** estimates that a **214 m²** Michigan drainage-wetland system could be installed for about **US$22,000**, or **US$102.90/m²**. The estimate includes design, vegetation clearing, installation, mats, plants, pots, and other materials; it excludes pond liners and monitoring equipment. The study describes an installation estimate, not a verified invoice or lake-edge bench. The Federal Reserve's **2025** annual average was **RM4.2809 per US$1** (page updated **5 January 2026**), giving a nominal conversion of about **RM441/m²** and **RM94,180** total. This is not an inflation adjustment. ([Cost study](https://doi.org/10.1111/1752-1688.70048); [Federal Reserve exchange rates](https://www.federalreserve.gov/releases/g5a/current/default.htm))

Putrajaya Corporation's drainage masterplan appendix gives a planting allowance of **RM9/m²**, based on **6 plants/m²** at **RM1.50 per plant**. The appendix has no publication date visible in the copy reviewed. This is a planting allowance, not earthworks, bank protection, access, or maintenance, and it is not a current market quote. ([Putrajaya masterplan appendix](https://www.ppj.gov.my/storage/volume02/279/279.pdf))

**Analyst's search:** I found no comparable published cost per linear metre or square metre for building a planted safety bench at a public lake or retention pond. The examples above price general wetland installation or planting, not the full edge-bench assembly.

## Water-quality co-benefits and who funds them

**Treatment evidence.** The indexed record for a **14 March 2023** global review reports **335** field-scale constructed wetlands. For hybrid systems, it gives median removal of **76%** for organic matter, **63%** for total nitrogen, and **72%** for total phosphorus. Results vary with temperature, hydraulic retention, pollutant loading, plants, substrate, and operation. **SNIPPET-ONLY:** the Nature article page did not open in this check. These are whole-system results, not a narrow lake-edge shelf result. ([Nature Reviews Earth & Environment review](https://www.nature.com/articles/s43017-023-00395-z))

A **2024** study of a stormwater-reuse reservoir and wetland complex reports downstream baseflow concentration reductions of **44%** in unfiltered total nitrogen and **50%** in unfiltered total phosphorus. Storm-event mean reductions were **31%** for total nitrogen, **54%** for total phosphorus, and **72%** for total suspended solids. This is a whole-system result, not an isolated littoral shelf result. ([Study](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/abs/pii/S004896972404765X))

Analyst's read: planted shelves may intercept sediment and take up nutrients where inflows pass through them, but the review and reservoir study do not quantify that effect for a narrow shelf around an existing urban lake. A pilot should measure inlet and lake water quality, flow or residence time, and sediment accumulation before claiming pollutant removal.

These are Google's portfolio figures. ([Google portfolio](https://blog.google/company-news/outreach-and-initiatives/sustainability/2026-water-stewardship-portfolio/))
- An **Amazon announcement dated 30 September 2026** says Amazon and Microsoft are funding wetland restoration with NABU across **40 ha** in Germany. GEC names Google and Bonneville Environmental Foundation (BEF) in funding roles, LimnoTech as monitoring verifier, and Petaling Jaya City Council (MBPJ) as a partner. ([GEC project page](https://gec.org.my/reference-project/rehabilitation-of-taman-aman-lake/))

([GEC quarterly report](https://gec.org.my/wp-content/uploads/2025/02/gec_q4_2024_report_20250118_final_website.pdf); [The Star](https://www.thestar.com.my/metro/metro-news/2026/09/30/taman-aman-lake-water-quality-better-after-rehab))

**Analyst's search:** The public Taman Aman pages reviewed do not state the project budget, a work-item funding split, or a quantified water-replenishment volume. Analyst's read: this project is a Malaysian partnership precedent; its public record does not establish an open Google or BEF grant programme for other township lakes.

**International prevention guidance and effect evidence.**

- WHO's **2014** global report was published on **17 November 2014**. **SNIPPET-ONLY:** the linked report PDF did not open; its indexed excerpt lists actions including barriers controlling access to water, safe rescue and resuscitation, and warning/communication measures. WHO's **2017** implementation guide is described in the official indexed result as a guide with **10** evidence-based interventions; the linked WHO page was generic at access. WHO's **2024** global status report, released **13 December 2024**, reviews global fatalities and prevention progress. These sources do not establish a lake-edge bench or lighting effect. ([WHO 2014 report](https://www.who.int/publications/i/item/9789241564786); [WHO 2017 implementation guide](https://www.who.int/publications/b/31148); [WHO 2024 report](https://www.who.int/publications/i/item/9789240103962))
- **New since the earlier report:** WHO announced its **PROTECT** technical package on **23 July 2026**. It organizes policy guidance into seven domains: physical infrastructure; rescue and resuscitation; occupational safety; transport safety; swimming and water-safety education; child-care and safeguarding; and threat preparedness for floods and other emergencies. It is broad policy guidance, not a measured lake-bench effect. ([WHO announcement](https://www.who.int/news/item/23-07-2026-who-launches-seven-strategies-to-prevent-drowning))
- A **2024** global scoping review covers people under **20** and included **12** studies with statistical evidence. It classifies barriers around water bodies, immediate resuscitation and first-responder training, and PFD use as effective; rescue/CPR training is described as promising. Most of the evidence came from high-income settings. The review does not test planted lake shelves or lake lighting. ([Ashraf et al., 31 December 2024](https://www.frontiersin.org/journals/public-health/articles/10.3389/fpubh.2024.1467478/full))
- Royal Life Saving Australia published inland-waterway guidance on **10 July 2024** covering risk assessment, signs, access management, and public rescue equipment. Water Safety New Zealand's page, dated **15 October 2024**, gives advice on signs, rescue equipment, and access control. ([Australia](https://www.royallifesaving.com.au/about/news-and-updates/news/2024/jul/royal-life-saving-australia-releases-new-guidelines-for-inland-waterway-safety); [Water Safety New Zealand](https://www.watersafetynz.org/news-media/making-inland-waterways-safer))
- RLSS UK's undated Water Safety Code advises calling **999**, floating, and throwing something buoyant to a person in difficulty; it does not use the quoted phrase “phone, float, throw.” Surf Life Saving New Zealand's undated public-rescue-equipment page describes life rings and throw bags and advises people not to enter the water to attempt a rescue. These are operational recommendations. ([RLSS UK](https://www.rlss.org.uk/listing/category/the-water-safety-code); [Surf Life Saving New Zealand](https://www.surflifesaving.org.nz/stay-safe/public-rescue-equipment-pre))
- A **15 March 2011** International Life Saving Federation paper reports more than **500** public trials of rescue equipment. Of **41** tested items, only **2–3** met both public-testing and manufacturing requirements. This supports careful equipment selection, not a quantified reduction in drowning. ([International Life Saving Federation paper](https://www.ilsf.org/library/public-water-safety-communication-and-information-systems-the-research-development-and-implementation-of-the-uk-national-guidelines-and-specifications-for-coastal-public-rescue-equipment/))

**Analyst's search:** I found no controlled lake-edge study quantifying the effect of lighting, warning signs, ring buoys, or throw bags on drowning deaths, and no Malaysian dataset coding calm or dry weather. Searches included `lighting lake drowning intervention`, `lake signage drowning prevention evaluation`, and Malay queries `lemas memancing tasik cuaca` and `lemas kolam memancing malam`. Analyst's read: evening calls and overnight recoveries suggest a possible detection gap, but they do not establish dry weather or prove that darkness caused a drowning.

**Analyst's search:** I found no published council or developer safety works specifically tied to the fishing drownings listed above, and no public SDP-wide fishing-permit or post-drowning standard for Elmina, Bandar Bukit Raja, or Serenia City. Analyst's read: for a township pilot, first confirm the lake owner or operator, applicable council by-law, current fishing status, and who checks permits. The cited examples show that a rule for a specific lake zone or event may differ from general park rules.

## Gaps

- **Analyst's search:** no direct before/after or controlled evidence found for drowning reduction from planted benches, lake lighting, warning signs, or public ring buoys at Malaysian lakes.
- **Analyst's search:** no national Malaysian lake/mine fatality dataset found that combines age, activity, time, weather, and outcome through **2026**. The KPKT figure located is a snippet-only series of rescue-operation counts through **2024**; DOSM's **117** deaths are for children and all water settings.
- **Analyst's search:** no comparable public cost found for a planted safety bench including earthworks, planting, edge protection, and long-term maintenance. The cited cost examples have different scopes.
- **Analyst's search:** Taman Aman's public pages do not disclose a budget or water-replenishment volume. The public SDP sources reviewed did not establish a company-wide fishing rule or named lake-safety pilot.
- **Analyst's search:** no independently validated lake-edge person-detection system or priced LoRaWAN ring-buoy monitor found in the sources reviewed.
