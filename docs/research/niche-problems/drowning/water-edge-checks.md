## Form G17 and CCC: what is certified

**Form G17.** The amended UBBL Second Schedule labels G17 “ROAD AND DRAIN CERTIFICATION.” Its operative statement begins “we have supervised and/or carried out” the works and says the signatories “accept full responsibility for the same.” It is signed by the applicable construction project manager/contractor, site supervisory staff and submitting person. The form identifies CIDB registration for the contractor/project manager and BEM registration for the supervisory staff and submitting person; where the work is not applicable, the PSP records that position. The certificate ties the road and drain works to approved or deposited plans. The form also calls for the relevant local authority or JKR confirmation letter; the amendment provides a deemed-confirmation route if no response is received within fourteen days. [S1](https://www.lam.gov.my/my-assets/docs/actRules/PEWARTAAN%20UKBS%201984%20SECARA%20G.N%20%28GAZETTE%20NOTIFICATION%29_0.pdf)

**CCC / Form F. [SNIPPET-ONLY]** KPKT’s FAQ search extract says only the PSP may issue a CCC, with PBT oversight. Search extracts of the cited UBBL documents describe Form F as certifying that a building is “safe and fit for occupation” and refer to stage Forms G1–G21. The cited PDFs and FAQ page could not be opened, so these details remain snippet-only. [S2](https://jkt.kpkt.gov.my/wp-content/d/sites/default/files/2019-06/2a.%20Uniform%20Building%20By-Laws%201986-Pg.P.U.%2041_1986%20%28Updated%29.pdf) [S3](https://jkt.kpkt.gov.my/about-lgd/faqs/certificate-of-completion-and-compliance-ccc-faq) [S4](https://jkt.kpkt.gov.my/wp-content/d/sites/default/files/2022-10/UKBS%201984%201C.pdf)

**Analyst’s read:** Neither G17 nor Form F has a separate pass/fail field for pedestrian access to water, edge protection, fencing, handrails, drain-cover continuity beside footpaths, or proximity to a playground. G17 ties certification to the approved/deposited plans; Form F is a broader completion and occupation certificate. This does not mean those matters can never be conditions on an approved plan or in a local authority checklist. [S1](https://www.lam.gov.my/my-assets/docs/actRules/PEWARTAAN%20UKBS%201984%20SECARA%20G.N%20%28GAZETTE%20NOTIFICATION%29_0.pdf) [S2](https://jkt.kpkt.gov.my/wp-content/d/sites/default/files/2019-06/2a.%20Uniform%20Building%20By-Laws%201986-Pg.P.U.%2041_1986%20%28Updated%29.pdf)

**Analyst’s read:** G17 records that the works were supervised and completed against approved/deposited plans. The form does not itself call for an as-built survey/drawing or a separate hydraulic performance test. Those may be required elsewhere in the approval and handover package, as the DBKL and MBSP examples show. [S1](https://www.lam.gov.my/my-assets/docs/actRules/PEWARTAAN%20UKBS%201984%20SECARA%20G.N%20%28GAZETTE%20NOTIFICATION%29_0.pdf) [S5](https://osc3plus.dbkl.gov.my/form/33) [S6](https://www.mbsp.gov.my/brgonline/kej/jalanparit/jp7-ccc.pdf)

**Local checklists add detail.** DBKL’s road and drainage checklist calls for an engineer-certified drainage/hydraulic calculation under MSMA, road, drain and sidewalk plans, cross-sections, and details for drains, sumps and covers. **Analyst’s read:** This shows that design and hydraulic review can occur at submission; I did not find a distinct water-edge access or playground-proximity risk assessment in this checklist. [S5](https://osc3plus.dbkl.gov.my/form/33)

MBSP’s JP7 CCC checklist is a local handover/site example. The current PDF is Keluaran No.7 dated 1 July 2026, replacing the cited bibliography note that said revision No.4 dated 1 January 2025. It requires a PSP or consultant to arrange a site visit with a department representative. It calls for a barrier beside a drain 1.200 m wide or deeper than 0.900 m. Other items check that drains are clean and flowing without standing water; manhole covers are level with the road or lane; sump grates and pond warning/code signs are installed; and a drain-gradient test is done with an MBSP officer after water is added one hour before the visit. The handover appendix records pond size, depth, volume and transfer status, and asks for pond photographs from four angles and a sign photograph. These are MBSP checks, not a Selangor or national standard. **Analyst’s read:** I found no playground-offset item in the listed checks. [S6](https://www.mbsp.gov.my/brgonline/kej/jalanparit/jp7-ccc.pdf)

## JPS and council handover of drains and ponds

The Selangor State Assembly answer titled “Serahan Kolam Takungan Oleh Pemaju Kepada JPS” is dated 2020. **SNIPPET-ONLY:** Its official search extract says developers for new and ongoing projects must send JPS the as-built plan, pre-computation plan and Form 12B under the National Land Code before JPS gives its CCC comment; JPS checks the pond against approved design and specifications before handover. The Assembly page could not be opened. [S7](https://dewan.selangor.gov.my/question/serahan-kolam-takungan-oleh-pemaju-kepada-jps/)

The cited Auditor General/Parliament PDF could not be opened and no supporting extract was found for the claimed documentary route. I removed the claim because it could not be checked. The 2024 “Kolam Tadahan Kampung Sungai Pinang” answer is also **SNIPPET-ONLY**: its extract concerns that specific pond, says its land remained under MB Klang (MBDK), and lists the developer’s as-built plan, pre-computation plan and Form 12B submissions before JPS’s CCC comment. Do not generalize this case to all ponds. [S8](https://www.parlimen.gov.my/ipms/eps/2023-11-22/CMD.67.2023%20-%20CMD67.2023.pdf) [S10](https://dewan.selangor.gov.my/question/kolam-tadahan-kampung-sungai-pinang/)

**SNIPPET-ONLY:** The 11 July 2024 Selangor Hansard extract refers to about 600 flood detention ponds in the state. It says new ponds should be completed and handed over before CCC, while older ponds had received CCC/CF without transfer; it gives 57 ponds as not gazetted. The Hansard PDF could not be opened. A 25 February 2025 Hansard extract says OSD should not remain with the developer company and describes community OSD being managed by a PBT or JPS. That PDF also could not be opened, so it supports a reported policy statement, not a verified statutory rule. [S9](https://dewan.selangor.gov.my/wp-content/uploads/2024/07/11-JULAI-2024-KHAMIS.pdf) [S11](https://dewan.selangor.gov.my/wp-content/uploads/2025/02/25-FEB-2025-SELASA.pdf)

**What is checked and what remains unclear.** **SNIPPET-ONLY:** A 2017 Selangor answer describes reviews involving hydraulic outlets and structures, drainage layout/reserves, stormwater/OSD and certified as-built plans. The 2020 answer extract mentions approved-design compliance and handover documents. [S7](https://dewan.selangor.gov.my/question/serahan-kolam-takungan-oleh-pemaju-kepada-jps/) [S13](https://dewan.selangor.gov.my/question/tebatan-banjir/)

**Analyst’s read:** I did not find a current published Selangor-wide joint JPS–council field checklist expressly covering public access, barriers, handrails, missing covers along footpaths or playground proximity. This search-based gap is not proof that no such checklist exists. MBSP’s directly opened checklist shows that a local handover can include a site visit, drain-condition and gradient checks, specified barriers, pond signs, photographs and transfer details; it is not evidence of Selangor practice. [S6](https://www.mbsp.gov.my/brgonline/kej/jalanparit/jp7-ccc.pdf)

## Automated design checking

**OSC 3.0 Plus.** KPKT’s FAQ describes online application/payment, digital review by technical agencies, decisions and real-time status tracking; the cited answer is dated 22 March 2024. DBKL’s road/drain checklist asks for engineering calculations and detailed plans. **Analyst’s read:** I found no official OSC material documenting automated geometry checks for drainage, slopes, covers or setbacks. [S14](https://osc3plus.kpkt.gov.my/faq) [S5](https://osc3plus.dbkl.gov.my/form/33)

**BIM e-submission / NBeS.** A CIDB media statement dated 28 August 2023 says CIDB and Majlis Perbandaran Kulim Kedah (MPKK) signed an agreement for MPKK to adopt and use NBeS for building-plan review. It describes automatic checks against UBBL and Malaysian Standard MS 1184, and CIDB claimed review time could fall from 21 days to 24 or 48 hours depending on BIM-model complexity. The same release says NBeS had been launched on 13 July 2023 after a pilot with five PBTs. These are CIDB’s dated claims and scope: building plans. They do not document drainage, grading, pond access or water-edge checks, or establish the system’s adoption status in 2026. An undated NBeS overview describes OSC integration as a future stage at the time it was written; that page does not establish the current status of integration. [S15](https://www.cidb.gov.my/eng/cidb-launches-national-bim-e-submission-initiative/) [S16](https://smart.cidb.gov.my/article/nbes-at-a-glance-8) [S31](https://www.cidb.gov.my/wp-content/uploads/2023/12/KENYATAAN-MEDIA-CIDB-MPKK-Meterai-Kerjasama-dlm-NBeS.pdf)

**Data and prototype feasibility.** MBSP’s current handover checklist asks for four certified as-built road/drain plans and a soft copy in AutoCAD, GIS and PDF formats. **SNIPPET-ONLY:** MyGDI’s search listing identifies a JPS hydrological-station dataset in shapefile and MapInfo formats, with metadata dated 27 August 2013. Selangor JPS search results identify a pond MapServer with several layers, but the service page could not be opened; its attributes, download terms and as-built drainage coverage remain unverified. [S6](https://www.mbsp.gov.my/brgonline/kej/jalanparit/jp7-ccc.pdf) [S17](https://mygdix.mygeoportal.gov.my/search) [S30](https://jpsselgis.selangor.gov.my/gis/rest/services/AppHydro/Kolam_Fasa_JPS/MapServer)

**Analyst’s read:** In searches of MyGDI, data.gov.my, JPS and council GIS portals, I did not locate a complete, publicly downloadable township package with georeferenced as-built drains, grading levels, pond edges, paths and playgrounds. A prototype may be feasible if SDP or a council supplies one approved/as-built project package with coordinates and footpath/playground layers. It could flag drawing-based risks against the listed MSMA thresholds and MBSP checks for engineer and site review. It should not claim to certify hydraulic performance or surveyed as-built condition. Access to a real sample package is the key feasibility dependency. They attribute to the Home Minister a call for fences or iron grilles on open drains, and to a residents’ committee representative the view that the nearby playground boundary was too close. These are reported post-incident statements, not an inquiry finding. The article pages could not be opened. - **Sibu, 2026:** Bernama reported on 4 February 2026 that three children drowned in a sewage pond. The Fire and Rescue Department said the pond had fencing, but damaged sections and openings made access easy, especially for children; it recommended regular inspections and stronger safety measures. This was a sewage pond, not necessarily a stormwater retention pond. [Bernama](https://www.bernama.com/en/news.php?id=2520195)
- **Sibu, 2025:** Sibu Municipal Council’s page, posted 5 August 2025, says its team put back opened manhole covers and installed warning lines at manholes still missing covers; it warns that missing or stolen covers endanger adults and children. The Borneo Post, also dated 5 August, reported the toddler died on 4 August and that a forensic team inspected the site; it described five uncovered manholes along about 50 metres. The forensic investigation was reported by The Borneo Post, not the council page. [Sibu Municipal Council](https://smc.gov.my/web/subpage/news_view/1341) [The Borneo Post](https://www.theborneopost.com/2025/08/05/forensic-team-probes-site-of-toddlers-fatal-fall-into-uncovered-manhole-in-sibu/)

This search result is not proof that no such finding exists. ## Gaps

- **Analyst’s read:** Plan certification can leave exposure implicit. G17 links work to approved plans, while CCC certifies overall completion. The forms do not themselves require a recorded pedestrian-access or playground-proximity review. [S1](https://www.lam.gov.my/my-assets/docs/actRules/PEWARTAAN%20UKBS%201984%20SECARA%20G.N%20%28GAZETTE%20NOTIFICATION%29_0.pdf) [S2](https://jkt.kpkt.gov.my/wp-content/d/sites/default/files/2019-06/2a.%20Uniform%20Building%20By-Laws%201986-Pg.P.U.%2041_1986%20%28Updated%29.pdf)
- **Analyst’s read:** Local practice differs. DBKL’s checklist is design-heavy; MBSP’s checklist adds site safety and handover items. I found no common national checklist that unifies these checks for drains, ponds and lake edges. [S5](https://osc3plus.dbkl.gov.my/form/33) [S6](https://www.mbsp.gov.my/brgonline/kej/jalanparit/jp7-ccc.pdf)
- **Analyst’s read (partly based on snippet-only Assembly extracts):** Selangor handover records emphasize approved design, as-builts and land paperwork. The sources located do not show a Selangor-wide joint form requiring public access, covers, fences, rails and nearby play spaces to be signed off together. [S7](https://dewan.selangor.gov.my/question/serahan-kolam-takungan-oleh-pemaju-kepada-jps/) [S9](https://dewan.selangor.gov.my/wp-content/uploads/2024/07/11-JULAI-2024-KHAMIS.pdf) [S10](https://dewan.selangor.gov.my/question/kolam-tadahan-kampung-sungai-pinang/) [S11](https://dewan.selangor.gov.my/wp-content/uploads/2025/02/25-FEB-2025-SELASA.pdf)
- **Analyst’s read:** Rules are trigger-based, and maintenance can undo them. MSMA has specific cover, slope, rail and sign provisions, but not a universal pond fence or playground setback. The Sibu pond incident also shows that a fence can exist but fail if damaged or left open. [S12](https://www.water.gov.my/jps/resources/MSMA2ndEdition_august_2012.pdf) [S27](https://www.bernama.com/en/news.php?id=2520195)
- **Analyst’s read:** The pilot bottleneck is access to real drawings and site records. Public sources found did not provide a complete, downloadable township design/as-built dataset. A pilot needs an SDP/council data agreement, a checklist linked to the applicable approval, and field verification to close drawing-to-site gaps. [S6](https://www.mbsp.gov.my/brgonline/kej/jalanparit/jp7-ccc.pdf) [S17](https://mygdix.mygeoportal.gov.my/search)
