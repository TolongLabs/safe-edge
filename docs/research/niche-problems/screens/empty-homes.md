## One-line problem

Analyst's read: Owners who leave new homes empty may miss early defects and basic upkeep, but public evidence does not establish how many post-handover homes are vacant or how much harm this causes. ([NAPIC 2025 report](https://napic.jpph.gov.my/storage/app/media/3-penerbitan/Shahrul/Bahagian%20Inventori%20Harta%20Tanah/Laporan%20Jadual%20Status%20Harta%20Tanah/Q4%202025/Laporan%20Status%20Harta%20Tanah%202025.pdf), [DOSM 2026 Living Quarters report](https://www.dosm.gov.my/portal-main/release-content/special-report-living-quarters-malaysia2020))

## Scale and 2024 to 2026 evidence (bullets with numbers and sources)

- NAPIC counted **30,471 completed unsold residential units worth RM17.73 billion at Q4 2025**, up **31.6%** in units from **23,149** in 2024. Selangor had **3,547** (11.6%); condos and apartments made up **14,357** (47.1%). NAPIC's overhang measure is completed, issued a CCC, unsold for more than nine months after launch. These are developer-held unsold homes, not homes bought and left empty after vacant possession. ([NAPIC Q4 2025 report](https://napic.jpph.gov.my/storage/app/media/3-penerbitan/Shahrul/Bahagian%20Inventori%20Harta%20Tanah/Laporan%20Jadual%20Status%20Harta%20Tanah/Q4%202025/Laporan%20Status%20Harta%20Tanah%202025.pdf); definition in [NAPIC H1 2025 report](https://napic.jpph.gov.my/storage/app/media/3-penerbitan/Shahrul/Bahagian%20Inventori%20Harta%20Tanah/Laporan%20Jadual%20Status%20Harta%20Tanah/Q2%202025/Laporan%20Status%20Harta%20Tanah%20H1%202025.pdf); [1], [2])
- Bank Negara Malaysia put broadly defined unsold housing at **2.5% of overall stock in Q4 2025**, versus **2.3% in Q2 2025**; this includes units under construction and completed units, plus serviced apartments and SOHO. BNM identifies individual property borrowers with **two or more** outstanding housing loans as investors, but that is not a count of investor-owned vacant homes. ([BNM Financial Stability Review 2H2025](https://www.bnm.gov.my/publications/fsr2025h2/ch1b); [3])
- DOSM's 2026 special report uses **2020 Census** vacancy data. Its national breakdown classifies **38.2%** of vacant living quarters as “newly completed/for rent or sale,” **23.5%** as repair or renovation, and **26.3%** as “others” (including homes without an owner or abandoned homes). The first category combines reasons and does not isolate newly completed homes; this is not a post-VP vacancy rate. DOSM counted **1.9 million** vacant living quarters in the 2020 Census, a broad snapshot of homes not usually occupied on Census Day. ([DOSM special report and infographic](https://www.dosm.gov.my/portal-main/release-content/special-report-living-quarters-malaysia2020); [DOSM 2020 Census key findings](https://www.dosm.gov.my/portal-main/release-content/launching-of-report-on-the-key-findings-population-and-housing-census-of-malaysia-2020-); [4], [5])
- Local dengue evidence supports the upkeep concern, but does not tie it to vacant new homes. Klang's council reported **501 Aedes breeding sites inside residential homes from 1 January to 16 March 2025**; the report described breeding sites such as bathroom containers and flower-pot trays. Kuala Lumpur Aedes research published in 2025 also found breeding in indoor wet areas and outdoor parking areas or vacant land. ([The Star, 20 March 2025](https://www.thestar.com.my/metro/metro-news/2025/03/20/mbdk-more-klang-homes-found-breeding-mosquitoes); [peer-reviewed study, 2025](https://www.tandfonline.com/doi/full/10.1080/09603123.2025.2488484); [8], [9])
- DOSM recorded **11,346 house break-in and theft cases in 2024**, down **1.8%** from **11,559 in 2023**. The published figure is not linked to vacant or newly handed-over homes. ([DOSM Crime Statistics, Malaysia 2025](https://www.dosm.gov.my/uploads/release-content/file_20251016113754.pdf); [11])
- **Not found:** a public 2024–2026 count or rate for homes bought and left unoccupied after VP, investor-owned empty units, or defects, break-ins, dengue breeding, or unpaid fees specifically in those homes. I searched NAPIC, DOSM, BNM/KRI, English and Malay news, and terms including “vacant after vacant possession,” “rumah kosong selepas serahan kunci,” and “rumah pelabur tidak diduduki.” The closest numbers above measure different populations.

## Why now (a 2025 or 2026 trigger, if any)

- DOSM released its special Living Quarters report on **23 April 2026**, adding a state, district, and local-authority view of 2020 vacancy reasons and ownership. ([DOSM release page](https://www.dosm.gov.my/portal-main/release-content/special-report-living-quarters-malaysia2020); [4])
- NAPIC's Q4 2025 release shows a sharp rise in completed unsold stock, including Selangor, while BNM's 2025 review also flags unsold stock. Neither is a measure of post-VP vacancy, but together they make a targeted pilot easier to motivate. ([NAPIC Q4 2025 report](https://napic.jpph.gov.my/storage/app/media/3-penerbitan/Shahrul/Bahagian%20Inventori%20Harta%20Tanah/Laporan%20Jadual%20Status%20Harta%20Tanah/Q4%202025/Laporan%20Status%20Harta%20Tanah%202025.pdf); [BNM Financial Stability Review 2H2025](https://www.bnm.gov.my/publications/fsr2025h2/ch1b); [1], [3])
- Analyst's read: The strongest “now” case is operational: Sime Darby Property already describes pre-handover inspections, an app-based defect workflow, and a defect rectification team, so a vacancy-aware service could test an adjacent gap using existing channels. ([SDP 2025 Integrated Annual Report](https://www.simedarbyproperty.com/sites/default/files/2026-04/SDP%20Integrated%20Annual%20Report%20Mobile.pdf); [SDP App FAQ](https://www.simedarbyproperty.com/app/faq.html); [19], [20])

## Who suffers and who owns the fix

- **Homeowner:** owns the unit, pays strata charges from VP where applicable, and needs to report visible defects to the developer within the DLP process. KPKT describes the ordinary Schedule G/H DLP as **24 calendar months** after VP; qualifying Tribunal claims have a separate deadline. ([KPKT DLP FAQ](https://dlp.kpkt.gov.my/faq); [KPKT homebuyer FAQ](https://ehome.kpkt.gov.my/index.php/pages/view/220); [KPKT payment FAQ](https://ehome.kpkt.gov.my/index.php/pages/view/236); [12], [13], [16])
- **Developer:** handles defects covered by the sale contract during the DLP. A KPKT FAQ says complaints should be made in writing during that period. A Malaysian Bar circular summarizing a **2026** Court of Appeal decision reports that a latent-defect claim may survive the ordinary 24-month period, so the DLP should not be described as an absolute cutoff for every hidden defect. ([KPKT DLP FAQ](https://dlp.kpkt.gov.my/faq); [Malaysian Bar Circular 108/2026](https://www.malaysianbar.org.my/cms/upload_files/document/Circular%20No%20108-2026.pdf); [12], [14])
- **JMB / management body:** maintains and manages common property and collects charges. It does not automatically become the caretaker for an owner's private parcel. ([Strata Management Act 2013, section 21](https://www.ppj.gov.my/storage/7465/Act-757--Strata-Management-Act-2013.pdf); [15])
- **Council and health inspectors:** local councils can address neglected compounds under their local processes. MBSA tells owners of unoccupied homes and empty lots to clear vegetation and clean at least every **two months**. Under the Destruction of Disease-Bearing Insects Act 1975, authorized health officers and inspectors can inspect premises and issue orders to remove breeding receptacles, cut vegetation, or address drainage. ([MBSA service page](https://www.mbsa.gov.my/ms-my/mbsa/perkhidmatan/perkhidmatanperbandaran/Halaman/perkhidmatan_kebersihan_rumah.aspx); [MOH Act 154](https://www2.moh.gov.my/moh/attachments/1196.pdf); [17], [18])
- Analyst's read: Owners and nearby residents bear the immediate cost of an unnoticed leak, visible pest damage, standing water, break-in, or overgrown compound; the developer, JMB, council, and health office each control only part of the response. I found no data showing how often these events happen in post-VP empty homes. ([KPKT DLP FAQ](https://dlp.kpkt.gov.my/faq); [Strata Management Act](https://www.ppj.gov.my/storage/7465/Act-757--Strata-Management-Act-2013.pdf); [DBKL Aedes guidance](https://www.dbkl.gov.my/en/news-events/cerita-kota-cerita-kita-jangan-biarkan-sikap-kita-mengundang-aedes); [10], [12], [15])

## SDP's lever (what SDP controls: before handover, defect period, township management, its own assets)

- **Before handover:** SDP's 2025 Integrated Annual Report describes a Pre-Handover Vacant Possession programme with quality inspections and inspection training. It names City of Elmina, Bandar Bukit Raja, Serenia City, Nilai Impian, and Bandar Universiti Pagoh among its townships. ([SDP 2025 Integrated Annual Report](https://www.simedarbyproperty.com/sites/default/files/2026-04/SDP%20Integrated%20Annual%20Report%20Mobile.pdf); [20])
- **During the defect period:** SDP says its app supports key-handover appointments, defect submissions after key collection, and rectification-status viewing; its 2025 report describes a We Fix Team for newly handed-over projects. The public app material does not describe a vacancy or scheduled home-watch feature. ([SDP App FAQ](https://www.simedarbyproperty.com/app/faq.html); [SDP 2025 Integrated Annual Report](https://www.simedarbyproperty.com/sites/default/files/2026-04/SDP%20Integrated%20Annual%20Report%20Mobile.pdf); [19], [20])
- **Township management:** the Elmina Valley Community app lists visitor registration, maintenance-fee payment, and resident communication for selected City of Elmina phases. That offers a possible opt-in channel, but published materials do not show vacancy monitoring. ([Elmina Valley Community app](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.framework7.simedarbycommunity); [21])
- Analyst's read: SDP can pilot at handover and coordinate follow-up through its customer and township teams. It cannot enter a privately owned home without the owner's permission, and common-area management does not transfer responsibility for an individual parcel to SDP or the JMB. ([SDP App FAQ](https://www.simedarbyproperty.com/app/faq.html); [Strata Management Act](https://www.ppj.gov.my/storage/7465/Act-757--Strata-Management-Act-2013.pdf); [15], [19])

## What already exists (products, services, programmes, hackathon projects)

- **Home-watch and property care:** Casa Keeper markets home monitoring in Kuala Lumpur and Selangor; IKI Links advertises visits for vacant or second homes, including ventilation, water-flow, and leak checks; NexaProp markets inspections and maintenance coordination for absentee owners. These are commercial offerings, not evidence of uptake or coverage in the named SDP townships. ([Casa Keeper](https://www.casakeeper.com.my/); [IKI Links](https://ikilinks.com/en/secondhouse-en/); [NexaProp](https://www.nexaprop.com/en/manage); [22]–[24])
- **Defect inspection:** ClickBina advertises new-home defect inspections in Kuala Lumpur and Selangor, including during DLP. ([ClickBina](https://clickbina.com/defect-inspection/); [25])
- **Public programmes and guidance:** MBSA has a cleaning and enforcement service for dirty unoccupied homes and neglected lots; DBKL publishes weekly Aedes inspection guidance for residents. Neither is a post-VP home-watch service. ([MBSA](https://www.mbsa.gov.my/ms-my/mbsa/perkhidmatan/perkhidmatanperbandaran/Halaman/perkhidmatan_kebersihan_rumah.aspx); [DBKL](https://www.dbkl.gov.my/en/news-events/cerita-kota-cerita-kita-jangan-biarkan-sikap-kita-mengundang-aedes); [10], [18])
- **Hackathons:** MYHackathon's public challenge areas include local service delivery and infrastructure; a MOSTI announcement describes its Tanjung Malim ServIT pilot for streetlights and solid-waste monitoring. KitaHack 2025 has a public event page. **Not found:** a public MYHackathon, KitaHack, university-competition, or Devpost project directly about post-VP empty homes, DLP monitoring, or home-watch in Malaysia. I searched those platforms and the terms “vacant homes,” “rumah kosong,” “home watch,” “defect period,” and “DLP.” This is a quick public-page search, not proof no project exists. ([MYHackathon programme](https://www.myhackathon.gov.my/maklumat-program); [MOSTI ServIT pilot](https://www.mosti.gov.my/en/berita/projek-rintis-myhackathon-sistem-servit-smart-city-tanjung-malim-perak/); [KitaHack 2025](https://www.monash.edu.my/it/news-and-events/articles/2025/kitahack-2025); [26]–[28])
- Analyst's read: Crowding is moderate around property care and defect inspections, but I did not find a publicly documented service tying owner-approved home checks to SDP's DLP reporting workflow and township operations. ([SDP App FAQ](https://www.simedarbyproperty.com/app/faq.html); [Casa Keeper](https://www.casakeeper.com.my/); [IKI Links](https://ikilinks.com/en/secondhouse-en/); [19], [22], [23])

## Real data a prototype could use (open datasets or APIs, with URLs)

- **OpenDOSM living-quarter counts:** state-level household and living-quarter dataset, with a [CSV download](https://storage.dosm.gov.my/demography/hh_lq_state.csv) and a dataset page describing the series. It covers living-quarter counts, including empty dwellings in census data; it is not a current unit-level vacancy feed. ([OpenDOSM dataset](https://data.gov.my/data-catalogue/hh_profile_state); [6], [7])
- **DOSM vacancy context:** 2026 special report tables provide 2020 vacancy reasons and ownership with state, district, and local-authority breakdowns. ([DOSM special report](https://www.dosm.gov.my/portal-main/release-content/special-report-living-quarters-malaysia2020); [4])
- **NAPIC overhang:** Q4 2025 tables provide completed unsold stock by state and housing type; useful as a market context layer, not an occupancy label. ([NAPIC Q4 2025 report](https://napic.jpph.gov.my/storage/app/media/3-penerbitan/Shahrul/Bahagian%20Inventori%20Harta%20Tanah/Laporan%20Jadual%20Status%20Harta%20Tanah/Q4%202025/Laporan%20Status%20Harta%20Tanah%202025.pdf); [1])
- **Dengue context:** iDengue exposes a public hotspot list by locality and outbreak period. I found no documented public API or house-level feed in this screen. ([iDengue hotspot list](https://idengue.mysa.gov.my/hotspotutama.php); [29])
- **Crime context:** Data.gov.my's district crime dataset is another area-level context source; its coverage ends in **December 2023** and it does not identify vacant properties. ([Data.gov.my crime dataset](https://data.gov.my/data-catalogue/crime_district); [30])
- **Pilot data needed from SDP:** with owner opt-in, use real VP date, occupied/vacant status, access permission, defect report and rectification timestamps, visit checklist, and consented photos. Add township complaints and common-area maintenance records. These are not publicly available in the sources checked; do not infer vacancy from utility consumption without a validated, consented method.

## What a strong solution could be (one paragraph; must be something SDP could pilot in 1 to 2 years and a student team could prototype in 7 days)

Analyst's read: Offer an opt-in “SDP Home Watch” choice during key handover for owners who expect to leave a home empty: schedule owner-authorized checks, record timestamped photos of standing water, leaks, visible pest damage, entry signs, and compound condition, and create an SDP App defect draft that the owner can submit and track during the DLP. Start with one phase in City of Elmina or Bandar Bukit Raja, use actual consenting homes and SDP's live defect workflow, and measure take-up, issues found, time to report and rectify, visit cost, and any Aedes breeding sites. A student team can prototype the consent, visit, evidence, and reporting flow in seven days; a live pilot would need SDP operations, privacy safeguards, and a vetted inspection or home-care partner. ([SDP App FAQ](https://www.simedarbyproperty.com/app/faq.html); [SDP 2025 Integrated Annual Report](https://www.simedarbyproperty.com/sites/default/files/2026-04/SDP%20Integrated%20Annual%20Report%20Mobile.pdf); [The Star, 20 March 2025](https://www.thestar.com.my/metro/metro-news/2025/03/20/mbdk-more-klang-homes-found-breeding-mosquitoes); [8], [19], [20])

## Kill risks (3 bullets)

- **The target may be too small or poorly defined:** public sources do not quantify post-VP vacancy or the share of new buyers who leave homes empty.
- **Access and liability:** checks inside private homes require clear owner consent, secure handling of photos and access, and a defined boundary between observation and repair.
- **Weak unit economics:** home-watch providers already exist; SDP must show that its handover access, defect reporting, or township coordination produces better outcomes at a cost owners or SDP will pay. ([Casa Keeper](https://www.casakeeper.com.my/); [IKI Links](https://ikilinks.com/en/secondhouse-en/); [22], [23])

## Scores (1 to 5 each, one line reason each): Pain, SDP lever, Evidence available, Crowding (5 = very crowded). Then: Edge = Pain x Lever x Evidence / Crowding.

- **Pain: 3/5** — credible upkeep and defect risks, but no measured post-VP incidence. ([The Star, 20 March 2025](https://www.thestar.com.my/metro/metro-news/2025/03/20/mbdk-more-klang-homes-found-breeding-mosquitoes); [KPKT DLP FAQ](https://dlp.kpkt.gov.my/faq); [8], [12])
- **SDP lever: 5/5** — controls handover channels, defect reporting, and township operations. ([SDP App FAQ](https://www.simedarbyproperty.com/app/faq.html); [SDP 2025 Integrated Annual Report](https://www.simedarbyproperty.com/sites/default/files/2026-04/SDP%20Integrated%20Annual%20Report%20Mobile.pdf); [19], [20])
- **Evidence available: 2/5** — national and local proxies exist, but no direct post-VP vacancy dataset. ([NAPIC Q4 2025 report](https://napic.jpph.gov.my/storage/app/media/3-penerbitan/Shahrul/Bahagian%20Inventori%20Harta%20Tanah/Laporan%20Jadual%20Status%20Harta%20Tanah/Q4%202025/Laporan%20Status%20Harta%20Tanah%202025.pdf); [DOSM special report](https://www.dosm.gov.my/portal-main/release-content/special-report-living-quarters-malaysia2020); [1], [4])
- **Crowding: 3/5** — several home-watch and defect-inspection services exist; no direct matching student project surfaced in the searched public sources. ([Casa Keeper](https://www.casakeeper.com.my/); [IKI Links](https://ikilinks.com/en/secondhouse-en/); [ClickBina](https://clickbina.com/defect-inspection/); [22], [23], [25])
- **Edge = Pain × Lever × Evidence / Crowding = 3 × 5 × 2 / 3 = 10.** Analyst's read: worth a small, data-gathering pilot if SDP can supply an opt-in cohort; evidence is too weak to claim a large, proven vacancy problem.
