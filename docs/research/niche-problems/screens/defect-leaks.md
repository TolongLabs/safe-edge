## One-line problem

Analyst’s read: For prescribed Schedule G/H homes, a defect liability period that starts at vacant possession can run before a buyer regularly occupies the home or before intermittent, concealed water ingress becomes visible; Malaysian public data do not quantify how often this happens. The National Housing Department describes the 24-month period [2](https://ehome.kpkt.gov.my/index.php/pages/view/220).

## Scale and 2024 to 2026 evidence (bullets with numbers and sources)

- KPKT reported **12,883** cases registered and **11,839** resolved by the combined Housing and Strata Claims Tribunal (TPPS) in 2024; Jan–Jun 2025 had **6,484** registrations and **6,505** resolutions. In 2024, awards totalled **RM26,444,180.82** at the homebuyer tribunal (TTPR) and **RM60,053,136.58** at the strata tribunal (TPS). These are all-issue totals, not leak or defect counts [1](https://www.kpkt.gov.my/kpkt/resources/user_1/media_akhbar/2025/SM_KPKT_TERUS_MEMPERKUKUH_PERKHIDMATAN__TPPS_NEGARA%2C_PERTINGKATKAN_KECEKAPAN_PENGENDALIAN_KES.pdf).
- A 2024 Malaysian study of **104** homebuyers who bought new homes within the previous five years ranked “defect discovery” as the most critical category among factors leading to DLP complaints. It does not report leak prevalence or represent the national housing stock [3](https://doi.org/10.1108/JEDT-01-2024-0017).
- In a 2024 news feature, an inspector estimated that **10%** of new homes/buildings have “problems”; the report does not give a sampling method or a clear denominator, so treat this as a practitioner anecdote, not an incidence estimate [4](https://www.sinarharian.com.my/article/661714/berita/laporan-khas/10-peratus-rumah-baharu-alami-kerosakan).
- **Post-DLP cost signal:** the same feature quotes an inspector warning that rectification after warranty expiry can reach five figures. This is anecdotal, not leak-specific, and not a representative repair-cost sample [4](https://www.sinarharian.com.my/article/661714/berita/laporan-khas/10-peratus-rumah-baharu-alami-kerosakan).
- A 2024 High Court judgment ordered Sime Darby Property to pay six bungalow owners about **RM4.8 million** for defects. The Court of Appeal affirmed the judgment in January 2026; the Malaysian Bar’s case note says complaints included water seepage and cracks at wall and ceiling joints. This is a severe, litigated case, not an average household cost or a leak-only award [5](https://www.freemalaysiatoday.com/category/nation/2024/04/19/sime-darby-ordered-to-pay-6-bungalow-owners-rm4-8mil-for-defects)[6](https://www.malaysianbar.org.my/cms/upload_files/document/Circular%20No%20108-2026.pdf)[7](https://www.freemalaysiatoday.com/category/nation/2026/01/30/rm4-8mil-judgment-against-sime-darby-property-for-defective-bungalows-affirmed).
- **Not found:** a public 2024–2026 Malaysian count of roof, wet-area, window or slab leaks; a leak-specific TTPR/TPS award breakdown; or a representative survey of repair bills after DLP. Searches covered KPKT/TPPS and TTPR terms in English and Malay, government statistics, consumer reporting and Malaysian news.
- CIDB’s QLASSIC is an objective workmanship assessment at completed construction before customer delivery and covers landed and strata categories. Its measure is a construction quality score, not an in-use leak rate [8](https://www.cidb.gov.my/kualiti-qlassic-quality-assessment-system-for-building-construction-works/). CIDB’s portal lists national average scores of **69% in 2014** and **72% in 2020** [9](https://smart.cidb.gov.my/program/qlassic). Analyst’s read: those scores are too old and too broad to estimate current leak incidence.

## Why now (a 2025 or 2026 trigger, if any)

- In January 2026, the Court of Appeal affirmed the **RM4.8 million** defect judgment against Sime Darby Property; the Malaysian Bar’s case note says the DLP applied to patent defects discoverable during the 24-month period and did not bar claims for latent defects discovered later [6](https://www.malaysianbar.org.my/cms/upload_files/document/Circular%20No%20108-2026.pdf)[7](https://www.freemalaysiatoday.com/category/nation/2026/01/30/rm4-8mil-judgment-against-sime-darby-property-for-defective-bungalows-affirmed). Analyst’s read: this makes earlier detection and a clear evidence trail relevant to residents and developers, but one case does not establish how courts will decide other facts or contracts.
- Analyst’s read: Water may appear only under a particular rain direction or intensity; concealed water can travel before staining becomes visible; and an empty, rented or intermittently occupied home may go unreported for longer. I found no Malaysian study quantifying these specific causes. JPS does provide rainfall by state, district and station, which could help test the weather link rather than assume it [10](https://publicinfobanjir.water.gov.my/hujan/data-hujan/).
- Analyst’s read: Because the DLP clock is tied to vacant possession, a gap between VP, move-in and the first relevant rain event can reduce the time available for a resident to observe and report a defect [2](https://ehome.kpkt.gov.my/index.php/pages/view/220).

## Who suffers and who owns the fix

- Analyst’s read: Purchasers and occupants may bear disruption, damaged finishes or belongings, health concerns from persistent dampness, and diagnosis/repair costs. Owners may need to coordinate access when the home is rented or vacant. This scan found no Malaysian leak-specific loss survey.
- Analyst’s read: The developer and its contractors have the clearest process lever during the contractual DLP. After that period, responsibility can depend on whether the defect is latent, the sale contract and evidence; the 2026 appellate case note is a reminder not to treat DLP expiry as automatically ending every possible claim [2](https://ehome.kpkt.gov.my/index.php/pages/view/220)[7](https://www.freemalaysiatoday.com/category/nation/2026/01/30/rm4-8mil-judgment-against-sime-darby-property-for-defective-bungalows-affirmed). In strata, the owner, developer and joint management body or management corporation may have different responsibilities for a unit versus common property; the public TPPS totals do not separate leak cases [1](https://www.kpkt.gov.my/kpkt/resources/user_1/media_akhbar/2025/SM_KPKT_TERUS_MEMPERKUKUH_PERKHIDMATAN__TPPS_NEGARA%2C_PERTINGKATKAN_KECEKAPAN_PENGENDALIAN_KES.pdf).
- Analyst’s read: Residents need an easy way to report; developers need earlier diagnosis and auditable repair closure; strata managers need a way to distinguish unit leaks from common-property ingress.

## SDP’s lever (what SDP controls: before handover, defect period, township management, its own assets)

- SDP’s 2025 annual report describes pre-handover Vacant Possession inspections supported by certified independent building inspectors, purchaser defect-claim tracking, and a “We Fix Team” for newly handed-over projects [11](https://www.simedarbyproperty.com/sites/default/files/2026-04/SDP%20IAR2025%20Mobile.pdf).
- SDP’s app accepts defect submissions after the purchaser has collected keys and lets them view rectification progress. SDP’s 2025 report says its Performance Analytics & Command Centre (PACC) gives customers real-time updates and manages defect inquiries [11](https://www.simedarbyproperty.com/sites/default/files/2026-04/SDP%20IAR2025%20Mobile.pdf)[12](https://www.simedarbyproperty.com/app/faq.html). Analyst’s read: this is a ready workflow to extend, while a new reporting app alone would add little.
- SDP reports **27 active townships** [13](https://www.simedarbyproperty.com/sites/default/files/2026-04/SDP%20IAR2025%20Mobile.pdf). Analyst’s read: SDP could test a targeted post-rain follow-up in one new phase, using its own inspection, contractor, customer service and township operations. Its control is strongest before handover and during developer-led rectification; it cannot assume control over every owner’s later interior repairs or strata common property.

## What already exists (products, services, programmes, hackathon projects)

- **Developer apps and defect systems:** SDP already has its app and PACC [11](https://www.simedarbyproperty.com/sites/default/files/2026-04/SDP%20IAR2025%20Mobile.pdf)[12](https://www.simedarbyproperty.com/app/faq.html). EcoWorld markets NiuAce Homebuyer Defect Management for defect submissions, appointments, documents and case tracking [14](https://homebuyer2.ecoworld.my/product/homebuyer-defect-management). Tropicana’s T360 app includes defect and vacant-possession features [15](https://www.tropicanacorp.com.my/tropicana360). SP Setia’s 2024 report describes a Defect Monitoring System App for pre- and post-construction defect tracking **(snippet only)** [16](https://spsetia.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/04/2024.pdf). Analyst’s read: generic defect intake and progress tracking are crowded.
- **Third-party inspection:** Perintis Bariz markets home defect inspections with reports for homeowners and developers [17](https://perintisbariz.com/services/home-defect-inspection/); HomeInspectionMalaysia advertises moisture-meter and thermal-camera checks [18](https://homeinspectionmalaysia.com/services). **Not found:** a clearly identifiable Malaysian inspection firm under the exact names “Nexus Inspection” or “Inspekt.” Exact-name searches were “Nexus Inspection Malaysia home defect,” “Inspekt Malaysia home defect inspection,” and “INSPEKT Malaysia building inspection house defects”; results did not establish either as a local home-inspection provider.
- **Sensors sold in Malaysia:** TP-Link Malaysia’s Tapo T300 detects water at its probes and sends an app alert [19](https://www.tp-link.com/my/smart-home/smart-sensor/tapo-t300/); Aqara Malaysia’s T1 detects water on a floor [20](https://aqaramalaysia.com/en/products/water-leak-sensor-t1/); Malaysian supplier IPSH sells the CMR SLD-1 leak alarm [21](https://www.ipsh.com.my/collections/cmr-water-and-oil-leak-detection/products/cmr-smart-water-leak-alarm-sld-1). Analyst’s read: these products can detect water reaching a sensor near a pipe, appliance or floor, but do not by themselves locate concealed roof, window, wall or slab ingress before water reaches the sensor.
- **Government programmes:** CIDB QLASSIC provides a pre-delivery workmanship assessment [8](https://www.cidb.gov.my/kualiti-qlassic-quality-assessment-system-for-building-construction-works/); KPKT provides the DLP framework and the homebuyer/strata tribunal route [1](https://www.kpkt.gov.my/kpkt/resources/user_1/media_akhbar/2025/SM_KPKT_TERUS_MEMPERKUKUH_PERKHIDMATAN__TPPS_NEGARA%2C_PERTINGKATKAN_KECEKAPAN_PENGENDALIAN_KES.pdf)[2](https://ehome.kpkt.gov.my/index.php/pages/view/220). **Not found:** a dedicated public post-occupancy water-ingress monitoring or leak-sensor programme in the scoped searches.
- **Hackathons and student projects:** An older 2021–2022 UM/INSPEN project proposed a smart vision-based building-defect assessment during DLP [22](https://www.inspen.gov.my/images/INSPEN/PDFDoc/NAPREC%20Conference/Projek%202021%202022/15.%20A%20Smart%20Vision-based%20Practical%20Building%20Defects%20Assessment%20During%20Defect%20Liability%20Period%20%28DLP%29.pdf). A 2023 student/research prototype monitored domestic water-pipe leakage with IoT sensors; this is adjacent, not roof or envelope ingress [23](https://ieeexplore.ieee.org/document/10237150/). Air Selangor’s 2024 Data & Digital Hackathon included a distribution-network leak-detection challenge, and the Fresh Air team from Universiti Malaya won with a pipe-network monitoring concept; that is water utility infrastructure, not homes [24](https://digital.airselangor.com/hackathon/)[25](https://mediaselangor.com/en/2024/06/220549). **Not found:** a directly matching home-ingress/DLP project in scoped searches of MYHackathon, KitaHack and Devpost using combinations of “water leak,” “home defect,” “DLP” and “building defect.” Analyst’s read: a generic photo-inspection app is already an explored idea.

## Real data a prototype could use (open datasets or APIs, with URLs)

- JPS Public InfoBanjir exposes rainfall by state, district and station, with hourly intensity bands [10](https://publicinfobanjir.water.gov.my/hujan/data-hujan/). Analyst’s read: use observed station readings as rain-event context, not as proof that a particular unit leaked.
- Malaysia’s government data API offers MET Malaysia weather forecasts and warnings by location; this can drive a resident prompt but is forecast data, not a record of rain at the house: [Weather API](https://developer.data.gov.my/realtime-api/weather) [26](https://developer.data.gov.my/realtime-api/weather).
- KPKT’s public TPPS release and time-series statistics provide tribunal aggregates, not leak-labelled cases [1](https://www.kpkt.gov.my/kpkt/resources/user_1/media_akhbar/2025/SM_KPKT_TERUS_MEMPERKUKUH_PERKHIDMATAN__TPPS_NEGARA%2C_PERTINGKATKAN_KECEKAPAN_PENGENDALIAN_KES.pdf). **Not found:** an open Malaysian dataset of home-level water-ingress reports, repairs, or DLP dates.
- Analyst’s read: The most useful real dataset would be SDP’s anonymised defect tickets, VP dates, location/building type, report and repair dates, repeat visits, defect category and repair cost, joined to the nearest JPS rainfall station. SDP would need to authorize access; public sources cannot replace its defect records. Do not claim a predictive model until labels and enough cases are available.

## What a strong solution could be

Analyst’s read: Build a weather-triggered defect follow-up queue inside SDP’s current service workflow. Join real, anonymised SDP defect and repair history to JPS station rainfall, highlight repeated leak reports and locations that should be checked after relevant rain, and send a resident a short guided check-in with an option to request inspection. A student team could prototype a map/timeline and rule-based queue in seven days if SDP provides real sample records; SDP could pilot it with one newly handed-over phase in a live township over one to two years. Track time from VP to first report, leak confirmation, repeat repair, and closure. Keep sensors as optional floor-level alerts for places where pooling can reach them; do not present them as coverage for hidden building-envelope leaks. Existing SDP app/PACC and inspection capacity make the workflow feasible, while actual benefit depends on access to SDP’s defect data [10](https://publicinfobanjir.water.gov.my/hujan/data-hujan/)[11](https://www.simedarbyproperty.com/sites/default/files/2026-04/SDP%20IAR2025%20Mobile.pdf)[12](https://www.simedarbyproperty.com/app/faq.html).

## Kill risks (3 bullets)

- **Analyst’s read — no usable SDP history:** if old tickets lack consistent leak labels, timestamps or repair outcomes, the team cannot validate a risk queue on real data; public rainfall alone cannot show where homes leak [1](https://www.kpkt.gov.my/kpkt/resources/user_1/media_akhbar/2025/SM_KPKT_TERUS_MEMPERKUKUH_PERKHIDMATAN__TPPS_NEGARA%2C_PERTINGKATKAN_KECEKAPAN_PENGENDALIAN_KES.pdf)[10](https://publicinfobanjir.water.gov.my/hujan/data-hujan/).
- **Analyst’s read — weak signal and missed leaks:** station rainfall is not parcel-level weather, and a leak may depend on workmanship, drainage or water pressure as well as rain. Sensor alerts only help after water reaches the sensor [10](https://publicinfobanjir.water.gov.my/hujan/data-hujan/)[19](https://www.tp-link.com/my/smart-home/smart-sensor/tapo-t300/)[20](https://aqaramalaysia.com/en/products/water-leak-sensor-t1/).
- **Analyst’s read — crowded intake and unclear ownership:** SDP and other developers already offer defect reporting, while inspection firms and sensors serve adjacent needs. Strata unit/common-property responsibility can complicate routing; a new dashboard without a faster inspection or repair outcome has little value [11](https://www.simedarbyproperty.com/sites/default/files/2026-04/SDP%20IAR2025%20Mobile.pdf)[12](https://www.simedarbyproperty.com/app/faq.html)[14](https://homebuyer2.ecoworld.my/product/homebuyer-defect-management)[15](https://www.tropicanacorp.com.my/tropicana360)[17](https://perintisbariz.com/services/home-defect-inspection/)[21](https://www.ipsh.com.my/collections/cmr-water-and-oil-leak-detection/products/cmr-smart-water-leak-alarm-sld-1).

## Scores (1 to 5 each, one line of reason each): Pain, SDP lever, Evidence available, Crowding (5 = very crowded). Then: Edge = Pain x Lever x Evidence / Crowding.

- **Pain: 4/5** — Analyst’s read: high consequence for affected owners and supported by a major seepage case, but incidence is unknown [6](https://www.malaysianbar.org.my/cms/upload_files/document/Circular%20No%20108-2026.pdf)[7](https://www.freemalaysiatoday.com/category/nation/2026/01/30/rm4-8mil-judgment-against-sime-darby-property-for-defective-bungalows-affirmed).
- **SDP lever: 4/5** — Analyst’s read: SDP has inspection, rectification and customer-service workflows across active townships; resident access and strata governance limit reach [11](https://www.simedarbyproperty.com/sites/default/files/2026-04/SDP%20IAR2025%20Mobile.pdf)[12](https://www.simedarbyproperty.com/app/faq.html)[13](https://www.simedarbyproperty.com/sites/default/files/2026-04/SDP%20IAR2025%20Mobile.pdf).
- **Evidence available: 2/5** — Analyst’s read: tribunal and weather data exist, but public records do not identify leak defects; useful evaluation needs SDP ticket history [1](https://www.kpkt.gov.my/kpkt/resources/user_1/media_akhbar/2025/SM_KPKT_TERUS_MEMPERKUKUH_PERKHIDMATAN__TPPS_NEGARA%2C_PERTINGKATKAN_KECEKAPAN_PENGENDALIAN_KES.pdf)[10](https://publicinfobanjir.water.gov.my/hujan/data-hujan/).
- **Crowding: 4/5** — Analyst’s read: developer apps, inspection firms, sensors and earlier DLP defect-app projects already exist; a rain-triggered repair workflow appears less directly served [14](https://homebuyer2.ecoworld.my/product/homebuyer-defect-management)[15](https://www.tropicanacorp.com.my/tropicana360)[17](https://perintisbariz.com/services/home-defect-inspection/)[19](https://www.tp-link.com/my/smart-home/smart-sensor/tapo-t300/)[22](https://www.inspen.gov.my/images/INSPEN/PDFDoc/NAPREC%20Conference/Projek%202021%202022/15.%20A%20Smart%20Vision-based%20Practical%20Building%20Defects%20Assessment%20During%20Defect%20Liability%20Period%20%28DLP%29.pdf).
- **Edge = 4 x 4 x 2 / 4 = 8.** Analyst’s read: worth a small discovery/pilot only if SDP will share real defect records; public evidence alone is too weak for a confident scale case.
