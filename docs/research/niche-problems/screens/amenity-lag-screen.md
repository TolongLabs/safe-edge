## One-line problem

Analyst's read: New residents can arrive before nearby public amenities have enough capacity or usable connections, leaving families to travel farther while public agencies complete land, approval, funding and construction steps.

## Scale and 2024 to 2026 evidence (bullets with numbers and sources)

- Malaysia's Ministry of Education (KPM) reported that SK Denai Alam and SK Taman Pelangi Semenyih began operating on **17 February 2025**; this is evidence of new public capacity in growing areas, but not proof that either township had no earlier school nearby. [KPM parliamentary reply](https://www.parlimen.gov.my/files/jindex/pdf/JBLDN25032025-v1.pdf)
- For City of Elmina, a 2018 report documents handover of homes in Elmina Valley 1. SK Denai Alam's 2025 start and SJK(C) Ladang Regent Elmina's first school day on **12 January 2026** create approximate **7- and 8-year** intervals from that documented phase handover to additional public-school capacity. These are not verified handover-to-first-school lags: SDP's own material lists existing schools in the wider Elmina/Bukit Subang area. [2018 handover report](https://www.starproperty.my/news/fast-internet-speed-for-sime-darby-property-s-elmina-homes/101667), [KPM opening dates](https://www.parlimen.gov.my/files/jindex/pdf/JBLDN25032025-v1.pdf), [Bernama, SJK(C) Ladang Regent Elmina](https://www.bernama.com/en/general/news.php?id=2511407), [SDP Elmina schools](https://www.simedarbyproperty.com/commercial/city-of-elmina)
- Bandar Bukit Raja was launched in **August 2002**. A Selangor Assembly answer says SK Bukit Raja was under construction and expected to finish in **2027**; the same answer says the SMK site was still in land acquisition, with construction to be proposed for the **2027** rolling plan. SDP lists nearby public schools, including SK/SMK Setia Alam about **4 km** away. This is a long launch-to-added-school interval, not a verified first-school lag or first-home-handover interval. [SDP launch history](https://www.simedarbyproperty.com/press-releases/sime-darby-property-launches-azira-bandar-bukit-raja), [Selangor Assembly answer](https://dewan.selangor.gov.my/question/pembinaan-sekolah-baru-di-bandar-bukit-raja/) (snippet only), [SDP Bandar Bukit Raja amenities](https://simedarbyproperty.com/bandar-bukit-raja/index.html)
- Selangor government media reported that **8** new schools were under construction by **July 2026**, including schools in Denai Alam, Setia Alam and Bukit Raja. It also reported **117** schools completed under the overcrowding programme since **2022**. KPM identifies need and JKR carries out approved public-school construction. [Media Selangor, 2026](https://mediaselangor.com/ms/2026/10/398892)
- In **2025**, an MP reported some classes in Shah Alam had as many as **50** pupils; KPM's cited standard was **30**, with up to **35** allowed in specified cases. Six Shah Alam schools, including SK Setia Alam, were slated for cabin classrooms. Treat the 50 figure as a reported local example, not a full-area count. [Media Selangor, 2025](https://mediaselangor.com/en/2025/02/252305)
- KPM reported **44** new school approvals in **2025**, compared with **26** in **2024** and **9** in each of **2022** and **2023**. Approval counts show public response, but do not mean schools were already open. [Media Selangor, 2025](https://mediaselangor.com/en/2025/02/252305)
- Residents in Puncak Alam and Setia Alam told Bernama in **October 2025** that overcrowding led parents to send children to more distant schools. Budget 2026 announced **38** new schools nationwide, including **9** in Selangor. This is testimony plus a government response, not a measured average school-run distance. [Bernama, Budget 2026 school plans](https://www.bernama.com/en/general/news.php?id=2478619)
- The new SJK(C) Ladang Regent Elmina started its **2026** school year with **266** pupils enrolled, with **300** expected by month end. [Bernama, 12 January 2026](https://www.bernama.com/en/general/news.php?id=2511407)
- The LRT3 Shah Alam Line began operating on **29 June 2026**. It improves rail access along its corridor, but the opening alone does not establish a first-rail-access date for each SDP township. [DanaInfra LRT3 FAQ](https://www.danainfra.com.my/faq/faq-lrt-3-project)
- A City of Elmina resident's **2025** opinion article describes peak-hour gridlock and school-pickup traffic, including a claim that a roughly **1 km** drive could take up to an hour. This is resident testimony, not an independently measured travel-time study. [MalaysiaGazette opinion article](https://malaysiagazette.com/2025/04/26/the-city-of-elminas-broken-promise/)
- Not found in this quick screen: a reliable public record pairing first home handover with first public clinic opening or first bus service for the five named SDP townships; verified first public-school opening dates for Serenia City, Nilai Impian and Bandar Universiti Pagoh. Searches included township names with school/clinic/bus opening, handover, and Malay terms _sekolah_, _klinik kesihatan_, _bas_, _mula beroperasi_ and _serahan kunci_. Available evidence mostly reports current amenities or new additions, not first-service dates.

## Why now (a 2025 or 2026 trigger, if any)

- Analyst's read: The issue is timely because new school capacity and temporary classrooms are being added while reported crowding continues in Shah Alam growth areas; the **2026** Elmina school opening and the LRT3 opening provide near-term changes that a pilot can measure. [2025 overcrowding response](https://mediaselangor.com/en/2025/02/252305), [2026 school opening](https://www.bernama.com/en/general/news.php?id=2511407), [LRT3 opening](https://www.danainfra.com.my/faq/faq-lrt-3-project)
- Rapid KL On-Demand added an Alam Budiman/Setia Alam zone from **15 May 2025**, linking the area to Setia City Mall. That shows a current demand-responsive service model in a nearby growth corridor, though it is not evidence of a dedicated SDP school shuttle. [Rapid Bus media release](https://myrapid.com.my/wp-content/uploads/2025/05/RAPID-BUS-SIARAN-MEDIA-MOBILITI-KOMUNITI-DAN-PELAJAR-DIPERTINGKAT-DENGAN-RAPID-KL-ON-DEMAND.pdf)

## Who suffers and who owns the fix

- Families with school-age children bear longer trips, time lost in traffic, and difficulty securing a place near home; resident testimony and the Shah Alam school reports point to those pressures. [Bernama, 2025](https://www.bernama.com/en/general/news.php?id=2478619), [MalaysiaGazette resident opinion](https://malaysiagazette.com/2025/04/26/the-city-of-elminas-broken-promise/)
- KPM decides public-school need, approvals and allocations; state education departments identify needs and support land requests, while JKR delivers approved construction. Land reservation through planning and land transfer are separate steps from funding and opening a school. [KPM Development Division](https://www.moe.gov.my/index.php/bahagian-pembangunan-pelajaran), [Selangor Assembly answer on school land](https://dewan.selangor.gov.my/question/rizah-tanah-untuk-sekolah-sk-smk-sjkc-sjkt-dan-sekolah-swasta/) (snippet only), [Media Selangor on JKR projects](https://mediaselangor.com/ms/2026/10/398892)
- KKM owns public-clinic planning and operations; local authorities, land agencies and state/federal ministries have roles in land use and approvals. Bus and rail service decisions sit with operators and public transport authorities, not the township developer. [KKM public facilities dataset](https://github.com/MoH-Malaysia/data-resources-public/blob/main/README.md), [Smart Selangor route catalogue](https://selgdx.selangor.gov.my/katalog), [DanaInfra LRT3 FAQ](https://www.danainfra.com.my/faq/faq-lrt-3-project)
- Analyst's read: Residents experience the gap, but no single actor controls the whole chain. The developer can make sites and local access ready; public agencies control whether and when public services are staffed, funded and operated.

## SDP's lever (what SDP controls: before handover, defect period, township management, its own assets)

- Analyst's read: Before handover, SDP can sequence phases against verified school, clinic and transit readiness; reserve and service sites; publish facility milestones; and design safe walking, pick-up and bus access. These measures can reduce avoidable lag but cannot compel public agencies to open a facility.
- SDP's Bandar Universiti Pagoh project is a government-linked public-private partnership for a multi-university education hub. This shows experience coordinating education infrastructure, but it is a tertiary hub, not evidence of an SDP-operated public primary or secondary school. [SDP Pagoh announcement](https://www.simedarbyproperty.com/press-releases/sime-darby-property-develop-malaysia%19-s-first-multi-varsity-education-hub-pagoh?id=&thankyou=true)
- SDP's community app supports visitor registration and tracking, including routine school transport providers, e-hailing and guests; this is an existing township-management channel that a pilot could extend. [SDP Community app](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?hl=en&id=com.framework7.simedarbycommunity)
- Not found: evidence in the quick search that SDP already operates a dedicated school shuttle, interim public clinic, or temporary public school at the named townships. Searches included SDP site/news pages and the township names with _shuttle_, _school bus_, _temporary school_, _clinic_ and Malay equivalents.

## What already exists (products, services, programmes, hackathon projects)

- Public programmes: KPM/JKR school construction and overcrowding works; Smart Selangor buses; Rapid KL On-Demand; and the LRT3 corridor. Smart Selangor states its routes target development and higher-density areas without existing bus routes and connect to public facilities such as schools, clinics and rail stations. [Media Selangor school programme](https://mediaselangor.com/ms/2026/10/398892), [Selangor Assembly on Smart Selangor](https://dewan.selangor.gov.my/question/perkhidmatan-bas-smart-selangor/) (snippet only), [Rapid KL On-Demand](https://myrapid.com.my/wp-content/uploads/2025/05/RAPID-BUS-SIARAN-MEDIA-MOBILITI-KOMUNITI-DAN-PELAJAR-DIPERTINGKAT-DENGAN-RAPID-KL-ON-DEMAND.pdf), [LRT3](https://www.danainfra.com.my/faq/faq-lrt-3-project)
- A 2020 EcoWorld case at Eco Tropics says the developer fully subsidised phase one of SJK(C) Ladang Grisek, which opened with **405** pupils and **23** teachers. This is a documented developer-funded school example, not a general rule for public schools. [EcoWorld, 2020](https://ecoworld.my/news/sjk-c-ladang-grisek-ecoworld-at-eco-tropics-officially-open/)
- Adjacent student projects include UKM's **2025** MySchoolCommute student-transport management app and UTHM's MyChildBus school-bus tracking application. They address transport coordination or tracking, not the timing and capacity of township amenities. [UKM technical report](https://ftsm.ukm.my/v6/public/assets/file/technicalreport/A177335_TReport.pdf), [UTHM MyChildBus paper](https://periodical.uthm.edu.my/index.php/aitcs/article/view/20195)
- Older adjacent hackathon work: Rapid Bus/UTM's **2023** data hackathon included bus-service-efficiency analytics. [Rapid Bus/UTM hackathon FAQ](https://myrapid.com.my/wp-content/uploads/2023/09/MyRapid-Hack-FAQ-1.pdf), [UTeM winner notice](https://ftmk.utem.edu.my/web/index.php/ptta-portfolio/data-hackhaton-2023-sekalung-tahniah/)
- Not found in a quick scan of public project lists: a direct township amenity-readiness or handover-to-school/clinic/transit tracking product in MYHackathon, KitaHack or the sampled university projects. Searches: _MYHackathon school township amenity_, _KitaHack school transport township_, and university student projects on school commute and township facilities. This is not proof that no such project exists. [MYHackathon 2024 programme](https://www.mosti.gov.my/en/berita/program-myhackathon-2024-kohort-1/), [KitaHack 2024](https://www.monash.edu.my/it/news-and-events/articles/2024/kita-hack)

## Real data a prototype could use (open datasets or APIs, with URLs)

- KPM school counts by district and school type, with annual series through **2025**: [MOE schools by district](https://data.gov.my/data-catalogue/schools_district). This supports area-level supply trends, not a school-level opening timeline or physical capacity measure.
- KPM enrolment counts by district, school stage and sex, updated **3 August 2026**: [dataset page](https://data.gov.my/ms-MY/data-catalogue/enrolment_school_district), [CSV](https://storage.data.gov.my/education/enrolment_school_district.csv). It can provide a crowding proxy at district level; it cannot establish a particular school's waitlist or capacity.
- KKM public-facility master list with locations, including clinics: [dataset documentation](https://github.com/MoH-Malaysia/data-resources-public/blob/main/README.md), [facility CSV](https://github.com/MoH-Malaysia/data-resources-public/blob/main/facilities_master.csv). It is a current facilities list; no clinic opening dates, patient throughput or spare capacity were found in that source.
- Current Prasarana GTFS static feed includes routes, stops, trips and stop times: [official GTFS API documentation](https://developer.data.gov.my/realtime-api/gtfs-static), [`rapid-bus-kl` feed](https://api.data.gov.my/gtfs-static/prasarana?category=rapid-bus-kl), [`rapid-rail-kl` feed](https://api.data.gov.my/gtfs-static/prasarana?category=rapid-rail-kl). It describes current scheduled service, not historical first-service dates. [KTMB GTFS feed](https://api.data.gov.my/gtfs-static/ktmb)
- Selangor's Smart Selangor route catalogue provides route-direction information: [SELGDX catalogue](https://selgdx.selangor.gov.my/katalog). Pair it with SDP phase handover and occupancy records if SDP can provide them; those private records were not located as open datasets.
- Analyst's read: A seven-day prototype can map current schools, clinics and transit stops against a township boundary and estimate access gaps. It should label district enrolment as a proxy and avoid presenting it as school-level capacity.

## What a strong solution could be (one paragraph; must be something SDP could pilot in 1 to 2 years and a student team could prototype in 7 days)

Analyst's read: Pilot a Township Amenity Readiness and School Access service in one live SDP catchment. In seven days, a student team could map public school and clinic locations, current bus/rail stops, walking or road access, and district enrolment using the cited live datasets, then mark where evidence is missing. In a one-to-two-year pilot, SDP could add its actual phase handover and occupancy schedule, confirm school capacity and opening milestones with JPN/PPD, and use the map to trigger a limited, safe peak-hour school or rail shuttle when a phase is occupied before access is adequate. The service should report verified gaps and route use to residents and agencies; it should not imply that SDP controls public-school or clinic opening decisions.

## Kill risks (3 bullets)

- SDP cannot direct KPM, KKM, JKR, local authorities or transit operators to allocate staff, budgets or routes; a reserved site does not guarantee an operating school or clinic. [KPM Development Division](https://www.moe.gov.my/index.php/bahagian-pembangunan-pelajaran), [Selangor school-land answer](https://dewan.selangor.gov.my/question/rizah-tanah-untuk-sekolah-sk-smk-sjkc-sjkt-dan-sekolah-swasta/) (snippet only)
- Open data lack reliable historical opening dates, per-school physical capacity, waitlists and clinic throughput. A map could overstate certainty if it treats district enrolment or facility location as proof of capacity. [MOE school dataset](https://data.gov.my/data-catalogue/schools_district), [MOE enrolment dataset](https://data.gov.my/ms-MY/data-catalogue/enrolment_school_district), [KKM dataset documentation](https://github.com/MoH-Malaysia/data-resources-public/blob/main/README.md)
- A dashboard alone does not shorten trips. Any shuttle requires operating budget, safety and licensing arrangements, reliable demand, and coordination with school calendars and families; a paid or private option may not serve households who need it most. Analyst's read.

## Scores (1 to 5 each, one line of reason each): Pain, SDP lever, Evidence available, Crowding (5 = very crowded). Then: Edge = Pain x Lever x Evidence / Crowding.

- Pain: **4/5** — Analyst's read: school crowding and longer trips are documented in the wider Klang Valley, but site-specific burden is not quantified. [Bernama, 2025](https://www.bernama.com/en/general/news.php?id=2478619)
- SDP lever: **3/5** — Analyst's read: SDP controls phasing, site readiness and township operations, but not public budgets or service openings. [KPM Development Division](https://www.moe.gov.my/index.php/bahagian-pembangunan-pelajaran)
- Evidence available: **3/5** — Analyst's read: location, enrolment and current transit datasets exist, but historic first-service dates and facility capacity are missing. [MOE datasets](https://data.gov.my/data-catalogue?source=MOE), [GTFS documentation](https://developer.data.gov.my/realtime-api/gtfs-static)
- Crowding: **3/5** — Analyst's read: transit and school-bus apps/programmes exist, but the exact amenity-readiness niche was not found in a quick scan. [Rapid KL On-Demand](https://myrapid.com.my/wp-content/uploads/2025/05/RAPID-BUS-SIARAN-MEDIA-MOBILITI-KOMUNITI-DAN-PELAJAR-DIPERTINGKAT-DENGAN-RAPID-KL-ON-DEMAND.pdf), [UKM project](https://ftsm.ukm.my/v6/public/assets/file/technicalreport/A177335_TReport.pdf)
- Edge = **4 × 3 × 3 / 3 = 12**.
- Analyst's read: Run a focused validation sprint before committing to deep research or a build. The clearest pilot wedge is school access and interim mobility in an SDP catchment; clinic lag and true handover-to-first-service intervals remain unverified.
