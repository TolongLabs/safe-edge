1. The Problem

  • Bomba counted 371 child and teen drowning deaths, Jan 2023 to Aug 2026
  • 8 of 21 reported township water incidents began in a drain; in 2, a child went in after a ball
  • After handover, fences break and covers go missing, and nobody walks every edge
  • “The bigger issue is … clear accountability for maintenance and public safety”: former SDP Head of Township

2. Target User

  • SDP's Elmina park maintenance manager, who must keep every retained water edge closed
  • No public record of edge fences, and a weekly walk misses what a storm breaks
  • A repair can look finished and still leave a way round the fence end
  • Supported by the drainage engineer, a reviewer and the township general manager

3. Your Solution

  • Safe Edge: one loop from patrol to proven fix for every edge SDP maintains
  • Drones, or a phone walk, check fences, covers, buoys and signs
  • A named owner confirms each flag; Fix Lens tests the repair on the site photo first
  • A second person proves the fix on site; the edge gets a pass that expires

4. Innovation

  • Drone inspection and AI change detection already exist, for example PUB in Singapore
  • New: the protocol around them, from flag to owner, fix, independent proof and expiry
  • Fix Lens shows when a partial repair leaves a recorded route open, before it is bought
  • AI can flag and suspend, never approve; it watches edges, not people

5. How it Works

  • Patrol: a drone or the walk crew covers each edge
  • Flag: a missing safety item suspends the pass
  • Confirm: the manager decides in the Edge Inbox
  • Fix: Fix Lens checks the repair closes every route
  • Prove: a second person re-tests it on site
  • Repeat: the pass expires and the next patrol checks

6. Impact

  • Main measure: confirmed open routes to water that stay closed at day 30
  • Days from finding to interim protection, and to independent acceptance
  • Parks stay usable: closing a whole park never counts as a fix
  • Families get safer parks; SDP gets a tested record to hand to the council

7. Feasibility

  • The phone patrol can start now, with no aviation approval
  • Drones follow after about two months of CAAM, JUPEM, MBSA and privacy approvals
  • Pilot ceiling: RM10,000 for one asset; drones are priced on top
  • Already built: web app, API, SDK, MCP server and DJI mission exports

7. Implementation

  • Pilot: one retained drain in Elmina Central Park, eight weeks
  • Before work: written access, named owner and engineer, a quote and funds
  • Count every route, then compare today's method with Fix Lens
  • Install the fix, re-test it independently, check again at day 30
  • Scale only if closure, park access, cost and ownership hold

8. Why Your Team Should Win

Township children keep drowning in drains and ponds, and after handover nobody clearly owns the fix. Safe Edge gives every water edge an owner and a working loop: patrol, flag, confirm, fix, prove. Fix Lens shows when a repair looks finished but still leaves a way round the fence end, before SDP pays for it. The AI can flag but never approve, and every simulated part is labelled. The phone patrol can start without aviation approval. Our ask: an eight-week pilot on one retained drain in Elmina Central Park, with a named owner and a RM10,000 ceiling.

Appendix: Drone Approvals

  • CAAM Standard ATF: at least 14 working days, RM250 per aircraft per area, up to 90 days
  • JUPEM imagery approval where required, and MBSA's drone request
  • An SDP landowner letter and a data protection impact assessment
  • Subang air traffic: Elmina Central Park is about 6.5 km from Subang, inside its control zone
  • Docked patrols only if CAAM accepts a safety case for a residential area
  • Until then, the phone patrol carries the whole loop

Appendix: Risks

  • It may not beat a good engineer: the pilot compares both on the same evidence
  • A record does not fund a repair: no work starts without funds and an owner
  • Photos can mislead: tape and a 100 mm gauge decide sizes; unknown never passes
  • Drones: control-zone limits and false flags from growth and glare
  • The pilot measures routes closed and kept closed, not lives saved
