# Pick Safe Edge for the submission

On 17 October 2026 we picked [Safe Edge](/docs/research/shortlist/safe-edge.md), People track, safety and security, from the three ideas in the [shortlist](/docs/research/shortlist.md). Safe Edge and the site breeding register are tied within the panels' noise, and Safe Edge wins on innovation, which carries 20%, and on evidence the team can gather without an active building site. This supersedes the picking rule in [issue #18](https://github.com/TolongLabs/sdp-hack/issues/18), which defaulted to Lepas Hujan before the round-5 panel scored it lowest of the three.

## Considered options

| Idea                   | Round-5 panel /100 | Scores of 2 out of 5               | Why                                                                                                                                                      |
| ---------------------- | -----------------: | ---------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Safe Edge              |                 66 | Commercial (10%)                   | Chosen. Its evidence needs one permitted water edge and a practitioner to compare checklists, both still to secure                                       |
| Site breeding register | 64 (71 in round 4) | Innovation (20%)                   | Closest to repackaging Procore, which SDP already uses. Its evidence needs an active SDP site and its environmental officer                              |
| Lepas Hujan            |                 55 | Impact (25%), commercial, evidence | Cheapest to build, but the figure that sizes its impact, leaks first reported after month 24, needs SDP's defect log or ten owner interviews in six days |

Safety and security is a named People focus area in the [brief](/docs/BRIEF.md#the-challenge); housing quality is not. Safe Edge also answers the [township practitioner][consult] on drains and ponds after handover: the issue is "clear accountability for maintenance and public safety".

[consult]: /docs/research/consultations/township-practitioner.md

## Consequences

- **Commercial case:** it is the weakest score, and the pitch's value case, smoother handover acceptance and less legal exposure, has no figure yet. The itemised repair quote matters most.
- **The Tasik Bukit Raja death:** SDP leaders judge the pitch day, so cite it only as a reason to inspect, as the pitch does.
- **Evidence before submission:** as listed in the pitch's [prototype plan](/docs/research/shortlist/safe-edge.md#prototype-by-23-october).
- **Open question:** the brief leaves unanswered whether drainage counts as a township problem ([issue #32](https://github.com/TolongLabs/sdp-hack/issues/32)).
- **Fallback:** if no water edge can be measured by 19 October, switch to Lepas Hujan, the only idea that needs no outside party.
