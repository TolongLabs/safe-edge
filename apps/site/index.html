    Six rules, posted at every edge.

    Safe Edge keeps them on every screen, for every flag, on every patrol.

    Swipe or scroll sideways to read all six.

    • People decide.

      Machines can flag, pause and draft. Only a named person confirms or signs.

    • Edges, not people.

      Frames with people are dropped before storage. No faces, no tracking.

    • Unseen is unknown.

      Glare, canopy or water make a field task, never a pass.

    • Two people per fix.

      Whoever installs a repair cannot accept it.

    • Passes expire.

      12 months at most. Damage or a missed patrol suspends one at once.

    • The drone is optional.

      Where a drone cannot fly, a phone patrol feeds the same Inbox.
